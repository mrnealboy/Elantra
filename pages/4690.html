Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the front seat assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  3. Remove the seat back.

    (Refer to Body - "FRONT SEAT BACK COVER ")

  4. Disconnect the lumber support motor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Separate the retaining clips (A) from the seat frame.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the lumber support assembly (A) after disengaging mounting clip.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
