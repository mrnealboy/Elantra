Repair Procedures: Replacement

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the automatic transaxle.

    (Refer to Automatic Transaxle System - "REPAIR PROCEDURES ")

  2. Remove the torsion damper.

    (Refer to Cylinder Block - "DRIVE PLATE ")

  3. Remove the rear oil seal (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Apply engine oil to a new oil seal lip.
  5. Using SST (09231-H1100, 09214-L1200) and a hammer, tap in the oil seal (A) until the SST face is aligned with the cylinder block assembly rear face.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Install in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:
    • Always use new oil seal.
