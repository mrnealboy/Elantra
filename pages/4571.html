Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the crash pad lower panel. (Refer to Body - "CRASH PAD LOWER PANEL ")
  3. Remove the steering column upper & Lower shroud.
  4. Remove the ignition switch and disconnecting the Key Warning/immobilizer connector.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. If it is necessary to remove the key lock cylinder (A), remove the key lock cylinder (A) after pushing lock pin (B) with key ACC.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Installation is the reverse of removal procedure.
