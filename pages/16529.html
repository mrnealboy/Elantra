Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal.
  2. Disconnect the variable intake solenoid valve connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the vacuum hose (A).
  4. Remove the VIS valve mounting bolts (B).

    Tightening Torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb.ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
