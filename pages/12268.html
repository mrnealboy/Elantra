Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedures.
    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to enter dust, foreign matters, etc in regulator.
    • When installing, apply grease on a O-ring part of regulator connector.
    • Replace the O-ring with new when install the clutch regulator.
  2. Perform bleeding air procedure in concentric slave cylinder after pouring the brake fluid.

    (Refer to CONCENTRIC SLAVE CYLINDER ASSEMBLY ")
