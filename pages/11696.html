Front Seat Belt Pretensioner (BPT)

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the door scuff trim. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "DOOR SCUFF TRIM ")
  3. Remove the center pillar lower trim. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "CENTER PILLAR LOWER TRIM ")
  4. Remove the center pillar upper trim. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "CENTER PILLAR UPPER TRIM ")
  5. Remove the front seat belt upper anchor (A) after loosening the mounting bolt.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Separate the BPT connector (A) and PSB connector (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Loosen the mounting bolts (B, C), remove the front seat belt pretensioner (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
