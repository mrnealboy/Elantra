When not using the CVVD fixture

  1. Provisionally tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M6) in sequence shown below.

    Tightening torque: 

    4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

    Fig 1: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Provisionally tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M8) in sequence shown below.

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Fig 2: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M8) in sequence shown below.

    Tightening torque: 

    18.6 - 22.6 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

    Fig 3: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M6) in sequence shown below.

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Fig 4: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Release the torque of control shaft bearing cap bolt (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Push the control shaft bearing cap as close as possible in the direction of the arrow.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Install the control shaft bearing cap mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Measure the clearance between the control shaft bearing cap and the end of control shaft using a clearance gauge.

    Clearance:  0.0 - 0.05 mm (0.0 - 0.002 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Install the camshaft front bearing cap (A) and camshaft bearing caps (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • If there is enough engine oil on the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt and bolt hole, tighten to the minimum required tightening torque.
    • Apply engine oil the upper part of the intake/exhaust camshaft journal, then install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap. The amount of application should be enough to flow down both the front and back sides when it is applied to the center.
    1. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Fig 5: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Fig 6: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    5. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      18.6 - 22.6 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

      Fig 7: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    6. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    7. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Install the other parts in the reverse order of removal.
  11. Fill with engine coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE:
    • The coolant must be injected according to the integrated thermal management module (ITM) coolant filling method.
  12. Refill engine with engine oil.

    (Refer to Lubrication System - "ENGINE OIL ")
