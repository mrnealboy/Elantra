[Rear Door Speakers]

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the rear door trim.

    (Refer to Body - "REAR DOOR TRIM ")

  3. Remove the rear door speaker (B) after disconnecting the connector (A) and loosening screws.
