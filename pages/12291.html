Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  2. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - Battery - "REMOVAL , and BATTERY TRAY ")

  3. Pull the snap pin (A) and then separate the clutch regulator (B).
  4. Separate the reservoir hose (C).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the ignition lock & clutch switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the nuts (A) and then removing the clutch pedal.

    Tightening torque: 

    16.7 - 25.5 N.m (1.7 - 2.6 kgf.m, 12.3 - 18.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
