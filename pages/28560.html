Rear camera

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Rear camera (Parking Guide System: PGS) operates with a gear R, and receives signals via CAN communication, and controls the system. The rear camera receives a steering angle sensor signal via CAN communication and sends the backward direction signal of the vehicle to the AVN monitor that provides parking information to the driver.
