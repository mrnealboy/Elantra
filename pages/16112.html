DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the CAN messages
  1. Open or short circuit of CAN communication line (intermittent contact failure)
  2. Faulty Gateway system
  3. Intermittent contact failure of Controller connector, power (including fuse) and chassis ground
Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery Voltage is between 9V and 16V
  • Ignition has been on for over 2 seconds (Time Delay)
  • CAN communication lines are normal
Threshold Value
  • An abnormal signal is received from CGW (Abnormal counter value)
Diagnostic Time
  • 0.5 seconds
MIL
  • FCA/SCC operation cancelled/inhibited
  • FCA/SCC warning lamp ON
