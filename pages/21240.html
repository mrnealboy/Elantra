DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy
  • Check the Clutch position
  1. Actuator stuck
  2. Clutch 1 motor Fault
Enable Condition
  • Battery voltage is higher than 9V
  • Engine speed is higher than 400 RPM
  • Clutch target transfer torque is higher than 100Nm
Threshold Value
  • Slip speed is higher than 1000 RPM
  • Actual transfer torque is less than 10Nm
Diagnostic Time
  • 1s
Fail Safe
  • Drive at Even gear (2/4/6/R)
