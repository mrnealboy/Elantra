DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble shooting Condition Possible cause
Main action
  • Monitor the neutral gear switch circuit
  1. Open or short neutral gear switch
  2. Faulty neutral gear switch
Threshold value
  • When the condition of the Gear N switch status is continuously "ON" while driving at more than 70 km/h (43.49 MPH) and stopping 3 times
  • When the condition of the Gear N switch status is continuously "OFF" while driving at more than 70 km/h (43.49 MPH) and stopping 3 times
  • When the Gear N Switch signal changes more than 80 times in 5 seconds
MIL
  • AVH/EPB/DBC warning lamps are activated
Reset condition
  • When the normal state is confirmed after ignition switch turned OFF and back ON
