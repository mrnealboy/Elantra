DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the EMS CAN messages
  1. Poor contact of connectors of each communication module
  2. Open/short in CAN communication circuit
  3. EMS (ECU)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery voltage is normal
  • 1.5 sec. after turning the ignition switch on
  • Engine is running
Threshold Value
  • A EMS CAN message was not received
Diagnostic Time
  • After 2 sec.
