Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Clean the timing chain cover.
    1. Using a gasket scraper, remove all the old packing material from the gasket surfaces.
    2. The sealant locations on the chain cover and the counter parts (cam carrier, cylinder head, cylinder block, and bedplate) must be free of harmful foreign materials, oil, dust and moisture. Spraying cleaner on the surface and wiping with a clean duster.
  2. Install the timing chain cover.
    1. Apply liquid gasket sealant to an interface between cylinder head and cylinder block.

      Bead width  : Ø 3.0 - 5.0 mm (0.11 - 0.20 in.)

      Sealant  : MS721-40AA or AAO

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. After applying liquid sealant on the timing chain cover, assemble the cover within 5 minutes after sealant was applied. Continuous bead of sealant should be applied to prevent any path from oil leakage.

      Bead width 

      Whole section: Ø 2.5 - 3.5 mm (0.10 - 0.14 in.)

      Section A: Ø 4.5 - 5.5 mm (0.18 - 0.22 in.)

      Section B: Ø 8.0 - 9.0 mm (0.32 - 0.35 in.)

      Sealant:  MS721-40AA or AAO

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Install the timing chain cover. The dowel pins on the cylinder block and holes on the timing chain cover should be used as a reference in order to assemble the timing chain cover in exact position.

      Tightening torque 

      Bolts (A, B):

      18.6 - 23.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.4 kgf.m, 13.7 - 17.4 lb-ft)

      Bolt (C):

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Bolts (D, E, F):

      39.2 - 49.0 N.m (4.0 - 5.0 kgf.m, 28.9 - 36.2 lb-ft)

      Bolt (G):

      19.6 - 23.5 N.m (2.0 - 2.4 kgf.m, 14.5 - 17.4 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • The engine running or pressure test should not be performed within 30 minutes after the timing chain cover & oil pump assembly was assembled.
  3. Replace the front oil seal if necessary.

    (Refer to "FRONT OIL SEAL ")

  4. Install the other parts reverse order of removal.
