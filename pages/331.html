WARNING: This page is about the Elantra N Line, Automatic DCT Trans, which is a different variant/trim than selected.
Includes: Adjust toe-in only. NOTE: Deduct .4 if alignment is also performed. NOTE: Manufacturer recommends removal of Rack and Pinion Steering Gear when replacing Inner Tie Rod End(s) on some models. If removal of the Rack and Pinion Gear is necessary, use Steering Gear (Complete) - R&R plus combinations.
