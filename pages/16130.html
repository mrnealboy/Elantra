Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the negative (-) battery cable.
  2. Remove the front bumper.

    (Refer to Body - "FRONT BUMPER ")

  3. Disconnect the front radar connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the front radar (A) from vehicle after loosening mounting nuts.

    Front radar installation nut: 

    9.5 - 10.5 N.m (0.97 - 1.07 kgf.m, 7.0 - 7.7 lb.ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
