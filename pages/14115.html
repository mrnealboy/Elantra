DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Rationality check
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Damaged wire harness
  3. Fuel tank pressure sensor
  4. PCSV
  5. Fuel cap is open
Case 1 Prerequisite Condition
  • The vehicle speed is lower than 30 km/h
  • The debouncing time for CCV opening is higher than 25 sec
  • the ambient Temp is between 0°C (32°F) and 42°C (107.6°F)
Threshold Value
  • The difference of two consecutive Fuel Tank Pressure is greater than 2.5 kPa
Diagnostic Time
  • 15 sec
Case 2 Prerequisite Condition
  • The BARO is greater than 74.5 kPa
  • The ambient Temp is between 0°C (32°F) and 42°C (107.6°F)
  • The fuel level is between 15% and 85%
  • The debouncing time for CCV opening is higher than 25 sec
Threshold Value
  • The fuel tank pressure is lower than -4.5 kPa or fuel tank pressure is higher than 6.1 kPa
Diagnostic Time
  • 14 sec
Case 3 Prerequisite Condition
  • The ambient Temp is between 0°C (32°F) and 42°C (107.6°F)
  • The vehicle speed is higher than 0 Km/h
  • The intake manifold press./amb. Press is lower than 0.62
  • The tank pressure is between -2.5 kPa and 2.5 kPa
  • The fuel level is between 15% and 85%
  • Δ Canister Purge flow is higher than 1.8kg
Threshold Value
  • The difference of raw Max Fuel Tank Pressure and Min Fuel Tank Pressure is lower than 10 Pa
Diagnostic Time
  • 100 msec
Case 4 Prerequisite Condition
  • The ambient Temp is between 0°C (32°F) and 42°C (107.6°F)
  • The BARO is greater than 74.5 kPa
  • The fuel level is between 15% and 85%
  • The vehicle speed is between 15 km/h and 88.5 km/h
  • CPSV and CCV plausibility check result or No error of CPSV & CCV after Leak monitoring
Threshold Value
  • The fuel tank pressure is lower than -2 kPa or fuel tank pressure is higher than 2 kPa
Diagnostic Time
  • 5 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
