DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Performance inspection
  1. Air cleaner is clogged
  2. Air leak in intake manifold
  3. Malfunction of EWGA and RCV oil leak or clog
  4. Boost pressure sensor
Prerequisite Condition
  • The engine revolution is greater than 300 RPM
Threshold Value
  • The Difference value between actual boost pressure and target boost pressure is greater than 30 kPa
Diagnostic Time
  • 3 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
