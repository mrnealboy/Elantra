Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    NOTE:
    • Before installing, check that the shift lever and manual control lever are placed in the "N" position.
    CAUTION:
    • When assembling shift cable, the projection (A) must face upwards.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • When assembling the snap pin, be careful about the correct direction.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
