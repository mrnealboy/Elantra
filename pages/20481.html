Repair Procedures: Removal: Battery

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  2. Disconnect the battery (-) terminal (A).

    Battery (-) terminal installation: 

    4.0 - 6.0 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 3.0 - 4.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the battery (+) terminal (A).

    Battery (+) terminal installation: 

    7.8 - 9.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.0 kgf.m, 5.8 - 7.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the battery mounting bracket (A).
  5. Remove the battery insulation pad after removing the battery (B).

    Battery mounting bracket installation bolt: 

    19.6 - 29.4 N.m (1.9 - 3.0 kgf.m, 13.7 - 30.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
