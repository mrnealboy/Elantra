Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery (-) terminal.
  2. Open the fuel filler door and unfasten the filler-neck assembly mounting screw (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Lift the vehicle.
  4. Remove the rear-LH wheel, tire, and the inner wheel house.
  5. Separate the fuel filler hose (A).
  6. Separate the ventilation hose (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the filler-neck assembly (B) after loosening the nut (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8.8 - 9.8 N.m (0.9 - 1.0 kgf.m, 6.5 - 7.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
