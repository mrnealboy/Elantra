Repair Procedures: Removal

Information 

  • Use a plastic panel removal tool to remove interior trim pieces without marring the surface.
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the AVN Head unit.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "AVN (AUDIO VIDEO NAVIGATION) HEAD UNIT ")

  3. Loosen the mounting screws and remove the ADAS_PRK Unit (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the ADAS_PRK Unit connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
