Passenger Side Impact Sensor (PSIS)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Side Impact Sensor are installed in the lower part of the B-pillar respectively.

The SRSCM detects the impact signals of the Pressure-Side Impact Sensor and the Side Impact Sensor and compares with signals of the safe sensor inside. If the signals of all sensors are judged as a collision, the side-airbag and the curtain-airbag would be unfolded.

NOTE: At inspecting the pressure sensor, keep the door airtight for an exact measuring.
