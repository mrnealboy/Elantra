Front Door Lock Module Inspection

  1. Remove the front door trim.

    (Refer to Body - "FRONT DOOR TRIM ")

  2. Remove the front door module.

    (Refer to Body - "FRONT DOOR MODULE ")

  3. Disconnect the connector from the actuator.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    No Pin Information
    LH RH
    1 Key Lock switch CL motor
    2 Key Unlock switch COM motor
    3 COM -
    4 Lock/Unlock switch AJAR switch
    5 AJAR switch Lock/Unlock switch
    6 - COM
    7 COM motor Key Unlock switch
    8 CL motor Key Lock switch
  4. Check actuator operation by connecting power and ground according to the table. To prevent damage to the actuator, apply battery voltage only momentarily.
