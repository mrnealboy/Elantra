Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF, and then disconnect the battery nagative (-) terminal.
  2. Lift the vehicle.
  3. Disconnect the canister close valve connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the vent hose (A).
  5. Loosen the fuel tank air filter mounting bolt (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Lifting the fixed part (A) and then rotating fuel tank air filter (B) 30 degrees in the direction of the clockwise.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the canister close valve (A) by rotating it 30 degrees.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
