Monitor Current Data

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn the ignition switch ON and warm up the vehicle up to an operational temperature.
  3. Select "In-car Temperature Sensor - Front" parameter on the "Current Data" of the GDS.
