DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor signals
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. O2 Sensor (Up)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Exhaust gas temperature is at between 350°C (662°F) and 900°C (1652°F)
  • ECT is greater than 55°C (131°F)
  • Required lambda: 1
  • Exhaust gas mass flow is between 4.2g/sec and 55.6g/sec
  • Accumulated exhaust gas mass is higher than 150g
Threshold Value
  • Secondary control variable by Downstream O2 sensor is lower than -0.08
Diagnostic Time
  • 20 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
