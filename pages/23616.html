Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the negative (-) battery cable.
  2. Release the parking brake.
  3. Remove the floor console assembly. (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ASSEMBLY ")
  4. Loosen the adjusting nut (A) of parking brake cable adjuster.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the parking brake cable (B) from the equalizer assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  7. Remove the rear wheel and tire (A) from the rear hub.
    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the rear wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the return spring (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Disconnect the parking cable (B) after remove the parking brake cable fixing clip (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Remove the parking brake cable bracket after loosening the mounting bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    19.6 - 29.4 N.m (2.0 - 3.0 kgf.m, 14.5 - 21.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Remove the floor under cover (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Remove the parking brake cable bracket mounting bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Remove the parking brake cable after loosening the mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
