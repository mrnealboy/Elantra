Valve relay

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The ESP supplies battery power to all solenoid valves with a valve relay which is controlled by the Electronic Control UNIT (ECU).

The valve relay and all solenoid valves are installed inside the ESP (Electronic Stability Program).
