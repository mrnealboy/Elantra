Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Fold the rear seat back assembly (A) by pulling the folding lever in the direction of the arrow.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Loosen the mounting clips and bolts, remove the rear seat folding lever (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
