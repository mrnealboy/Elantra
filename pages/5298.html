[Rear torsion beam parking cable type]

  1. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  2. Remove the rear wheel and tire (A) from the rear hub.

    Tightening torque:  107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the rear wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Loosen the brake hose bolt (A) from the caliper separate the brake hose.

    Tightening torque:  24.5 - 29.4 N.m (2.5 - 3.0 kgf.m, 18.1 - 21.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the return spring (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the parking brake cable (B) after remove fixing clip (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the caliper body (A) by loosening the guide rod bolt.

    Tightening torque:  21.6 - 31.4 N.m (2.2 - 3.2 kgf.m, 15.9 - 23.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the brake pad (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the pad liner (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the caliper carrier (A) by loosening the caliper mouniting bolts.

    Tightening torque:  78.5 - 98.1 N.m (8.0 - 10.0 kgf.m, 57.9 - 72.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Remove the front brake disc (A) by loosening the screws.

    Tightening torque:  4.9 - 5.8 N.m (0.5 - 0.6 kgf.m, 3.6 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
