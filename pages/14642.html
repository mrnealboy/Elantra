DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor RF signals
  1. Faulty TPMS Sensor
Prerequisite Condition
  • Ignition switch ON
  • Voltage of BCM/IBU is normal
Threshold Value
  • When BCM/IBU receives more than 3 signals consecutively about abnormal data among data from learned sensor
Diagnostic Time
  • Occurs when 180 seconds are maintained after 3 consecutive times of reception
Lamp
  • ON
