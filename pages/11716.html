Repair Procedures: Replacement

NOTE:
  • When installing the belt, make sure not to damage the pretensioner.
  1. Remove the center pillar upper trim. (Refer to Interior Trim - "CENTER PILLAR TRIM ")
  2. Remove the center pillar lower trim. (Refer to Interior Trim - "CENTER PILLAR TRIM ")
  3. After loosening the front belt upper anchor mounting bolt, remove the front belt upper anchor (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.8 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Separate the seat belt retractor connector (A) and pre-active seat belt connector (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Loosen the mounting bolt (B) and (C), remove the front seat belt retractor (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    Bolt B: 7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Bolt C: 39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.9 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Install in the reverse order of removal.

    Information 

    • After installing the front seat belt retractor, check that the air bag warning indicator illuminates for about 6 seconds when the ignition is ON and then goes out.
    • Make sure the connector is plugged in properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
