Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • Thoroughly clean all parts to be assembled.
  • Always use new cylinder head and manifold gaskets.
  • Always use new cylinder head bolts. Cylinder head bolts are toque-to-yield bolts designed to be permanently elongated beyond the state of elasticity when torqued, so if the bolts are removed and reused, it may cause the bolts to break or fail to maintain clamping force.
  • The cylinder head gasket is a metal gasket. Take care not to bend it.
  • Rotate the crankshaft to set the No. 1 piston at TDC (Top dead center) on compression stroke.
  1. Install the cylinder head gasket on the cylinder block.
    1. Remove hardening sealant, oil, dust, moisture and harmful foreign materials from the cylinder block and the cylinder head.
    2. Apply liquid gasket on the edge of the cylinder block.
    3. Install the cylinder head gasket with the dowel pins of the cylinder block.
    4. Apply liquid gasket on the edge of the cylinder head gasket.
      NOTE:
      • Apply liquid gasket as shown.

      Sealant:  Threebond 1217H or equivalent
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      CAUTION:
      • Assemble the cylinder head gasket and the cylinder head within 5 minutes after applying sealant.
  2. Place the cylinder head (A) carefully to protect damage to the head gasket (B) during installation.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install the cylinder head bolts with washers.

    Using SST (09221-4A000), install and tighten the 10 cylinder head bolts, in several passes, in the sequence as shown.

    Tightening torque 

    1st step:

    32.4 - 36.3 N.m (3.3 - 3.7 kgf.m, 23.9 - 26.8 lb-ft)

    2nd step: 90-95°

    3rd step: 90-95°

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Fig 1: Cylinder Head Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Do not reuse cylinder head bolts, always use new cylinder head bolts.
    • Do not apply engine oil on the bolt threads to achieve correct torque.
    • Remove the extruded sealant within 5 minutes after installing cylinder head bolts.
    • The engine running or pressure test should not be performed within 30 minutes after installing cylinder head bolts.
  4. Install the ignition coil.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "IGNITION COIL ")

  5. Install the Hydraulic lash adjuster (HLA) (A) and swing arm (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    1. When installing HLA, it should be held upright so that engine oil in HLA may not spill and assured that dust does not adhere to HLA.
    2. HLA should be inserted carefully to the cylinder head not to spill engine oil.

      If engine oil has spilled out of the lash adjuster, HLA bleeding should be performed according to the below procedure.

      CAUTION:
      • Stroke HLA in engine oil 4 - 5 times by pushing its cap while pushing the ball down slightly by hard steel wire.

        (Take care not to severely push hard steel wire down since ball is several grams.)

        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Install the cam carrier
    1. The sealant locations on the cam carrier and the cylinder head must be free of harmful foreign materials, oil, dust and moisture. Spray cleaner on the surface and wipe with a clean duster.
    2. Applying liquid sealant on the bottom surface of the cam carrier.

      Continuous bead of sealant should be applied to prevent any path from oil leakage.

      Bead width (A):  3.0 - 3.5 mm (0.118 - 0.138 in.)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Apply sealant carefully to prevent sealant from seeping into the PCV hole and the fresh gases inlet hole.
      • After assembly, check whether sealant is in-flowed inside the hole.
      • The sealant locations on the cam carrier and the cylinder head must be free of harmful foreign materials, oil, dust and moisture.
      • There should be no over application or unapplicition of sealant around PCV hole and the fresh gases inlet hole.
    3. Place the cam carrier on the cylinder head. The dowel pins on the cam carrier and holes on the cylinder head should be used as a reference in order to assemble the cam carrier in exact position.
    4. Fasten the cam carrier bolts (A).

      Tightening torque: 

      18.6 - 22.6 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      CAUTION:
      • Assemble the cam carrier within 5 minutes after applying sealant.
      • Assemble the camshaft bearing cap within 5 minutes after assembling the cam carrier.
      • The engine running or pressure test should not be performed within 30 minutes after assembling the cam carrier.
  7. Install the CVVT & Camshaft (A).

    [Intake CVVT & Camshaft assembly] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Exhaust CVVT & Camshaft assembly] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Install the camshaft front bearing cap (A) and camshaft bearing caps (B).

    Tightening torque 

    M6 Bolts: 11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    M8 Bolts: 18.6 - 22.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Install the camshaft bearing caps, in the sequence as shown.
      Fig 2: Camshaft Bearing Caps Bolts Tightening Sequence
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Install the order parts in the reverse order of removal.
  10. Add all the necessary fluids and check for leaks.
    NOTE:
    • When changing the coolant and draining air, be sure to follow the filling instructions of the Integrated Thermal Management Module (ITM).
  11. Start engine and check for leaks.
