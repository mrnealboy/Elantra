Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  3. Drain the coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  4. Remove the cooling fan assembly.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLING FAN ")

  5. Disconnect the radiator lower hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Disconnect the radiator upper hose (A)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the front bumper.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "FRONT BUMPER ")

  8. Remove the air guard (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 Nm (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the side air guard (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 Nm (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Loosen the intercooler mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Remove the radiator upper mounting bracket bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 Nm (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Separate the A/C condenser from the radiator and then remove the radiator (A) from the vehicle.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Install in the reverse order of removal.
  14. Fill with engine coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE:
    • The coolant must be injected according to the integrated thermal management module (ITM) coolant filling method.
  15. Start engine and check for leaks.
