Repair procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
    WARNING:
    • Disconnect the battery negative cable, and wait for at least three minutes before beginning work.
  2. Remove the front seat assembly. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY ")
  3. Remove the seat back cover. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "FRONT SEAT BACK COVER ")
    NOTE:
    • When the front side airbag has deployed after a collision, replace the seat back as an assembly.
  4. Remove the side airbag (B) after loosening the mounting nuts (A).
