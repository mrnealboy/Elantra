Repair Procedures: Replacement

NOTE:
  • When installing the belt, make sure not to damage the retractor.
  1. Remove the package tray trim.

    (Refer to Interior Trim - "PACKAGE TRAY TRIM ")

  2. After loosening the mounting bolt, remove the rear seat belt lower anchor (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.8 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Separate the rear seat belt connector (A) and loosen the mounting bolt, remove the rear seat belt retractor (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.8 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install in the reverse order of removal.

    Information 

    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
