Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  2. Remove the front wheel and tire (A) from the front hub.

    Tightening torque: 

    107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the front wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the front brake caliper. (Refer to Brake System - "FRONT DISC BRAKE ")
  4. Loosen the screw and the remove the front disc (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    4.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.6 kgf.m, 3.6 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. By hammering on a chisel, unlock the driveshaft lock hub nut caulking.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • If there is screw thread (A) on the end of the nut, unlock the caulking by using a chisel and then loosen the nut to prevent damaging driveshaft screw thread.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the driveshaft caulking nut (A) from the front hub.

    Tightening torque: 

    274.6 - 294.2 N.m (28.0 - 30.0 kgf.m, 202.5 - 217.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • The driveshaft lock nut must be replaced with new one.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • When replacing the drive lock hub nut, use only the nut with screw thread (A) on the end.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • Tighten the driveshaft lock hub nut to the specified tightening torque, and caulk by using a chisel and hammer.
    • If there are two key seats, perform on all two seats.

      Caulking depth (A):  1.5 mm (0.0591 in)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Loosen the mounting bolts from the knuckle and then remove the hub bearing assembly and dust cover (A) by using the plastic hammer.

    Tightening torque: 

    78.5 - 98.1 N.m (8.0 - 10.0 kgf.m, 57.9 - 72.3 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • When removing the drive shaft, use a plastic hammer in order to avoid shock applied to the front knuckle.
    • When removing the drive shaft, do not forcibly pull or excessively bend the front knuckle to separate.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the tie rod end ball joint by using the special service tool.
    1. Remove the split pin and castle nut (A).
    2. Remove the tie rod end ball joint (B) by using the SST (09568-1S100).

      Tightening torque: 

      34.3 - 44.1 N.m (3.5 - 4.5 kgf.m, 25.3 - 32.5 lb-ft)

      NOTE:
      • Do not reuse castle nut.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the wheel speed sensor (A) from the knuckle after loosening the mounting bolt.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Separate the lower arm ball joint by using the SST (09568-1S100) after loosening the lower arm mounting nut (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    68.6 - 80.4 N.m (7.0 - 8.2 kgf.m, 50.6 - 59.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Remove the front knuckle (A) after loosening the bolts and nuts.

    Tightening torque: 

    156.9 - 176.5 N.m (16.0 - 18.0 kgf.m, 115.7 - 130.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
