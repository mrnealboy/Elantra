Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the dual clutch transmission from the vehicle. (Refer to "REPAIR PROCEDURES ")
  2. Remove the retaining (A) and then removing the spline hub (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the snap ring (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the dual clutch assembly (A) by using the special service tool [SST No.: 09430-C1180].
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
