Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery (-) terminal.
  2. Remove the relay box (Passenger Compartment).

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "RELAY BOX (PASSENGER COMPARTMENT) ")

  3. Disconnect the DC/DC converter connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the DC/DC converter (A) after loosening mounting nuts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
