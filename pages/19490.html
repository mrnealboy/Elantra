Monitor GDS Data

  1. Connect the GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Ignition switch ON and the Engine OFF, select "Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC)" mode.
  3. Monitor "Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC)".
  4. Using the GDS, clear the DTC.
  5. Adults needs to be sat down on the passenger seat.
  6. Monitor the "OCS Type2 Real Time Stauts-Passenger Of "Current Data" parameter on the GDS.
