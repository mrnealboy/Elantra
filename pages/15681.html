DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor input signal (Local CAN Tx)
  1. Unconnected door handle connector
  2. Unconnected connector of the door inline grommet
  3. Bending, deterioration or damage of the door handle connector pins
  4. Simultaneous short to battery or ground in the Local CAN HIGH LOW line
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn the ignition switch ON.
Threshold Value
  • No receiving message from the door handle to the IAU* during the timeout period. And this persists for more than 10 sec. (Including 1.5 sec of the time out).
Diagnosis Time
  • It persists for 10 sec. (Including 1.5 sec of the time out)
DTC Clearing
  • The present DTC is immediately cleared when the controller receives the message
  • The present/history DTC is cleared as soon as there is a command to clear the DTC
  • The history DTC is cleared when there is no present DTC and the ignition switch is turned ON/OFF for 30 consecutive times.
NOTE: IAU*: "Identity Authentication Unit"
IBU*: "Integrated Body control Unit"
