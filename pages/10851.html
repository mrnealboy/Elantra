Auto Defog Actuator

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The auto defog system monitors the humidity inside a vehicle with a sensor installed at the front glass. It allows a driver to enjoy safe driving by curbing and eliminating the moisture at in advance and thus improving visibility and convenience of driving. The driver can predetermine the threshold value of in-car temperature and wind. Then the system will automatically activate defogging if the moisture on the front window surface exceeds the specified level. If the humidity drops, it will be turned off and go back to the predetermined setting. The system controls the intake actuator, A/C, auto defog actuator (Auxiliary DEF actuator), blower motor, and mode actuator according to the humidity of the surface.
