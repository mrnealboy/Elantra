Center muffler & Catalytic converter assembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the front muffler heat protector (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Remove the center muffler & catalytic converter assembly (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 58.8 N.m (4.0 - 6.0 kgf.m, 28.9 - 43.4 lb-ft)

    Fig 1: Center Muffler & Catalytic Converter Assembly Component Location
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When installing, replace with new gaskets.
  3. Install in the reverse order of removal.
