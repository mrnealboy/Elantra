Monitor Scantool Data

  1. Ignition switch OFF, connect GDS.
  2. Ignition switch ON and the engine OFF, select "Current Data" mode.
  3. Monitor the "Adaptive load limiter resistance, Driver" parameter on the GDS.
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Reference  : Resistance of "Adaptive load limiter, Driver" is between 1.0Ω and 6.9Ω

Reference  : In a case of an open in the "Adaptive load limiter, Driver" circuit: FAIL

In a case of a short to battery in the "Adaptive load limiter, Driver" circuit: FAIL

In a case of a short to ground in the "Adaptive load limiter, Driver" circuit: FAIL
