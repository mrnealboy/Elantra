Passenger Pressure Side Impact Sensor

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Pressure-Side Impact Sensor is installed in the left and the right side of the front door respectively.

The Pressure-Side Impact Sensor senses an impact based on an acceleration at crashing. Unlike the conventional acceleration sensor, the Pressure Sensor senses an air pressure by a distorted door at crashing and measures an impact.

The SRSCM detects the impact signals of the Pressure-Side Impact Sensor and the Side Impact Sensor and compares with signals of the safe sensor inside. If the signals of all sensors are judged as a collision, the side-airbag and the curtain-airbag would be unfolded.

NOTE: At inspecting the pressure sensor, keep the door airtight for an exact measuring.
