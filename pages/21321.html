Monitor GDS Data

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Lift vehicle with suitable lift and Engine "ON".
  3. Operate vehicle or system in accordance with Enable Condition in DTC Detecting Condition.
  4. Move selector lever from "P" range to "D" range. And Driving at 1st gear to 7th gear.
  5. Monitor "Shift Motor 1, 2 position" parameter in "Current Data" with GDS.
