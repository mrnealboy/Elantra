Lateral G Sensor/Longitudinal G Sensor/Yaw Rate Sensor

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The yaw rate sensor, lateral G sensor, longitudinal G sensor are packaged in the IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit).

If the yaw velocity reaches a certain point after IMU detects the vehicle's yawing, the ESP (ESC) control is activated

IMU is installed in SRSCM and it interchanges signals with ESP through extra CAN line which is only used for communication between ESP and the IMU.
