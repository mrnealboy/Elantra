Brake Hose [Rear]

  1. Disconnect the brake fluid level sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Remove the brake fluid from the master cylinder reservoir with a syringe.
    NOTE:
    • Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle, it may damage the paint; if brake fluid does contact the paint, wash it off immediately with water.
  3. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  4. Remove the front wheel and tire (A) from the front hub.

    Tightening torque:  107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the front wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the brake hose clip (A).
  6. Disconnect the tube after loosening the tube flare nut (B).

    Tightening torque:  12.7 - 16.7 N.m (1.3 - 1.7 kgf.m, 9.4 - 12.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the hose after loosening the brake hose bolt (A) from the caliper.

    Tightening torque:  24.5 - 29.4 N.m (2.5 - 3.0 kgf.m, 18.1 - 21.7 lb-ft)

    [Rear torsion beam parking cable type] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
