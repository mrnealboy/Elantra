Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • Prolonged and repeated contact with mineral oil will result in the removal of natural fats from the skin, leading to dryness, irritation and dermatitis. In addition, used engine oil contains potentially harmful contaminants which may cause skin cancer.
  • Exercise caution in order to minimize the length and frequency of contact of your skin to used oil. Wear protective clothing and gloves. Wash your skin thoroughly with soap and water, or use water-less hand cleaner, to remove any used engine oil. Do not use gasoline, thinners, or solvents.
  • In order to preserve the environment, used oil and used oil filter must be disposed of only at designated disposal sites.
  1. Drain the engine oil.
    1. Remove the oil filler cap (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Remove the oil drain plug (A), and drain the oil into a container.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Replace the oil filter.
    1. Remove the safety pin (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Remove the pre-drain plug (A) by rotating it by 90° and then drain the engine oil in the oil filter.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Using the socket wrench, remove the oil filter cap (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Replace the new o-ring (A) on the oil filter cap.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Always use a new O-ring.
      • Wipe off the oil and foreign substances remaining inside the filter housing, threads and cover cap threads as much as possible.

        * If the residual oil is not removed, it may leak between the cover cap and the housing mating surfaces, which can be mistaken for oil leakage. (The amount of leaking oil and the leaking period may differ depending on the amount of residual oil)

    5. Install the new oil filter (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • When install the oil filter, apply engine oil to the drain plug O-ring (A).
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      • Must use the drain plug (A) that is installed in a new oil filter.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    6. Rotate the free drain plug (A) by 90° so that the drain pin is in the locked position.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    7. Install the new safety pin (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Always use a new safety pin.
    8. Lightly tighten the O-ring of the oil filter cap until it contacts with the installed surface, then tighten the oil filter cap (A) to the specified torque using a socket wrench.

      Tightening torque: 

      34.3 N.m (3.5 kgf.m, 25.3 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Always use a new O-ring.
      • When install the oil filter cap, Check if the new o-ring (A) is installed correctly the cap.
      • When install the oil filter cap, apply engine oil to the new O-ring (A).
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      • Assemble the drain plug to the specified torque using a torque wrench. * When manually assembling the drain plug using a hexagonal wrench, it may result in the damage to the drain plug or leakage due to the failure to comply with the specified torque.
  3. Refill with engine oil.
    1. Install the oil drain plug (A) with a new gasket.

      Tightening torque: 

      34.3 - 44.1 N.m (3.5 - 4.5 kgf.m, 25.3 - 32.5 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Fill with fresh engine oil.

      Capacity 

      Total: 5.0 L (1.32 U.S.gal., 5.28 U.S.qt., 4.4 lmp.qt.)

      Oil pan: 4.4 L (1.16 U.S.gal., 4.65 U.S.qt., 3.87 lmp.qt.)

      Drain and refill including oil filter:

      4.8 L (1.27 U.S.gal., 5.07 U.S.qt., 4.22 lmp.qt.)

    3. Install the oil filler cap (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Engine and check for oil leaks.
  5. Recheck the engine oil level.
