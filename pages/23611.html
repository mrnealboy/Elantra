Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the negative (-) battery cable.
  2. Release the parking brake.
  3. Remove the floor console assembly. (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ASSEMBLY ")
  4. Remove the floor console mounting bracket (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the parking brake switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Disconnect the parking brake cable (B) from the equalizer assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the parking brake lever assembly (A) after loosening the parking brake lever mounting bolts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
