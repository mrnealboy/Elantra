If Diagnostic Tool is available, change the coolant and drain air according to the descriptions below

  1. Make sure the engine and radiator are cool to the touch.
  2. Remove the coolant reservoir tank cap (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  4. Loosen the drain plug (A), and drain the coolant.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Tighten the radiator drain plug securely after draining the coolant.
  6. Clean the coolant reservoir tank after drain the coolant.
  7. IG Key OFF, Fill with fluid mixture of coolant and water (45 - 60%) slowly through the coolant reservoir tank cap.

    Coolant capacity 

    Approx. 6.8 L (1.79 US.gal., 7.19 US, qt., 5.98 Imp.qt.)

    NOTE:
    • Fill the coolant as much as possible with the engine OFF.
    • Press the upper/lower hoses on the radiator so that the air can get out easily.
    • Use only genuine antifreeze/coolant.
    • For best corrosion protection, the coolant concentration must be maintained year-round at 45 - 60% minimum. Coolant concentrations less than 45 - 60% may not provide sufficient protection against corrosion or freezing.
    • Coolant concentrations greater than 60% will impair cooling efficiency and are not recommended.
    • Do not mix different brands of antifreeze/coolants.
    • Do not use additional rust inhibitors or antirust products; they may not be compatible with the coolant.
  8. Connect the diagnostic tool and then Key 'IG ON'.
  9. Use the diagnostic tool to activate the "integrated thermal management module (ITM) coolant refilling mode".
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Complete the ITM coolant refilling mode according to the procedure of the diagnostic tool.
  11. Key-off and then disconnect the diagnostic tool.

    Information 

    Key-off: 

    • Turn Key: The start key has been removed from the keybox.
    • Button key (including SmartKey): Power (ACC, ON) is not activated.
  12. Install the coolant reservoir tank cap (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Check the coolant quantity after driving the vehicle.
  14. If the coolant is insufficient, replenish it.
  15. Install the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")
