DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Rationality check
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Leak or clog in intake air system
  3. O2 Sensor (Up)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Sensor Ceramic Temp. is higher than 685°C (1, 265°F)
  • ECT is higher than 55°C (131°F)
  • Purge active time is higher then 5 sec
  • Engine load is higher than 25% and lower than 130%
  • Fuel cut condition OFF
  • Closed loop fuel control
  • Engine speed is higher than 1320 RPM and lower than 3520 RPM
Threshold Value
  • Ratio of expected area to actual area is lower than 0.4
Diagnostic Time
  • -
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
