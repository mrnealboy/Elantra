GDS data Analysis

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn ignition switch "ON".
  3. Hit the brake pedal.
  4. Monitor the "Brake Light Switch" parameter on the "Current Data" menu of the GDS.

    Specification  : Brake Light Switch go on and off.
