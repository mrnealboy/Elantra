Mood lamp unit

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the main crash pad assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "MAIN CRASH PAD ASSEMBLY ")

  3. Loosen the mounting screws and remove the main crash pad air duct (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting screws and remove the mood lamp unit (A).
