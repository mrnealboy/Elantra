Actuation Test

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn the ignition switch "ON" & engine "OFF".
  3. Select the "Actuation Test" mode on the GDS.
  4. Inspect operating status of all valves with Actuation Test.

    Specification:  It's normal if operating sound is heard.
