Description & Operation: Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  • Components location:  DCT (Dual Clutch Transmission)

    (Refer to "COMPONENTS & COMPONENTS LOCATION ")

  • Function 

    The dual clutch is installed within the transmission.

    The dual clutch comprises an odd clutch and an even clutch. The odd clutch transfers and cuts off engine power to the transmission when shifting odd gears.

    The even clutch transfers and cuts off engine power to the transmission when shifting even gears.
