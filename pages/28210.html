Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Remove the glove box. (Refer to Crash Pad - "GLOVE BOX ")
  2. Remove the crash pad side cover [RH]. (Refer to Crash Pad - "CRASH PAD SIDE COVER ")
  3. (Remove the crash pad garnish [RH]. (Refer to Crash Pad - "CRASH PAD GARNISH ")
  4. Loosen the mounting screws and bolts, remove the glove box housing cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
