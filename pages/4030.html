DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the voltage
  1. Overheated Cockpit Mood Lamp 1*
  2. Open/Poor Connection in LED Power Circuit
  3. Short-to-Ground in LED Power Circuit
  4. Open/Poor Connection in LED Ground Circuit
  5. Short-to-Battery in LED Ground Circuit
  6. Faulty Cockpit Mood Lamp 1*
Prerequisite Condition
  • IG key "ON"
  • Mood Lamp "ON"
  • Power Voltage: Between 9V and 16V
Threshold Value Case 1
  • Cockpit Mood Lamp 1*'s temperature: Above 185°F
Case 2
  • Cockpit Mood Lamp 1*'s LED Circuit: Open
Case 3
  • Cockpit Mood Lamp 1*'s LED Circuit: Short
Diagnosis Time
  • 3 sec.
Fail-Safe
  • Cockpit Mood Lamp 1* LED: "OFF"
Clearing Condition
  • Change the color and brightness of the LED in the USM* after solve the problem
Component Name_ETM ETM
MLM* "MOOD LAMP UNIT" SD929-4
Cockpit Mood Lamp 1* "FRONT MOOD LAMP LH"
USM* "User Settings" in the cluster
