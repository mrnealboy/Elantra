Repair Procedures: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check the crankshaft bearing oil clearance.
    1. To check main bearing-to-journal oil clearance, remove the bedplate and lower bearings.
    2. Clean each main journal and bearing with a clean shop towel.
    3. Place one strip of plastigage across each main journal.
    4. Reinstall the bedplate and lower bearings, and then tighten the main bolts.

      Tightening torque: 

      27.5 - 31.4 N.m (2.8-3.2 kgf.m, 20.3 - 23.1 lb-ft) + 120 - 125°

      NOTE:
      • Do not turn the crankshaft.
    5. Remove the bedplate and lower bearings.

      Bearing oil clearance: 

      0.014 - 0.032 mm (0.00055 - 0.00126 in.)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    6. If the plastigage measures too wide or too narrow, remove the upper and lower bearing and then install a new bearings with the same color mark. Recheck the oil clearance.
      NOTE:
      • Do not file, shim, or scrape the bearings or the caps to adjust clearance.
    7. If the plastigage shows the clearance is still incorrect, try the next larger or smaller bearing. Recheck the oil clearance.

      (Refer to selection Chart For Crankshaft Bearings - in below charts)

      NOTE:
      • If the proper clearance cannot be obtained by using the appropriate larger or smaller bearings, replace the crankshaft and start over.
      NOTE:
      • If the marks are indecipherable because of an accumulation of dirt and dust, do not scrub them with a wire brush or scraper. Clean them only with solvent or detergent.

      Crankshaft Bore Identification Mark 

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

  2. Check crankshaft end play.

    Using a dial indicator, measure the thrust clearance while prying the crankshaft back and forth with a screwdriver.

    If the end play is greater than maximum, replace the center bearing.

    Crankshaft end play 

    Standard: 0.100 - 0.280 mm (0.00394 - 0.01102 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Inspect main journals and crank pins.

    Using a micrometer, measure the diameter of each main journal and crank pin.

    Main journal diameter: 

    54.942 - 54.960 mm (2.16307 - 2.16378 in.)

    Crank pin diameter: 

    44.954 - 44.972 mm (1.76984 - 1.77055 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
