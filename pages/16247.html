Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the IVTF drain plug allow the fluid to drain out and then reinstall the drain plug.

    (Refer to Hydraulic System - "FLUID ")

  2. Remove the Intelligent Variable Transmission (IVT).

    (Refer to Intelligent Variable Transmission (IVT) System (C0GF1) - "REPAIR PROCEDURES ")

  3. Remove the torque convertor (A) from convertor housing.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the bolts and nut and then removing the position switch (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    Manual lever mounting nut: 17.7 - 24.5 N.m (1.8 - 2.5 kgf.m, 13.0 - 18.1 lb-ft)

    Position switch mounting bolt: 9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Loosen the bolts and then removing the tube (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the bolts and then removing the torque convertor (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    27.5 - 34.3 N.m (2.8 - 3.5 kgf.m, 20.1 - 25.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Loosen the bolt and then removing the manual guide (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    19.6 - 26.5 N.m (2.0 - 2.7 kgf.m, 14.5 - 19.5 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the bolts (A) and then removing the manual shaft (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the parking rod assembly (A) in the "F" direction.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Remove the parking rod cam (A) from parking rod assembly.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
