Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the battery (-) terminals.
  2. Remove the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ASSEMBLY ")

  3. Remove the UBS port assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the AVN head unit.

    (Refer to AVN System - "AVN (AUDIO VIDEO NAVIGATION) HEAD UNIT ")

  5. Disconnect the USB connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Disconnect the USB connector (B). [Display Audio only]
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the USB jack (A) after releasing the fixed hooks.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
