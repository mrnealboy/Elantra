ESP (ESC) Control Module

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The VSM (Vehicle Stability Management) is a safety function which prevents a vehicle from crashing with the integrated control by the FCA/SCC (Smart Cruise Control) centered on the ESP (ESC). The FCA/SCC computes the likelihood of a collision with a radar that senses the speed and gap of a vehicle ahead. In case of crashing it reduces damage from it through the ESP (ESC) controlling the FCA/SCC system.

When a driver's vehicle is about to crash, the FCA/SCC measures the distance from the car ahead and makes a decision accordingly.

If collision is expected, the ESP (ESC) brakes the vehicle even thought the driver doesn't hit a brake pedal by actuating motor to reduce damage of crash.

The ESP (ESC) exchanges signals with the ECM, TCM, FCA/SCC via CAN lines in order to control VSM properly.
