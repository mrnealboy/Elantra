DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor ECV output voltage or current
  1. Poor contact/open in wire harness
  2. ECV connection connector is not connected
  3. Open in power circuit
  4. Open in ground circuit
  5. Faulty ECV
  6. Faulty A/C control module
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn the ignition switch ON
Threshold Value
  • Case 1: Within 500 ms after the ignition switch is turned on, if the STATUR terminal of the ECV control IC is FAULT for more than 100 ms
  • Case 2: When ECV F/B is less than 100 mA for more than 4 seconds when ignition switch is ON condition
Fail Safe
  • ECV does not output
