DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • During auto-learning, Check its sensor's ID
  1. Faulty TPMS sensor
  2. Interference by an external cause
  3. Faulty Vehicle Speed CAN signal
Prerequisite Condition
  • Ignition switch ON
  • Sensor is normal
  • Voltage of BCM/IBU is normal
  • The vehicle speed is above 35 Km/h (22 mile/h) for 9 (EU, DOM)/19 (NA) minutes
Threshold Value
  • 9 (EU, DOM)/19 (NA) minutes after the completion of the learning mode if BCM/IBU does not learn the sensor
Diagnostic Time
  • 9 (EU, DOM)/19 (NA) minutes
Lamp
  • OFF
