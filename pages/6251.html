Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the AVN Head unit.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "AVN (AUDIO VIDEO NAVIGATION) HEAD UNIT ")

  3. Loosen the mounting screws and remove the start/stop button (A).
