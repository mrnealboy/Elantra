Assembly Check

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn ignition switch "ON".
  3. Select "S/W Management" menu of the GDS.
  4. Perform "Assembly Check (ECU replacement)" in "S/W Management". (Fig.1).
