Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Remove the front door trim. (Refer to Front Door - "FRONT DOOR TRIM ")
  2. Cut the heat-welded part with a knife and loosen the mounting screw (B).
  3. Remove the front door inside handle (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Be careful of breakage and cut the heat-welded part with a knife.
