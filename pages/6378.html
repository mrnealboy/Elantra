Removal and Installation

  1. Drain the coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  2. Disconnect the degassing hose (A) and coolant reservoir tank hose (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the coolant reservoir tank (A).

    Tightening torque:  7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install in the reverse order of removal.
  5. Fill with engine coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE: The coolant must be injected according to the integrated thermal management module (ITM) coolant filling method.
