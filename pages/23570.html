Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  2. Remove the rear wheel and tire (A) from the rear hub.

    Tightening torque: 

    107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the rear wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the rear brake caliper. (Refer to Brake System - "REAR DISC BRAKE ")
  4. Loosen the screw and the remove the rear break disc (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    4.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.6 kgf.m, 3.6 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the rear wheel speed sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the hub bearing assembly after loosening the hub bearing mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    88.3 - 107.9 N.m (9.0 - 11.0 kgf.m, 65.1 - 79.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the dust cover (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.9 - 10.8 N.m (0.7 - 1.1 kgf.m, 5.1 - 8.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the rear lower arm mounting bolt and nut (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    117.7 - 132.4 N.m (12.0 - 13.5 kgf.m, 86.8 - 97.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Loosen the rear assist arm mounting bolt and nut (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    137.3 - 156.9 N.m (14.0 - 16.0 kgf.m, 101.3 - 115.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Loosen the rear upper arm mounting bolt and nut (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    137.3 - 156.9 N.m (14.0 - 16.0 kgf.m, 101.3 - 115.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Loosen the trailing arm mounting bolt and nut (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    137.3 - 156.9 N.m (14.0 - 16.0 kgf.m, 101.3 - 115.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Remove the rear axle carrier.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
