DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible cause
Main action
  • Monitor the steering angle sensor signal
  1. Incorrect calibration of steering angle sensor (SAS)
  2. Faulty steering angle sensor
  3. Incorrect installation of SAS
  4. Incorrect wheel alignment
  5. SAS power supply failure
Threshold value Case 1
  • A failure is detected when the steering wheel is out of the physical specific range for more than 600 milliseconds
Case 2
  • A failure is detected when sensor values has changed more than 40 degree for more than 40 milliseconds
Case 3
  • In case of straight and stable driving over 30 km/h (18.6 MPH), a failure is detected if it is more than 25° to difference between previous SAS offset and mean of estimated SAS offset.
  • A failure is detected when the fault signal of SAS Failsafe Logic is transmitted (SAS status), received SAS message's Alive counter fault, checksum value fault, or CRC fault
    • Driving or Control: 100 milliseconds
    • Standstill: 500 milliseconds
    • MDPS Initialization while standstill: 1 second
MIL
  • ESP (ESC) warning lamp is activated
Reset condition Case 1, 2
  • After ignition switch turned off and on again, driving the vehicle over 12 km/h (7.45 MPH) and SAS signal is in normal condition.
Case 3
  • After ignition switch turned OFF and ON, when SAS signal is normal.
