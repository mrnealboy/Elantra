DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the CAN data
  1. Open/Poor Connection in M-CAN High line
  2. Open/Poor Connection in M-CAN Low line
  3. Short both M-CAN High line and Low line coincidentally
Prerequisite Condition
  • Power Voltage: Between 9V and 16V
  • M-CAN communication: Active
Threshold Value Case 1
  • M-CAN "High" line error
Case 2
  • M-CAN "Low" line error
Diagnosis Time
  • 2 sec

Cluster* "INSTRUMENT CLUSTER" SD200-8
