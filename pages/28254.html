Outside Combination Lamp

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the combination lamp cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the rear combination lamp connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting nuts and remove the rear combination lamp (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the socket (A) and replace the bulb (B).
