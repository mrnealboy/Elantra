ECTS #1

  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal.
  2. Remove the intercooler outlet hose.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "INTERCOOLER ")

  3. Disconnect the ETC connector.
  4. Loosen the mounting bolt (A).

    Tightening Torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)
