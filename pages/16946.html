Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Remove the rear door module. (Refer to Rear Door - "REAR DOOR MODULE ")
  2. Remove the rear door inside handle cage (A) by pulling it in the direction of the arrow.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Separate the rear door outside handle connector wiring harness (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the rear door latch assembly (A) by pulling it in the direction of the arrow.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the rear door outside handle base (A) by pushing in the lock pins located in the back.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Slide the locking pin (A) in the direction of the arrow to release the lock and then remove the rod (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector is connected properly.
    • Make sure the door locks/unlocks and opens/closes properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
