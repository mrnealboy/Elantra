DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Performance inspection
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Throttle stuck
  3. Electronic Throttle Control (ETC) motor
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn ignition switch ON
Threshold Value
  • When the difference between actual and target throttle position is higher than limit value after ETC motor drives
Diagnostic Time
  • 0.2 sec
MIL
  • 1st Driving Cycle
