The Type Can Replace the Wheel Side Joint Boot

  1. Remove the front drive shaft.

    (Refer to "FRONT DRIVESHAFT ")

  2. Remove the transaxle side joint.

    (Refer to "JOINT ASSEMBLY (TRANSAXLE SIDE) ")

  3. Remove the dynamic damper.

    (Refer to "DYNAMIC DAMPER ")

  4. Remove the wheel side joint small diameter and large diameter boot band using driver (-).
  5. Remove the wheel side joint boot .
NOTE:
  • Do not remove the wheel side joint housing .
  • Reinstall the wheel side joint housing may cause leakage, so replace it with the shaft as an assembly.
