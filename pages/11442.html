Monitor GDS Data

  1. Ignition switch OFF, connect GDS.
  2. Ignition switch ON and the engine OFF, select "Current Data" mode.
  3. Monitor the "Passenger Buckle Switch status" parameter on the GDS.
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Reference  : Buckle Switch on: Buckled, Buckle Switch off: Unbuckled
