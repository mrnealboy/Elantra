Monitor DTC Status

  1. Connect the GDS and select "Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC)" mode.
  2. Turn ignition switch ON.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn ignition switch OFF.
  5. Disconnect the vehicle power battery terminal (-) for 10 sec and reconnect.
  6. Turn the ignition switch ON.
  7. Dose the same DTC return.
