DTC Description

C162800 is set when the CGW* cannot receives the C-CAN/M-CAN message from the Cluster* although the C-CAN/M-CAN Bus is normal.

It can occur when there is a Faulty Power Supply to the Cluster*, or an Open/Poor Connection in the High line or the Low line of the Cluster*'s branch circuit.
