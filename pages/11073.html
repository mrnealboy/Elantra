Repair procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
    WARNING:
    • Disconnect the battery negative cable, and wait for at least three minutes before beginning work.
  2. Remove the main crash pad assembly. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "MAIN CRASH PAD ASSEMBLY ")
  3. Loosen the passenger airbag mounting nuts (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the PAB module connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the PAB module (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • If the crash pad is damaged by the passenger's airbag deployment, replace the crash pad at the same time.
    CAUTION:
    • The removed airbag module is placed with the airbag cover side facing up.
