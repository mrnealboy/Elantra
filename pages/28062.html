Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Remove the front seat assembly.

    (Refer to Front Seat - "FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  2. Remove the rear seat assembly.

    (Refer to Rear Seat - "REAR SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  3. Remove the front pillar trim.

    (Refer to Interior Trim - "FRONT PILLAR TRIM ")

  4. Remove the center pillar upper trim.

    (Refer to Interior Trim - "CENTER PILLAR TRIM ")

  5. Remove the rear pillar trim.

    (Refer to Interior Trim - "REAR PILLAR TRIM ")

  6. Remove the sunvisor and retainer.

    (Refer to Roof Trim - "Sunvisor - SUNVISOR ")

  7. Remove the assist handle.

    (Refer to Roof Trim - "ASSIST HANDLE ")

  8. Remove the overhead console lamp.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "OVERHEAD CONSOLE LAMP ")

  9. Remove the room lamp.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "ROOM LAMP ")

  10. Disconnect the rear view mirror connector.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "ELECTRO CHROMIC INSIDE REAR VIEW MIRROR ")

  11. Disconnect the rain sensor connector.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "RAIN SENSOR ")

  12. Disconnect the roof trim main connector (A) and the mounting clips in the front pillar.

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Disconnect the sunroof motor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the rear window glass.

    (Refer to "REAR WINDOW GLASS (EXCEPT HEV) ")

  15. Remove the roof trim mounting clip and remove the roof trim assembly (A).
    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to wrinkle on the roof trim.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Remove roof trim wiring harness (A) from the roof trim.
    NOTE:
    • Take care not to damage the roof trim wiring harness (A) when removing.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    1. When installing the roof trim wiring harness (A) on the roof trim, apply the hot melt continuously by using the hot melt gun tool.
    2. Put anti-noise pad on roof trim to prevent the noise.
  17. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector is connected in properly.
    • Make sure the connector are connected in properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
