Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  2. Drain the engine oil.

    (Refer to "ENGINE OIL ")

  3. Using the SST (09215-3C000) and remove the oil pan (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Insert the SST between the oil pan and the ladder frame by tapping it with a plastic hammer in the direction of (1) arrow.
    • After tapping the SST with a plastic hammer along the direction of (2) arrow around more than 2/3 edge of the oil pan, remove it from the cylinder block.
    NOTE:
    • Do not turn over the SST abruptly without tapping. It be result in damage of the SST.
