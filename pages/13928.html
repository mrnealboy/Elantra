DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Electrical inspection
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Short to battery in power circuit of sensor B (Sensor B: APS2)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn ignition switch on
Threshold Value
  • Reference Voltage (5V) is higher than 5.1V
Diagnostic Time
  • Continuously
MIL
  • 1st Driving Cycle
