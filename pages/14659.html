DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor CAN Signal
  1. Open or short in ABS/ESC CAN communication line
  2. Faulty Wheel Speed Sensor
Prerequisite Condition
  • ABS/ESC Option: TRUE
  • Ignition switch ON
  • Voltage of BCM/IBU is normal
  • No CAN Bus off
Threshold Value
  • When vehicle speed signal has not been received through the CAN signal from the ABS/ESC for 30 seconds
Diagnostic Time
  • 30 seconds
Lamp
  • ON
