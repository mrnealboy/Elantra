Vehicle S/W Management

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Ignition "ON".
  3. Select "Audio Video Navigation" system and perform "VEHICLE S/W MANAGEMENT " with GDS.
  4. Perform "AVN/Audio Diagnostic Mode" of "Vehicle S/W Management".
