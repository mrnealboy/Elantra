Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check that the gear is shifted to "N".
  2. Lightly tighten the bolts after installing the position switch (A).
    NOTE:
    • Lightly tighten the bolts so that adjustment.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install the position switch guide pin (A) SST No.: (09480-A3800).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Tighten the manual control lever nut (A) and the position switch mounting bolts (B) with specified toque.

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 17.7 - 24.5 N.m (1.8 - 2.5 kgf.m, 13.0 - 18.1 lb-ft)
    2. 9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. After pushing the "F" direction to remove the clearance, install the shift cable (B) and tighten the nut (A) to the specified torque.

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.0 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the position switch guide pin SST No.: (09480-A3800) (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Connect the position switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Install the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  9. Install the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")
