Repair Procedures: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check the connector contact condition, pollution, and wire harness wire open & short.
  2. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  3. Disconnect the IATS connector.
  4. Measure resistance between the IATS terminals 3 and 4.
  5. Check that the resistance is within the specification.

    Specification:  Refer to "SPECIFICATION "
