Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the Intake & Exhaust camshaft (A).

    [Intake camshaft] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Exhaust camshaft] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Install the camshaft front bearing cap (A) and camshaft bearing caps (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    M6 Bolts: 11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    M8 Bolts: 18.6 - 22.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Tighten the bolts, in several passes, in the sequence as shown.
    Fig 1: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts Tightening Sequence
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install the Intake CVVT (A) and Exhaust CVVT (B).

    [Intake CVVT] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Exhaust CVVT] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install the OCV & Center bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    88.3 - 98.0 N.m (9.0 - 10.0 kgf.m, 65.1 - 72.3 lb-ft)

    [Intake OCV & Center bolt] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Exhaust OCV & Center bolt] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Use care to keep foreign material out of the Exhaust OCV & Center bolt.
    • Be careful not to drop the CVVT.
    • When removing the Exhaust OCV & Center bolt, hold the camshaft with a wrench (A) to prevent the camshaft from rotating.

    [Intake camshaft hexagonal wrench] 
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Exhaust camshaft hexagonal wrench] 
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Install the Timing chain.

    (Refer to Timing System - "TIMING CHAIN ")
