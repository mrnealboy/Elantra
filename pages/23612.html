Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. Install the parking brake lever assembly (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    19.6 - 29.4 N.m (2.0 - 3.0 kgf.m, 14.5 - 21.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Install the parking brake cable (B) in the equalizer assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Apply a coating of the specified grease to each sliding parts (A) of the ratchet plate or the ratchet pawl.

    Specified grease:  CASMOLY 623

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install the parking brake cable adjuster, then adjust the parking brake lever stroke by turning adjusting nut (A).

    Parking brake lever stroke:  5 - 7 clicks (Pull the lever with 196N (20 kgf, 44 lbf))

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Connect the parking brake switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
