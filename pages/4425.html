General Type

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat-tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the bumper cover and panels.
  1. Remove the front bumper assembly.

    (Refer to "FRONT BUMPER ASSEMBLY ")

  2. Detach the wiring (A) mounted on the front bumper assembly and energy absorber.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the front bumper energy absorber (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the radiator grille (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector is connected properly.
