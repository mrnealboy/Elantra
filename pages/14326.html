Electronic Control Unit (ECU)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The engine Electronic Control Unit (ECU) controls the internal operation of the engine. The engine ECU controls ignition timing, variable valve timing, booster level of turbocharger and other devices related to the engine.
