Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing the fender, wrap protective tape around a screwdriver or remover not to damage components
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries
  1. Remove the front bumper. (Refer to Front Bumper - "FRONT BUMPER ASSEMBLY ")
  2. Remove the head lamps. (Refer to Body Electrical System - "HEAD LAMPS ")
  3. Remove the front wheel guard. (Refer to Body Side Molding - "FRONT WHEEL GUARD ")
  4. Remove the front bumper side mounting bracket (A) after loosening the bolts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Loosen the mounting clips and remove the insulator cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. After loosening the mounting bolts, remove the fender assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    Bolt A: 7.8 - 18.6 N.m (0.8 - 1.9 kgf.m, 5.8 - 13.7 lb-ft)

    Bolt B: 4.9 - 7.8 N.m (0.5 - 0.8 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
