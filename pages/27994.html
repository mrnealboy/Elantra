Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the driver door trim.

    (Refer to Body - "FRONT DOOR TRIM ")

  3. Remove the memory power switch (A) after disengaging the mounting clips.
