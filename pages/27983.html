Operation Linked with Getting On/getting Off

  1. Returning motion to backwards during getting off and to forwards during getting on (Corresponding to slide only)
  2. Starting of operation when the engine is off.
  3. Moving to the targeted position of ACC ON
  4. Behavior prohibited conditions
    • Vehicle speed 3 km/h
    • Power seats, if the operation of the manual control switch.
    • Driver Settings menu (USM) is Off,
    • Passenger action on receipt of the play command
