Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

Engine removal is not required for this procedure.

CAUTION:
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • To avoid damage, unplug the wiring connectors carefully while holding the connector portion.
  • To avoid damaging the cylinder head, wait until the engine coolant temperature drops below normal temperature before removing it.
  • When handling a metal gasket, take care not to fold the gasket or damage the contact surface of the gasket.
NOTE:
  • Mark all wiring and hoses to avoid misconnection.
  • Turn the crankshaft pulley so that the No. 1 piston is at top dead center (T.D.C).
  1. Disconnect the battery negative cable.
  2. Remove the engine cover.

    (Refer to Engine And Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE COVER ")

  3. Remove the duct and air cleaner assembly.

    (Refer to Intake And Exhaust System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  4. Remove the RH front wheel.

    (Refer to Suspension System - "WHEEL ")

  5. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine And Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  6. Loosen the drain plug, and drain the engine coolant. Remove the radiator cap to help drain the coolant faster.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  7. Remove the Integrated Thermal Management Module (ITM).

    (Refer to Cooling System - "INTEGRATED THERMAL MANAGEMENT MODULE (ITM) )

  8. Remove the alternator.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "ALTERNATOR ")

  9. Remove the exhaust manifold.

    (Refer to Intake And Exhaust System - "EXHAUST MANIFOLD ")

  10. Remove the heater pipe.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "INTEGRATED THERMAL MANAGEMENT MODULE (ITM) ")

  11. Remove the timing chain.

    (Refer to Timing System - "TIMING CHAIN ")

  12. Remove the camshaft front bearing cap (A) and camshaft bearing caps (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Unfasten the camshaft bearing caps in the sequence as shown.
    Fig 1: Camshaft Bearing Caps Bolts Loosening Sequence
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Remove the CVVT & Camshaft (A).

    [Intake CVVT & Camshaft assembly] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Exhaust CVVT & Camshaft assembly] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the cam carrier mounting bolts, and then remove the cam carrier (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Remove the Hydraulic Lash Adjuster (HLA) (A) and Swing arm (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Remove the ignition coil.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "IGNITION COIL ")

  17. Remove the cylinder head bolts, and the remove the cylinder head.
    1. Using bit socket (12PT) uniformly loosen and remove the cylinder head bolts at two or three times in the sequence as shown.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      Fig 2: Cylinder Head Bolts Loosening Sequence
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      CAUTION:
      • Head warpage or cracking could result from removing bolts in an incorrect order.
    2. Lift the cylinder head (A) from the dowels on the cylinder block and place the cylinder head on wooden blocks on a bench.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      CAUTION:
      • Be careful not to damage the contact surfaces of the cylinder head and cylinder block.
    3. Remove the cylinder head gasket (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
