Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat-tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the bumper cover and panels.
  1. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the service cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Detach the rear combination lamp connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the rear combination mounting nuts and remove the rear combination lamp (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting nuts and clips, remove the rear bumper under cover (A).

  5. Loosen the rear bumper assembly mounting bolts (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the rear bumper lower mounting clips.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the rear bumper upper mounting clips and bolts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the mounting screws on the side of rear bumper (A), detach the side part of rear bumper.
    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the tabs on the side part of front bumper (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Disconnect the rear bumper connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Remove the rear bumper assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Install in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:
    • Make sure the connector is connected properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
