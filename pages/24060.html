N BRAKE SYSTEM INFORMATION

N BRAKE SYSTEM INFORMATION

TEHCNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 21-BR-005H, Date of Issue:  November 23, 2021
HYUNDAI: Veloster N (JSN) Elantra N (CN7N) Kona N (OSN)
GROUP: BRAKES
Superceded Bulletin(s): 18-BR-002-1

REVISION HISTORY

This bulletin supersedes TSB 18-BR-002-1 to include additional models and update information about the brake system. 

DESCRIPTION

Hyundai N vehicles utilize low steel brake pads and DOT4 brake fluid. The brake system components were developed to maximize braking performance and fade resistance during severe driving conditions. As a result, these vehicles may exhibit different characteristics compared to conventional brakes.

Applicable Vehicles:  Veloster N (JSN), Elantra N (CN7N), Kona N (OSN)

These are all considered normal and do not require additional service.

  • Squeal noise during low speed braking.
  • Groan noise when slowly releasing the brake pedal after stopping.
  • The brake pads and rotor may experience greater wear.
  • Temporary shallow grooves or scoring (A) around the brake rotor surface. If the grooves or scoring results in a rotor below the minimum rotor thickness specification of 28.0 mm (1.10 in), replace the rotor.
  • The brake pads will create brake dust which will accumulate on the wheel surface. Regular wheel cleaning is recommended.

Additional information:

  • It is not recommended to mix DOT4 (SAE J1704 DOT4 LV, ISO4925 CLASS6, FMVSS 116 DOT4) with DOT3 brake fluid. If the fluids are mixed, the boiling point will be reduced to the lowest rated fluid. This may reduce braking performance for severe driving conditions.
  • Consult the owner's manual for the normal maintenance schedule for brake pads and fluid. However if the vehicle is driven in severe conditions, more frequent maintenance may be required.
  • Consult the latest Warranty Coverage Guide  for applicable warrantable information.
