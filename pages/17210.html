DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy
  • Long term fuel trim deviation reached min limit (too rich)
  1. Poor connector connection
  2. Air leakage
  3. Faulty fuel pressure
  4. Faulty injector
Enable Condition
  • Engine coolant temperature is more than 56°C (132.8°F)
  • Barometric pressure is more than 75 kPa
  • Engine speed is more than 512 RPM
  • Air-fuel ratio control activated
    • Front O2 sensor ready (Measured O2 sensor tip temperature is more than 720°C (1328°F)
    • Integrated air mass flow after start is more than 20 to 1400g
  • Stable driving condition:
    • Absolute value difference between actual engine speed and filtered engine speed is less than 300 RPM
    • Absolute value difference between actual air mass flow and filtered air mass flow is less than 70.01 mg/stroke
    • Absolute value difference between actual torque scale factor and filtered torque scale factor is less than 0.2
    • Absolute value difference between actual lambda and filtered lambda is less than 4.5%
    • Integrated air mass flow is more than 5 to 60g
Threshold Value
  • Additive long term trim value is less than -3.5 mg/stroke or multiplicative long term trim value is less than -25%
Diagnostic Time
  • Continuous
MIL ON Condition
  • 2 driving cycles
