Repair Procedures: Replacement

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Remove the front seat assembly. (Refer to Front Seat - "FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY ")
  2. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly. (Refer to Rear Seat - "REAR SEAT ASSEMBLY ")
  3. Remove the floor console assembly. (Refer to Floor Console - "FLOOR CONSOLE ASSEMBLY ")
  4. Remove the center pillar lower trim. (Refer to Interior Trim - "CENTER PILLAR TRIM ")
  5. Remove the cowl side trim. (Refer to Interior Trim - "COWL SIDE TRIM ")
  6. Remove the footrest (A).
  7. Remove the floor carpet assembly (B).
  8. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
