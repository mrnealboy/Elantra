Rear Power Window Motor

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the rear door trim.

    (Refer to Body - "REAR DOOR TRIM ")

  3. Disconnect the motor connector from the motor.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Connect the motor terminals No. 1 and No. 2 directly to battery voltage (12V) and check that the motor operates smoothly.

    If the operation is abnormal, replace the motor.

    [Safety Window Motor] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
