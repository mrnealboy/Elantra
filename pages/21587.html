Rear Disc Brake Parking Cable Type

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  2. Remove the rear wheel and tire (A) from the rear hub.

    Tightening torque: 

    107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the rear wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the rear caliper.

    (Refer to Brake System - "REAR DISC BRAKE ")

  4. Disconnect the rear wheel speed sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the rear wheel speed sensor bracket after loosening the mounting nut (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the parking brake cable bracket after loosening the mounting bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    19.6 - 29.4 N.m (2.0 - 3.0 kgf.m, 14.5 - 21.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the rear shock absober (A) from the torsion beam axle after loosening the mounting bolt.

    Tightening torque: 

    98.1 - 117.7 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 72.3 - 86.8 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Set up the jack under the torsion beam axle in order to remove it in no-load condition.

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the torsion beam axle (D) after loosening the mounting nuts (A) and bolts (B, C).

    Tightening torque 

    1. 98.1 - 117.7 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 72.3 - 86.8 lb-ft)
    2. 98.1 - 117.7 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 72.3 - 86.8 lb-ft)
    3. 44.1 - 53.9 N.m (4.5 - 5.5 kgf.m, 32.5 - 39.8 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
