Repair Procedures: Replacement

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • Replace the Intelligent Variable Transmission (IVT) assembly as a whole when replacement of inner parts of the IVT is required, Otherwise, dust or foreign substance may get into the IVT during disassembly, which may adversely affect its quality.
