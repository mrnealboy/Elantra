Driver Seat Mode Actuator

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The mode control actuator is mounted on the heater module and controlled by a driver when the driver enters signals through the control panel. It consists of a actuator motor that runs the mode door, and a potentiometer that detects the door position. If a driver manipulates the panel, it ventilates the wind in the order of VENT→ B/L → FLOOR.
