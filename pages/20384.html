Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Check that the shift lever is placed in the "N" position.
  2. Lightly tighten the inhibitor switch mounting bolts after installing the inhibitor switch (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Lightly tighten the manual control lever mounting nut (B) after installing the manual control lever (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Align the hole in the manual control lever with the "N" position hole of the inhibitor switch and then insert the inhibitor switch guide pin (SST No.: 09480 - A3800) (A) in the matched hole.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Tighten the nut (A), bolts (B) with the specified torque.

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 17.7 - 24.5 N.m (1.8 - 2.5 kgf.m, 13.0 - 18.1 lb-ft)
    2. 9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Lightly tighten the nut (A) after connected the shift cable in the manual control lever.
  7. Push shift cable lightly to "F" direction shown to eliminate free play of shift cable.
  8. Tighten the nut (A) with the specified torque.

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.0 - 1.4 kgf.m, 7.2 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Connect the inhibitor switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Remove the inhibitor switch guide pin (SST No.: 09480-A3800) from the hole.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Install the air cleaner and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

    CAUTION:
    • Check that operating surely at each range of the inhibitor switch corresponding to each position of Shift lever.
