BCM/IBU (TPMS Module integrated)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The TPMS is the acronym for Tire Pressure Monitoring System. This system monitors the pressure of the tire and when it drops below a certain pressure, the BCM/IBU (TPMS Module integrated) makes the warning lamp turn on for notifying the driver. This system consists of BCM/IBU (TPMS Module integrated) and TPMS sensors. Among components of TPMS system
