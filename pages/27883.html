Repair Procedures: Removal

CAUTION:
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
  • Use seat covers to avoid damaging any surfaces.
  1. Remove the roof side molding.

    (Refer to Body Side Moldings - "ROOF SIDE MOLDING ")

  2. Remove the rear package tray trim.

    (Refer to Interior Trim - "REAR PACKAGE TRAY TRIM ")

  3. Detach the rear window glass hot wire connector (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Spread WD-40 on the outer edges of rear window glass.
  5. Cut off the sealant on the edge (B) while pulling the piano wire (A) to the left and right.
    CAUTION:
    • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
    • Heat may occur when working on the wires. Be careful not to burn your hands.
    • Be careful not to disconnect the piano wire.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Put in a piano wire (A) through the corner of the windshield glass and insert wire grips into both ends of the wire.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Cut off the sealant on (A-A), (B-B), (C-C), while pulling a piano wire (A) to the left and right.
    CAUTION:
    • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
    • Heat may occur when working on the wires. Be careful not to burn your hands.
    • Be careful not to disconnect the piano wire.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Carefully remove the rear window glass (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
