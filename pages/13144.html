Removal and Installation: Notes

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Remove the engine cover.

    (Refer to Engine And Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE COVER ")

  3. Disconnect the oxygen sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the front muffler heat protector (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the front muffler.

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 58.8 N.m (4.0 - 6.0 kgf.m, 28.9 - 43.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When installing, replace with new gaskets.
  6. Install in the reverse order of removal.
