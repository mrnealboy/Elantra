Brake (Stop Lamp) Switch

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Brake (Stop Lamp) Switch is used to judge whether the acceleration system is abnormal or not. The brake (stop lamp) switch has a duplex system (signals brake test or brake light) to memorize the abnormality when the signals relating to hitting and releasing the brake pedal are detected simultaneously.
