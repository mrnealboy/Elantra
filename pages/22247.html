Repair Procedures: Inspection: [Motor]

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF.
  2. Disconnect the EEGR valve connector.
  3. Check that the EEGR valve is stuck by foreign material.
  4. Measure resistance between motor 4 and 5 control terminals of the motor.
  5. Check that the resistance is within the specification.
  • Specification
Item Specification
Coil Resistance (Ω) 2.21 - 2.99 [20°C (68°F)]
