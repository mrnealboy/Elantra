Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  2. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  3. Drain the coolant.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "COOLANT ")

  4. Disconnect the driven pulley speed sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Separate the shift cable.
    1. Loosen the shift cable mounting nut (A).

      Tightening torque: 

      9.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.0 - 1.4 kgf.m, 7.2 - 10.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Loosen the bolts (B) and then removing the shift cable (C).

      Tightening torque: 

      14.7 - 21.6 N.m (1.5 - 2.2 kgf.m, 10.9 - 15.9 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Disconnect the position switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Loosen the bolt and then separate the ground line (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Separate the IVT oil cooler hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Disconnect the main connector (A) and then separate the fasteners (B).
    NOTE:
    • After detaching the main connector (A), wrap a plastic bag around the connector to prevent coolant from getting into it.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • If coolant gets into the connector, corrosion may occur inside the connector, thus causing an open/short circuit. In this case, trouble codes and shift failure may also occur.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Loosen the starter mounting bolts (A) and IVT upper mounting bolts (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)
    2. 42.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.3 - 5.5 kgf.m, 31.1 - 39.8 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Assemble the engine support fixture (A) (beam No.: 09200-3N000, supporter No.: 09200-2S000, 09200-2S100, 09200-2S200, adapter No.: 09200-2W000).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Remove the dust cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Loosen the transmission bracket support mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    98.1 - 117.7 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 72.3 - 86.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the transmission mounting bracket (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque: 

    58.8 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.9 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Remove the under cover.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  16. Remove the sub frame.

    (Refer to Suspension System - "SUB FRAME ")

  17. Remove the drive shaft assembly.

    (Refer to Driveshaft and Axle - "FRONT DRIVESHAFT ")

  18. Loosen the bolts and then removing the driveshaft heat protector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Loosen the bolts and then removing the roll rod support bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    49.0 - 68.6 N.m (5.0 - 7.0 kgf.m, 36.2 - 50.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  20. Loosen the bolt and then separate the wiring bracket (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  21. Loosen the bolt and then removing the dust cover (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    42.2 - 48.1 N.m (4.3 - 4.9 kgf.m, 31.1 - 35.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • When mounting, install so that the fixing hook (A) is inserted inward.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  22. Loosen the torque converter mounting bolts (A) by rotating the crankshaft.

    Tightening torque: 

    45.1 - 52.0 N.m (4.6 - 5.3 kgf.m, 33.3 - 38.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  23. Support the transmission safely on a jack.
  24. Loosen the transmission lower mounting bolts (A, B).

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 42.2 - 48.1 N.m (4.3 - 4.9 kgf.m, 31.1 - 35.4 lb-ft)
    2. 42.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.3 - 5.5 kgf.m, 31.1 - 39.8 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  25. After separating the transmission from the engine, remove the transmission by lowering the jack slowly.
    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage other nearby systems or parts when removing the transmission assembly.
