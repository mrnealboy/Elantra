Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery (-) terminal.
  2. Release the residual pressure in fuel line.

    (Refer to Fuel Delivery System - "RELEASE RESIDUAL PRESSURE IN FUEL LINE ")

  3. Remove the rear seat.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "REAR SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  4. Remove the fuel pump service cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the fuel pump connector (A).
  6. Disconnect the fuel feed tube quick-connector (B).
  7. Disconnect the fuel tank pressure sensor connector (C).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the locking ring (A) by using the special service tool [No.: 09310-B8100].
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When removing the locking ring, be careful not to damage the fuel tank and fuel pump.
    • When removing the locking ring, press the upper area with hand and rotate the wrench counter clockwise.
  9. Remove the fuel pump from the fuel tank.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
