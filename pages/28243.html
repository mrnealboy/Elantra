Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the crash pad air vent [RH].

    (Refer to Body - "CRASH PAD AIR VENT ")

  3. Loosen the mounting screws and remove the hazard lamp switch (A).
