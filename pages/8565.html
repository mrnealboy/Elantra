DTC Description

P045600 is set when the engine Electronic Control Unit (ECU) detects a leak in the evaporation system. The ECM monitors the output value of a fuel tank pressure sensor under specific conditions and determines whether the fuel tank vacuum level exceeds the predetermined value or not.
