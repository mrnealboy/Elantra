DTC Description

C183186 is set when a regular navigation device is mistakenly installed or when a driver replaces the system with a device that does not feature the Highway Driver Assistance (HDA) function. In those cases, the HDA function will not be supported as well.

* The Highway Driver Assistance (HDA) prevents the driver from steering off the lane due to dozing behind the wheel or unconscious driving by detecting the driving lane with cameras and assisting steering on the highway. It also features a cutting-edge function that helps the vehicle maintain the distance from a vehicle ahead using the SCC radar system.
