Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
CAUTION:
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • To avoid damage, unplug the wiring connectors carefully while holding the connector portion.
NOTE:
  • Mark all wiring and hoses to avoid misconnection.
  • Turn the crankshaft pulley so that the No. 1 piston is at top dead center (T.D.C).
  1. Disconnect the battery negative cable.
  2. Remove the cylinder head cover.

    (Refer to Cylinder Head Assembly - "CYLINDER HEAD COVER ")

  3. Turn the crankshaft pulley so that the NO. 1 piston is at T.D.C.
  4. Set No. 1 cylinder to TDC (Top dead center) on compression stroke.
    1. Turn the crankshaft pulley and align its groove with the timing mark of the timing chain cover.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Check that the TDC marks of the intake and exhaust CVVT sprockets are in straight line on the cylinder head surface as shown in the illustration. If not, turn the crankshaft by one revolution (360°) more. (Do not turn the crankshaft pulley counter clockwise.)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Mark the timing chains corresponding to the timing marks of the CVVT sprockets.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the timing chain

    (Refer to Timing System - "TIMING CHAIN ")

  6. Remove the OCV & Center bolt (A).

    [Intake OCV & Center bolt] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Exhaust OCV & Center bolt] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Use care to keep foreign material out of the Exhaust OCV & Center bolt.
    • Be careful not to drop the CVVT.
    • When removing the Exhaust OCV & Center bolt, hold the camshaft with a wrench (A) to prevent the camshaft from rotating.

    [Intake camshaft hexagonal wrench]
