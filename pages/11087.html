Supplemental Restraints System Control Module (SRSCM)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

In collision, the Supplemental Restraints System Control Module (SRSCM) sends current to pertinent airbag for deployment.

Besides SRSCM performs diagnosis of overall airbag system and light warning lamp if there's any fault in it.

Main functions of the SRSCM are as follows.

  1. Collision and Passenger presence Detection.
  2. Determination of the BPT and the airbag deployment in accordane with given condition.
  3. Internal and external diagnosis of the airbag system.
  4. Warning by warning lamp if there's any fault in the airbag system.
  5. Proffering data for the scantool diagnosis.
  6. In a case of a vehicle's rollover, It senses the ratio of a vehicle's rotation.
