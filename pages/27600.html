Pressure Sensor

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The pressure sensor, installed in the ESP, converts the brake pressure to judge the driver's brake intention when the ESP (ESC)/DBC/AVH program is operated. When pressure is applied to the sensor, the resistance of the sensor's strain gauge changes, which affects the voltage output from the sensor.
