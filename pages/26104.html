Exhaust System Inspection

  1. Visually and physically check the items as follows:
    • Air leak, clog, or damage of the exhaust system between the oxygen sensor and the catalytic converter
    • Damage, loose connection, or poor installation
  2. Did you find the cause?

    YES 

    • Repair or replace relevant component(s) as necessary and see if a DTC is stored.

    NO 

    • Go to next procedure.
