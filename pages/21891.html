Repair Procedures: Reassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • Thoroughly clean all parts to be assembled.
  • Before installing the parts, apply fresh engine oil to all sliding and rotating surfaces.
  1. Install the crankshaft main bearings.

    Information 

    • Upper bearings have an oil groove of oil holes, lower bearings do not.
    1. Align the bearing claw with the groove of the cylinder block, and push in the 5 upper bearings (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Align the bearing claw with the groove of the bedplate (B), and push in the 5 lower bearings (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Install the thrust bearings.

    Install the 2 thrust bearings (A) on both sides of the No. 3 journal of the cylinder block with the oil groove facing out.

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Place the crankshaft (A) on the cylinder block.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Apply liquid sealant on the top surface of the bedplate.
    1. Using a gasket scraper, remove all the old packing material from the gasket surfaces.

      Information 

      • Spraying cleaner on the surface and wiping with a clean duster.
    2. Assemble a new O-ring (A) on the top of bedplate.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Apply liquid sealant on the bottom of the cylinder block. Continuous bead of sealant should be applied to prevent any path from oil leakage.

      Bead width:  2.5 - 3.5 mm (0.10 - 0.14 in.)

      Sealant:  LOCTITE 5900 or equivalent

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Assemble the bedplate within 5 minutes after applying sealant.
    • The engine running or pressure test should not be performed within 30 minutes after assembling the bedplate.
    • Excess sealant on application surface of sealant of following process should be removed before hardening.
    • If the sealant is applied to the top surface of the bedplate, it should be the same position as the cylinder block.
    • To prevent leakage of oil, apply sealant gasket on the inner threads of the bolt holes.
  5. Install the bedplate (C) and main bearing cap bolts (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    1st step (All):

    27.5 - 31.4 N.m (2.8 - 3.2 kgf.m, 20.3 - 23.1 lb-ft)

    2nd step (All): 120 - 125°

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Install the bedplate bolt, in the sequence as shown.
      Fig 1: Main Bearing Caps Bolts Tightening Sequence
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Install the bedplate mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    18.6 - 23.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.4 kgf.m, 13.7 - 17.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Check that the crankshaft turns smoothly.

  7. Check the crankshaft end play.
  8. Assemble the other parts in the reverse order of disassembly.

    Information 

    In case the crankshaft is replaced with a new one, select the proper connecting rod bearing according to the pin journal mark on the crankshaft.

    • Connecting rod bearing selection

      (Refer to Cylinder Block - "PISTON AND CONNECTING ROD ")
