Occupant Classification System (OCS)

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
    WARNING:
    • Disconnect the battery negative cable, and wait for at least three minutes before beginning work.
  2. Remove the passenger's side front seat assembly. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY ")
  3. Remove the passenger seat cushion assembly. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "FRONT SEAT CUSHION COVER ")
  4. Disconnect occupant classification sensor unit connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the occupant classification sensor unit (A) after removing the clip.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
