WARRANTY POLICY TO HOLD 12-VOLT BATTERY FOR WTC PART RETURN

WARRANTY POLICY TO HOLD 12-VOLT BATTERY FOR WTC PART RETURN

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 24-EE-009H, Date of Issue:  July 01, 2024
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: ENGINE ELECTRICAL
Superceded Bulletin(s): 19-EE-005H, Date of Issue:  July 01, 2024

REVISION HISTORY

This TSB supersedes 19-EE-005H to update shipping procedures for AGM batteries. 

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin is to inform that all 12-Volt batteries replaced under warranty must be tagged and held for 10 days after each battery claim payment approval  . Batteries must be held separate from general cores intended for interstate pickup until they are released from the 10-day holding requirement.

Certain batteries may be subject to Warranty Technical Center (WTC) Part Request. In the event the requested battery is not sent or the wrong battery is sent, the claim could be subject to a warranty chargeback.

Information 

Dealers must ship all WTC requested battery in the following manner: 

  • FLOODED BATTERIES: 
    • Do NOT  ship until packaging materials are received from the WTC
    • Packaging materials contain: liner, absorbent material, wire tie, and Non-Spillable labels
  • AGM BATTERIES: 
    • Can be shipped utilizing normal processes
    • Apply a label to the shipping container identifying as Non-Spillable 
    • Refer to page 4  for battery label examples and proper identification

Applicable Vehicles:  All

WARRANTY INFORMATION

NOTE:
  1. Hyundai will credit the dealership $35 per battery to reimburse for Interstate core charge and Dealer handling.
  2. Discard tags and release batteries to the Interstate cores pickup area, only after the 10 day retention period has passed and battery has not been requested.
  3. The Dealer Parts Scrap Report can be referenced to identify batteries that can be released after the 10-day retention period.

SERVICE PROCEDURE

DEALER-WARRANTY BATTERY HOLD

  1. Dealer must tie a warranty tag (A) to every battery replacement under warranty:
    • Preferred method:  Tie the tag to the handle.
    • Secondary method:  Tie the tag to the terminal or tape the tag to the battery.

    Write the date, RO number, and incident vehicle information onto the tag.
  2. Store the tagged warranty battery separately from the used battery cores that are held for Interstate pickup.
  3. Ensure the Interstate representative does NOT  pick up tagged warranty batteries.

HMNA-BATTERY RETURN REQUEST

Information 

The P/N within the WTC Part Return Form is the claimed Interstate replacement P/N. This P/N does not appear on the OEM battery.

  1. Identify the applicable battery based on the RO and VIN located on the part tag.
  2. Notify WTC if the requested battery is damaged.

    Information 

    The address listed in the Part Return may not be the WTC address.

DEALER-PACKING & SHIPPING

  1. Locate the requested battery held separate from the Interstate core pickup area.
  2. Contact WTC at WTC@HMAUSA.com  or call 877-446-2982 with:
    • Battery type
    • DUR code
    • RO #
    • Claim #
  3. Receive packaging material (A) from HMNA which includes:
    • Clear bag
    • Absorbent material
    • Wire tie

    Information 

    If packaging materials are not received within 1 week then contact WTC.

  4. Flooded Battery Packaging Procedure: 
    1. Place the battery in the liner bag with absorbent material then place items in a cardboard box (B).
    2. Ensure terminals are up.
    3. Place the WTC Part Return Notice, first page of RO showing RO#, and battery test result on top of the battery.
    4. Close the liner with the supplied wire tie (C).
    5. Seal the box with provided tape.
  5. AGM Battery Packaging Procedure: 
    1. Use the QR Code to print all labels and affix (1) "Non-Spillable" label onto the AGM Battery.
    2. Place the battery in a suitable cardboard box.
    3. Ensure terminals are up.
    4. Place the WTC Part Return Notice, front page of RO #, and battery tester result ticket on top of the battery.
    5. Seal the box with the provided tape.
    6. Attach the remaining (2) Non-Spillable labels on each side of the package.
    7. Attach System Generated Part Return labels to the box.
