Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the shift cable retainer (A) and then tightening the nuts.

    Tightening torque: 

    11.8 - 14.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.5 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Install the cable after placing the shift lever and the manual control lever in the N position.
  2. Install the shift cable socket (B) and snap pin (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Lug (A) must assemble upward.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • When assembling the snap pin, be careful about the correct direction.
      • The band portion (A) must point to the front of the vehicle.
      • The band portion (A) must point outwards.
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ")

  4. Push the shift cable (B) slightly in the direction of the arrow to remove the clearance, and then tighten the nut (A) to the specified torque.

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.0 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Replace the cable clip, use a new one.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Install the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  6. Install the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

    CAUTION:
    • Check that operating surely at each range of the inhibitor switch corresponding to each position of shift lever.
