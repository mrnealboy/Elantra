DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor RF signals
  1. Damaged tire
  2. Surrounding high temperature
  3. Driving with low pressure tire
  4. Excessive braking at driving
Prerequisite Condition
  • Ignition switch ON
  • Sensor is normal
  • Voltage of BCM/IBU is normal
Threshold Value
  • When BCM/IBU can receive more than 1 signals about temperature data of over 115°C (239°F) from learned sensor consecutively
Diagnostic Time
  • Occurs when 180 seconds are maintained after 1 message reception
Lamp
  • OFF
