Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) cable.
  2. Turn the steering wheel so that the front wheels can face straight ahead.
  3. Remove the drive airbag module. (Refer to Restraint - "DRIVER AIRBAG (DAB) MODULE & CLOCK SPRING ")
  4. Disconnect the steering wheel connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the steering wheel after loosening the steering wheel lock bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    44.1 - 53.9 N.m (4.5 - 5.5 kgf.m, 32.5 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Do not use the hammer for disconnecting on the steering column not to damage the steering column.
    • Do not reuse the steering lock bolt.
    • When assembling, match the gear teeth .
