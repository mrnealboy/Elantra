Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
  1. Remove the inside rear view mirror cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the rain sensor cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the inside rear view mirror connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting screw and push the inside rearview mirror base upward to remove the inside rearview mirror assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure the connector is connected properly.
    • Be careful not to damage the windshield glass.
