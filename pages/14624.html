Monitor GDS data

  1. Connect the GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Select "Vehicle S/W Management" function.
  3. Select "Sensor Status" mode.
  4. Place the GDS TPMS as near as possible to the tire.

    (Reading may take 30 to 60 seconds depending on the sensor's characteristics.)

  5. Monitor the current data of the temperature.
