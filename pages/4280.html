DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the data coming from a transponder
  1. Faulty transponder
Threshold Value
  • Launch the antenna -> IMMO-> sends it to the transponder the IMMO computes the value. Then, the IMMO confirms whether or not the serial number it received correspond to the one registered in its memory. If the numbers are different, the IMMO sets DTC.
Diagnostic Time
  • Immediately
DTC Clearing
  • Clear the DTC using GDS
