PCB Block

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Disconnect the PCB block connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Push the four hooks (B) in the direction of the arrow and lift up the PCB block (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the PCB block by disconnect the connector.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
