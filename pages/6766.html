Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal
  2. Disconnect the knock sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the installation bolt (A), and then remove the sensor from the cylinder block.

    Tightening Torque: 

    18.6 - 24.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.5 kgf.m, 13.7 - 18.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
