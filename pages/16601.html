Projected Dimensions

  1. The dimensions are measured by projecting the measurement points on the vehicle surface onto the reference dimensions.
  2. If the length of the tracking gauge proves is adjustable, measure the dimensions by lengthening one of the two probes to the height difference between the two measuring surfaces.
