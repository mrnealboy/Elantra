Repair Procedures: Installation

CAUTION:
  • Check the operation of the rain sensor and the light sensor before mounting.
    • Check the sensor for silicon damage.
    • Check whether the rain sensor connector is tightened.
    • Check whether the rain sensor and bracket are properly tightened.
    • Check whether the sensor mounted area is free from foreign matters.

      Recalibrate sensitivity of the switch if the auto-wiping operation is insensitivity or too much sensitive.

  • In case the silicone of the rain sensor is damaged it should be replaced it as shown in the below image.
    1. Remove the damaged silicone gel pad from the lens plate completely.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to damage or contaminate the surface of the lens plate during removal.
    2. Attach the new silicone gel pad after removing the silicone paper (A) then remove the transparent film (B).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:

  • It is very important that the silicon (coupling pad) is completely pushed against the windshield and stuck without any air bubble.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  • In case of replacing the damaged windshield, you may reuse the undamaged existing rain sensor.
  • The windshield glass has to be clean and free of contamination or foreign substances.
  1. Connect the rain sensor connector.
  2. Push one of the latch of Spring arm (A) down until it snaps and you hear a click sound. And then push the latch of second spring arm (B) for keeping the sensor in right position.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Do not snap both spring arms at the same time.
    • An incorrectly mounted snap connection may cause malfunctions of the rain/light sensor.
