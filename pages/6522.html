Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. Install the intake CVVT (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Install the CVVD assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • After installing the CVVD, check whether the swing arm is separated or is installed correctly.
    • When installing the intake camshaft, spray the engine oil to the camshaft journal installing part (A) of the cam carrier.

      The amount of application should be enough to flow down both the front and back sides when it is applied to the center.

    • For installation, the timing mark (A) of the intake CVVT and the timing chain mark (B) should be matched.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • During the installation, there should be no artificial rotation or movement of the guide bracket and control shaft.
    • When removing the CVVD fixing jig, be sure to securely tighten the M6 bolts.
    • If CVVD fixing jig is available, be sure to work by using it.

    When Using the CVVD fixture 

    1. When installing the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M6), tighten sequence shown.

      Tightening torque: 

      1st

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      2nd

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Fig 1: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Remove the CVVD fixture (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Provisionally tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M8) in sequence shown below.

      Tightening torque: 

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Fig 2: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Remove the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M6) (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    5. Provisionally tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M6) in sequence shown below.

      Tightening torque: 

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Fig 3: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    6. Tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M8) in sequence shown below.

      Tightening torque: 

      18.6 - 22.6 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

      Fig 4: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    7. Tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M6) in sequence shown below.

      Tightening torque: 

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Fig 5: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    8. Measure the clearance between the control shaft bearing cap and the end of control shaft using a clearance gauge.

      Clearance:  0.0 - 0.05 mm (0.0 - 0.002 in.)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    When not using the CVVD fixture 

    1. Provisionally tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M6) in sequence shown below.

      Tightening torque: 

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Fig 6: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Provisionally tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M8) in sequence shown below.

      Tightening torque: 

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Fig 7: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M8) in sequence shown below.

      Tightening torque: 

      18.6 - 22.6 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

      Fig 8: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Tighten the CVVD assembly mounting bolts (M6) in sequence shown below.

      Tightening torque: 

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Fig 9: CVVD Assembly Mounting Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    5. Release the torque of control shaft bearing cap bolt (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    6. Push the control shaft bearing cap as close as possible in the direction of the arrow.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    7. Install the control shaft bearing cap mounting bolts (A).

      Tightening torque: 

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    8. Measure the clearance between the control shaft bearing cap and the end of control shaft using a clearance gauge.

      Clearance:  0.0 - 0.05 mm (0.0 - 0.002 in.)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install the intake OCV & center bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    22.6 - 26.5 N.m (2.3 - 2.7 kgf.m, 16.6 - 19.5 lb-ft) + 32 - 36°

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • To prevent impurities from entering intake oil control valve (OCV) & center bolt, wear rubber gloves.
    • intake oil control valve (OCV) and center bolt, hold the hexagonal portion on the camshaft with a wrench to prevent the camshaft from rotating.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Compress the timing chain tensioner piston.
    1. With a pry bar (A), compress the timing chain tensioner piston (B).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. With a thin gimlet (A), raise the ratchet plate (B) of the timing chain tensioner and fix the piston.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

      Information 

      • Do not remove the pick (A).
  5. Install the exhaust CVVT (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When installing the timing chain, be sure that the timing mark (A) of each sprocket is matched with the timing mark (B) (color link) of the timing chain.

    [Exhaust CVVT Timing Mark] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Install the exhaust camshaft (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • After installing exhaust camshaft, check whether the swing arm is separated or is installed correctly.
    • When installing the exhaust camshaft, spray the engine oil to the camshaft journal installing part of the cam carrier.

      The amount of application should be enough to flow down both the front and back sides when it is applied to the center.

  7. Install the exhaust oil control valve (OCV) and center bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    22.6 - 26.5 N.m (2.3 - 2.7 kgf.m, 16.6 - 19.5 lb-ft) + 32 - 36°

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • To prevent impurities from entering exhaust oil control valve (OCV) & center bolt, wear rubber gloves.
    • When installing the exhaust oil control valve (OCV) and center bolt, hold the hexagonal portion on the camshaft with a wrench to prevent the camshaft from rotating.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Install the camshaft front bearing cap (A) and camshaft bearing caps (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • If there is enough engine oil on the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt and bolt hole, tighten to the minimum required tightening torque.
    • Apply engine oil the upper part of the intake/exhaust camshaft journal, then install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap. The amount of application should be enough to flow down both the front and back sides when it is applied to the center.
    1. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Fig 10: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

      Fig 11: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    5. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      18.6 - 22.6 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

      Fig 12: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    6. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    7. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

      Tightening torque: 

      11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the fine gimlets (A) that hold the timing chain tensioner.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. When installing the timing chain, be sure that the timing mark (A) of each sprocket is matched with the timing mark (B) (color link) of the timing chain.

    [Intake CVVT Timing Mark] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Exhaust CVVT Timing Mark] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Install the fuel pump bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    18.6 - 23.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.4 kgf.m, 13.7 - 17.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Install the cam to cam guide (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Install the intake and exhaust variable force solenoid (VFS) valves (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Install the cylinder head cover.

    (Refer to Cylinder Head Assembly- "CYLINDER HEAD COVER ")
