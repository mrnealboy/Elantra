Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the dual clutch transmission from the vehicle. (Refer to DCT System - "REPAIR PROCEDURES ")
  2. Remove the dual clutch assembly. (Refer to "DUAL CLUTCH ASSEMBLY ")
  3. Remove the clutch engagement bearing 2 (A) and 1 (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the clutch engagement fork (A) and engagement bearing sleeve (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
