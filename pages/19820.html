Actuation Test

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn the ignition switch ON and warm up the vehicle up to an operational temperature.
  3. Select "Driver Air Mix Potentiometer" parameter on the "Current Data" of the GDS.
  4. Select "Actuation Test" for "Driver Seat Air Mix Door - 0%/50%/100%".
  5. During an actuation test, check whether the value of the Driver Air Mix Potentiometer changes.

    Specification  : Max. cooling - Approx. below 10%, Intermediate - 50%, Max. heating - Approx. 90%
