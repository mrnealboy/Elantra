Vehicle Identification Number

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  1. World Manufacturer Identifier (WMI)
    • KMH: Passenger vehicle or MPV (Multipurpose Passenger Vehicle)/SUV (Sports Utility Vehicle)/RV (Recreational Vehicle)
  2. Vehicle line
    • L: CN7
  3. Model & Series
    • L: Low grade (L)
    • M: Middle-Low grade (GL)
    • N: Middle grade (GLS, JSL, TAX)
    • P: Middle-High grade (HGS)
    • R: High grade (TOP)
    • S: Premium GL grade (PGL)
    • T: Super HGSL grade (SGH)
  4. Body/Cabin type, Gross Vehicle Weight Rating
    • 4: 4DR
  5. Restraint system, Brake system
    • B: Seat Belt, FRT A/bag (Driver, Passenger)
    • E: Seat Belt, FRT A/bag (Driver)
    • F: Seat Belt FRT A/bag (1st row)
    • N: Seat Belt
    • A: Seat Belt, FRT A/bag (Driver, Passenger), Side A/bag (1st row), Curtain A/bag (1st row, 2nd row)
  6. Engine type
    • F: Gasoline engine (GAMMA II 1.6 T-GDI)
    • G: Gasoline engine (NU 2.0 MPI)
  7. Check digit or Driver's side & Transmission
    • A: LHD & MT
    • B: LHD & AT
    • E: LHD & CVT
    • G: LHD & DCT
    • L: RHD & MT
    • M: RHD & AT
    • T: RHD & CVT
    • V: RHD & DCT
  8. Model year
    • M: 2021, N: 2022, P: 2023, R: 2024....
  9. Plant of production
    • A: Asan (Korea)
    • B: Beijing (China)
    • C: Cheonju (Korea)
    • H: Alabama (U.S.A)
    • M: Chennai (India)
    • U: Ulsan (Korea)
    • Z: Izmit (Turkey)
    • J: Ostrava (Czech)
    • P: Piracicaba (Brazil)
  10. Vehicle production sequence number
    • 000001 ~ 999999
