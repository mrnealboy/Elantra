DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble shooting Condition Possible cause
Main action
  • Monitor the CAN message
  1. Wrong tire pressure
  2. Different size of mounted wheel or tire
  3. Steering misaligned
  4. Defective Yaw rate sensor
  5. Faulty wheel speed sensors
  6. Faulty ESP (ESC)
Threshold value
  • When the ESP (ESC) continuously controls the vehicle front wheel for more than 10 seconds
Diagnosis time
  • 10 seconds
MIL
  • ESP (ESC) warning lamp is activated
Reset condition
  • After the ignition switch is turned OFF and ON, when every wheel speed signal is normal after driving 12 km/h (7.4 MPH) or more.
