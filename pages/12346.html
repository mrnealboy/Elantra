Repair Procedures: Installation

CAUTION:
  • Place the shift lever and transaxle side lever in "4th" shift position and insert the lever fixing pin (A).
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  1. Install the retainer (A) and then tighten the nuts.

    Tightening torque: 

    11.8 - 14.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.5 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Install the shift cable end (A) on the shift lever (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install the shift lever (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install the select cable (B) and the snap pin (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Install the shift cable socket (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Move the locker (B) in the direction of "F" with pulling the holder (A) in the direction of "P".
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Install the shift cable (B) and the select cable (C) and then fixing the snap (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Tighten the holder (A) to the "P" directions after fixing the rockers (B) in the "F" direction.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Install the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  10. Install the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  11. Remove the 4th fixing pin (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Install the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ")

  13. Install the shift knob & boots (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
CAUTION:
  • Check operating of lever of transaxle side when operating shift lever after assembly. (1~6, R)
  • If there is sticky feel, adjust the control cable length again.

    (Refer to Manual Transaxle Control System - "CONTROL CABLE ")
