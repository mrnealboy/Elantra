DTC Data Analysis

  1. Plug GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Start the engine and turn on the headlights and heating wire.
  3. Monitor the "BATTERY VOLTAGE" parameter in the "Current Data" menu of GDS.
  4. Maintain the engine RPM 2500 RPM for over 2 minutes at idle. And check the "Current Data".

    Specification:  Approx. 13 ~ 15V
