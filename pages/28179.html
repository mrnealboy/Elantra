Crash Pad Mood Lamp (LH)

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the crash pad lower panel.

    (Refer to Body - "CRASH PAD LOWER PANEL ")

  3. Loosen the mounting screws and remove the crash pad lower switch (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the crash pad lower switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Loosen the mounting screw and remove the crash pad air vent [LH]
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Disconnect the mood lamp connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Loosen the mounting screw and connector and remove the crash pad mood lamp [LH] (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
