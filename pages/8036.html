Ambient Air Temperature Sensor (AATS)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Ambient Air Temperature Sensor (AATS) measures the temperature of the air coming into the engine with Negative Temperature Coefficient Thermistor. The engine Electronic Control Unit (ECU) adjusts the fuel amount in accordance with the temperature information it received from the AATS and Intake Air Temperature Sensor (IATS).
