Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • Before mounting, remove the sealant on the oil pan mounting surface on the transmission side.
  1. Apply sealant to the oil pan mounting surface (A) on the transmission side.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Install the oil pan (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Loosen the injection plug (A) and supply IVTF.

    Recommended rating:  SP-IVT1

    Recommended oil:  MICHANG SP-IVT1

    Oil quantity:  6.5 L (1.71 U.S.gal., 6.86 U.S.qt., 5.71 lmp.qt.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install the IVTF injection plug.

    Tightening torque: 

    34.3 - 44.1 N.m (3.5 - 4.5 kgf.m, 25.3 - 32.5 lb-ft)

  5. Perform the IVTF level check procedure.

    (Refer to Hydraulic System - "FLUID ")
