DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the CAN messages
  1. Open/short in CAN communication circuit
  2. Communication error between CGW and SJB
  3. Faulty CGW system
Prerequisite Condition
  • Engine is on (Confirm that engine operation signal is sent from EMS for over 2 seconds)
  • Battery Voltage is between 9V and 16V
  • Ignition has been on for over 2 seconds (Time Delay)
  • CAN communication lines are normal
Threshold Value
  • The SJB messages from the CGW are invalid
Diagnostic Time
  • 0.5 seconds
MIL
  • SCC operation cancelled/inhibited
  • SCC warning lamp ON
