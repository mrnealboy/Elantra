DTC Description

The PODS (Passive Occupant Detection System) ECU sets B149654 when the PODS initialization was not carried out at a seat factory. It determines a fault and turns on a telltale lamp.

GDS-Vehicle S/W management cannot solve B149654 because it can calibrate seats only that has been initialized at a seat factory, newly replaced at a field, and completed a PODS inspection.
