Oil cooler pipe

  1. Remove the heater pipe
  2. Disconnect the cooler hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the cooler hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the oil cooler pipe (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Install in the reverse order of removal.
  6. Fill with engine coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE:
    • The coolant must be injected according to the integrated thermal management module (ITM) coolant filling method.
  7. Start engine and check for leaks.
