Normal mode

  1. PDW-F: Lin communication starts and keeps the routine after IGN1 ON+D gear + below 10 km/h.

    PDW-R: Lin communication starts and keeps the routine after IGN1 ON+R gear

  2. After initializing, the routine starts at once without PDW starting warning sound.
  3. Alarms of obstacle consists of 3 level 1, 2, 3 step and 1, 2 alarm sounds intermittently and 3 alarm sounds continuously. 1 level alarm doesn't exist in the front ultrasonic sensor.
  4. In display, the data of each sensor is sent from IBU to display, for example cluster. CAN communication is used for transmission and maximum gateway time is 50 ms.
  5. The efficient vehicle speed of PDW operation is under 10 Km/h.
  6. Operation doesn't start or stops at gear N, P.
