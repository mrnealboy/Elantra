DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor data status
  1. Software errors (Reset the vehicle's battery for repair)
  2. Short to ground due to deteriorating environmental conditions such as corrosion/dust/moisture/pollution and etc. (Clean PCB top plate with an air gun after removing IAU*)
  3. Open to chip circuit or PCB circuit in the controller (In this case, need to place the new IAU*)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn the ignition switch ON.
Threshold Value
  • I2C* communication errors more than three times
Diagnosis Time
  • Immediately
DTC Clearing
  • "Immediately after confirming "normal operation"
NOTE: IAU*: "Identity Authentication Unit"
12C*: communication protocols among chips
