DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble shooting Condition Possible cause
Main action
  • Monitor the SRSCM signals
  1. Faulty IMU (SRSCM)
  2. Incorrect installation condition
Threshold value
  • In case that the invalid IMU CAN message (Internal failure of Yaw rate sensor, Lateral G sensor, Longitudinal G sensor and MCU) is received from SRSCM.
  • While driving or controlling: 100 milliseconds
  • While stopping or not controlling: 500 milliseconds

    * SRSCM: Supplementary Restraint System Control Module
MIL
  • ESP (ESC) warning lamp is activated
Reset condition
  • When the normal state is confirmed after ignition switch turned OFF and back ON.
