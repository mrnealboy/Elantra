12V BATTERY MAINTENANCE FOR INVENTORY & PRE-DELIVERY INSPECTIONS (PDI)

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 24-EE-014H, Date of Issue:  October 01, 2024
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: ENGINE ELECTRICAL
Superceded Bulletin(s): 20-EE-008H, Date of Issue:  October 01, 2024

REVISION HISTORY

This TSB supersedes TSB # 20-EE-008H to provide updated information and battery warranty claim procedures. 

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin provides information on 12V battery maintenance for inventory & Pre-Delivery Inspections (PDI).

  • Dealers shall inspect 12V batteries as part of the PDI within 7 days of inventory delivery.
  • A Battery Test/Claim shall be performed when the PDI Tablet Application results in No Good (NG  ) result during the AUTO CHECK function (refer to TSB # 24-EE-003H  , "Guide to Battery Diagnosis TSBs (Battery App Phase 2)  ").
  • It is the dealer's responsibility to ensure proper 12V battery condition (maintain SOC above 80%) on all dealer inventory, including offsite storage lots.
  • 12V Battery aged maintenance should be performed regularly in accordance with the Dealer Vehicle Stock Battery Maintenance Schedule  on page 3  .
  • Perform 12V battery maintenance per WebDCS Aged Inventory - Bluelink Alerts  notifications (refer to TSB # 24-EE-013H  ).

Follow the Dealer Vehicle Stock Preparation  tips on pages 5 - 6  to ensure all vehicles in inventory are prepared to ensure minimal parasitic current draw on the 12V battery during storage.

Applicable Vehicles: 

  • All Dealer Stock vehicles except hybrid electric models equipped with Lithium 12V batteries.

VEHICLE BATTERY INSPECTION AT DEALER

Use the Battery Test/Claim feature on GDS-Smart to check battery condition within 7 days from vehicle arrival. Please refer to TSB # 24-EE-002H  , "GDS Battery Diagnosis Function  ", for Diagnostic Code descriptions using GDS Battery Diagnosis.
NOTE: A battery in a prolonged discharged state may adversely be damaged from internal plate sulfation. After jump-starting the vehicle, take it to the Service Department and have the battery tested following TSB # 24-EE-003H  , "Guide to Battery Diagnosis TSBs  ".

DEALER VEHICLE STOCK PREPARATION

  1. Ensure the IGN switch is in the "OFF"  position and key is removed from key cylinder.

    Ensure the Start Stop Button is turned "OFF"  .
  2. Ensure that the glove box is firmly closed.
  3. Ensure all doors are closed (including hood, trunk, tail gate, and fuel filler door).
  4. Ensure that all interior and exterior lights are turned "OFF"  .
  5. New production vehicles are equipped with Dark Current Control System. 

    It is highly recommended to set the Dark Current Control to "Dealer/Shipping Mode"  using the instructions in TSB # 24-EE-012H  .

    When vehicles are in dealer stock, setting the Dark Current Control to "Dealer/Shipping Mode"  minimizes vehicle current draw and reduces battery discharge.

    Information 

    Dark Current Control set to "Dealer/Shipping Mode" will NOT affect vehicle functionality or performance.
