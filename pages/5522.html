DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible cause
Main action
  • Monitor the reverse switch circuit
  1. Short or open in Reverse Gear switch circuit [A/T]
  2. Short or open in Back up light switch circuit [M/T]
Threshold Value Case 1
  • When Reverse switch signal is input for more than 1 minute while driving more than 60 km/h (37.2 MPH)
Case 2
  • During backward driving, reverse gear switch state is inactive for more than 1 minute
  • When the reverse gear switch state change more than 80 times for 5 seconds
MIL
  • DBC/AVH warning lamps are activated
Reset condition Case 1
  • Normal after ignition switch turned off and back on again and the state change of Reverse switch is not detected more than 3 seconds
