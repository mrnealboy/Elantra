Parking Collision-Avoidance Assist (PCA)

PCA is a parking safety system that assists in collision warning and emergency braking in the event of a collision with rear pedestrians and obstacles during reversing at low speed (collision warning is displayed on the instrument cluster and monitor and audible warning sounds)
