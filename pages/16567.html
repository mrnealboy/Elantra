Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery (-) terminal.
  2. Release the residual pressure in fuel line.

    (Refer to Fuel Delivery System - "RELEASE RESIDUAL PRESSURE IN FUEL LINE ")

  3. Disconnect the injector connectors (A) and then remove the wiring protector bolts (B).

    Tightening Torque: 

    7.8 - 9.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.0 kgf.m, 5.8 - 7.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the fuel feed tube quick-connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Open the clamp cover (A) before disconnecting the quick connector (if the clip is applied).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • When removing the quick-connector by using a clip removing tool (A), be careful not to damage the plastic clip (B).
    • If the clip is damaged, fuel line leakage may occur due to poor connection.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the delivery pipe & injector assembly (A).

    Tightening Torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.9 - 2.4 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Release the fixing clip (A), and then separate the injector from the delivery pipe.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
