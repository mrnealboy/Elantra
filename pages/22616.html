Maintenance System Reminder Reset - Procedure 01

The Maintenance system informs the driver when to replace engine oil and rotate tires.

  1. When the vehicle is at a standstill, pressing the DISP button for more than 2 seconds with the ignition switch in the ON position or engine running, the LCD display on the cluster will change to the "Maintenance" group. You can move to an item by pressing the arrow-up or arrow-down button and select the item by pressing the DISP button. See Fig 1.
  2. Select the desired maintenance schedule.
  3. When finished, you can exit out of the "Maintenance" group by pressing the DISP button for more than 2 seconds.
