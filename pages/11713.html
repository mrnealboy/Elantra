Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly.

    (Refer to Rear Seat - "REAR SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  2. Remove the rear seat belt buckle.

    [LH, CTR] 

    1. Press the lock pin, separate the rear seat belt connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Loosen the mounting bolt, remove the rear seat belt buckle (A).

      Tightening torque: 

      39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.9 - 39.8 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    1. Press the lock pin, separate the rear seat belt connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Loosen the mounting bolt, remove the rear seat belt buckle (A).

      Tightening torque: 

      39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.9 - 39.8 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • The buckle and tongue of different types are impossible to be latched, so check that the buckle & tongue have the same types.
    • Distinguish the rear side seat buckle from the center seat buckle before latching.
    • If foreign materials are stuck in the inside button of buckle, it may cause interference during the latching process, leading to a functional problem (for example - beverages (viscous drinks), toothpicks, sand, plastic, vinyl, metal pieces, etc.)
    • After latching the buckle and tongue, check the fastening force using a Push - Pull Gauge (fastening force: 3 kgf or less).
