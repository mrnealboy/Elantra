Brake lamp switch

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The ESP receives information on connection state of components and operation state through brake circuit. When you hit the brake pedal, the brake circuit sends a "ON" state signal to ESP and the pressure sensor data curve rises. While you release the brake pedal, brake circuit sends an "OFF" state signal to ESP.
