DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor resistance
  1. Check assembly of Squib
  2. Poor connection of Squib connector
Prerequisite Condition
  • Ignition switch ON
Threshold Value
  • DAB (1st stage) Squib resistance is greater than 6.9Ω
Diagnostic Time Qualification
  • More than 3 sec
De-Qualification
  • More than 6 sec
