Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. To install, reverse the removal procedures.
  2. Check the assembled state of the dowel pins (A) before installing the clutch actuator assembly.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Perform the clutch touch point learning procedure using the diagnostic tool.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Even if you removed and reinstalled the clutch actuator, be sure to perform the touch point learning.
    • If you have replaced the dual clutch assembly or clutch actuator, be sure to perform the touch point learning.
