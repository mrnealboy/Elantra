Crash Pad Antenna

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the main crash pad assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "MAIN CRASH PAD ASSEMBLY ")

  3. Loosen the mounting screws and then remove the crash pad antenna (A).
