DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Troubleshooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Rationality check
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Oil Pressure Sensor (OPS)
Prerequisite Condition
  • The vehicle speed is lower than 204 mph
  • Time after VDOP Close state is higher than 0 sec
Threshold Value Case 1
  • Differential of Oil Pressure is too low
Case 2
  • Differential of Oil Pressure is too high
Diagnostic Time
  • Immediately
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
