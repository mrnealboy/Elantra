Warning Lamp ON

  1. ESP warning lamp turn on for 3 sec after IGN ON.
  2. ESP function lamp blinks when ESP Act.
  3. If ESP fail occurred, ESP warning turns ON.
  4. ESP OFF lamp turn on in case of
    • ESP Switch OFF
    • 3 sec after IGN ON
