DTC Description

ECU interior when the output value of the internal temperature sensor exceeds the predetermined level (sensor specifications).

CAUTION: Continued and excessive handling of the steering wheel while driving or idling can make the steering wheel stiff. If this occurs, do not replace the steering column or EPS unit assembly as this is caused by entering the overheating protection mode, not a breakdown.
