Battery Sensor

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Battery sensor is connected to the (-) battery terminal. It sends battery voltage, current, temperature information to the engine Electronic Control Unit (ECU). Then, the ECU adjusts voltage by duty cycle based on these signals.
