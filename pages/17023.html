Repair Procedures: Removal

CAUTION:
  • It may be damaged when you try to remove the rain sensor from the windshield forcibly. Use caution when separating it from the windshield.
  • When detaching the silicone under a high temperature, it may be easily damaged because of the adhesive strength of the silicone. Remove the silicone in a cool environment.
  1. Remove the inside rear view mirror cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Using a remover and remove the rain sensor cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Be careful not to damage the cover latch by applying excessive force. To remove the latch, pull aside the latch using the cover hole with a small flat - blade screwdriver (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the rain sensor.
    1. Insert a small flat-blade screwdriver (-) into a space at the bottom (B) or top (A), between the rain sensor and bracket.
    2. Pull the rain sensor slowly up and down in the vertical direction of the glass, then disassemble the rain sensor slowly from windshield without damaging the silicone on the sensor.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
