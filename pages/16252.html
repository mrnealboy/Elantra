Intelligent Variable Transmission Fluid (IVTF) Level Check

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • When checking the IVTF level, be careful not to allow foreign substance (like dust) to enter through the filler hole.
  1. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - Battery - "REMOVAL , and BATTERY TRAY ")

  2. Add 0.3 liters of SP-IVT1 through the IVTF filler hole after removing the IVTF injection plug (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Start the engine to warm up the IVTF.
    NOTE:
    • Do not step on the brake and accelerator simultaneously to warm up the IVTF.
  4. Check by using Diagnostic tool that the temperature of the IVTF is between 50°C and 60°C (122 - 140°F).
  5. Move the shift lever slowly from "P" to "D", then back to "P". Repeat this sequence two times and then move the shift lever to "P" range.
    NOTE:
    • Maintain at each speed position for more than 2 sec.
  6. Remove the under cover.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  7. Lift the vehicle and remove the IVTF level plug (A) from the valve body cover.
    NOTE:
    • When removing the IVTF level plug, keep the vehicle on a level ground.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Check the IVTF level.

    Normally:  IVTF flows out in a thin oil stream.

    Excessive (abnormal):  Over 0.4 liters of IVTF flows out for 2 minutes.

    Insufficient (abnormal):  There is no fluid flow.

    NOTE:
    • If the IVTF level is too high or low, follow the instructions below.
    • Excessive: Drain until the IVTF flows out in a thin oil stream.
    • Insufficient: Add until the IVTF flows out in a thin oil stream.
  9. Install the IVTF level plug (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    34.3 - 44.1 N.m (3.5 - 4.5 kgf.m, 25.3 - 32.5 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Add 0.3 liters of SP-IVT1 through the IVTF filler hole after lower the vehicle
  11. The IVTF injection plug (A) is install with a new gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    34.3 - 44.1 N.m (3.5 - 4.5 kgf.m, 25.3 - 32.5 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Do not resuse gasket (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
