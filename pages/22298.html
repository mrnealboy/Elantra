Repair Procedures: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the idler (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 49.0 N.m (4.0 - 5.0 kgf.m, 28.9 - 36.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Remove the OAP cap (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When installing, replace with new OAP cap.
  3. Remove the OAP pulley (A) by using the SST (09373-27000).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the alternator cover (A) after loosening the mounting nut.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the regulator assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Unsolder the stator leads (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the rectifier assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the 4 through bolts (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Separate the rotor (A) and cover (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Install in the reverse order of removal.
