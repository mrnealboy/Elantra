DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • During auto-Location, Check its sensor's ID
  1. Jamming about RF signal.
  2. Faulty a signal of vehicle speed signal.
  3. Faulty a signal of Wheel Pulse signal.
  4. Faulty TPMS sensor.
Prerequisite Condition
  • Ignition switch ON
  • Sensor is normal
  • Voltage of BCM/IBU is normal
  • The vehicle speed is above 35 Km/h (22 mile/h) for 9 (EU, DOM)/19 (NA) minutes
Threshold Value
  • After 9 minutes of driving, ID learning succeeded with one or more new sensors included and autolocation failed. (EU, DOM: 9min/NA: 19min)
Diagnostic Time
  • 9 (EU, DOM)/19 (NA) minutes
Lamp
  • OFF
