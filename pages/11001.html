Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Recover the refrigerant with a recovery/recycling/charging station.
  3. When the engine is cool, drain the engine coolant from the radiator.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "COOLANT ")

  4. Remove the bolts and the expansion valve (A) from the evaporator core.

    Tightening torque: 

    8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Plug or cap the lines immediately after disconnecting them to avoid moisture and dust contamination.
    • When installing, replace with a new O-ring.
  5. Disconnect the heater hoses (A) from the heater unit.
    CAUTION:
    • Engine coolant will run out when the hoses are disconnected; drain it into a clean drip pan. Be sure not to let coolant spill on electrical parts or painted surfaces. If any coolant spills, rinse it off immediately.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the cowl top cover.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "COWL TOP COVER ")

  7. Remove the battery.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  8. Loosen the cowl cross member mounting bolts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the front pillar trim.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "FRONT PILLAR TRIM ")

  10. Remove the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "FLOOR CONSOLE ASSEMBLY ")

  11. Remove the shift lever assembly.

    (Refer to Automatic Transmission System - "SHIFT LEVER ")

  12. Remove the cowl side trim.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "COWL SIDE TRIM ")

  13. Remove the crash pad lower panel.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "CRASH PAD LOWER PANEL ")

  14. Remove the steering column shroud lower panel.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "STEERING COLUMN SHROUD PANEL ")

  15. Remove the steering wheel.

    (Refer to Steering System - "STEERING WHEEL ")

  16. Remove the multifunction switch.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "MULTIFUNCTION SWITCH ")

  17. Lower the steering column after loosening the mounting bolts.

    (Refer to Steering System - "MDPS COLUMN & HOUSING ")

  18. Disconnect the junction box connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Disconnect the multi box connectors (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  20. Remove the center console duct (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  21. Disconnect the airbag control module (SRSCM) connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  22. Remove the rear air duct (A) and separate the floor carpet (B) to obtain space for removing the rear heating duct.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  23. Remove the front air duct (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  24. Loosen the cowl blower unit mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  25. Remove the drain hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  26. Disconnect the connectors (A) and the mounting clips in the front pillar.

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  27. After loosening the bolts and nuts remove the main crash pad and cowl cross bar assembly (A) together.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  28. Disconnect the heater & blower unit connectors.

    [Driver 

    1. Loosen the mounting screw, remove the shower duct (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Disconnect the temperature control actuator connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Disconnect the mode control actuator connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [Passenger's side] 

    1. Loosen the mounting screw, remove the shower duct (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Disconnect the intake actuator connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Disconnect the temperature control actuator connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Disconnect the power mosfet connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    5. Disconnect the blower motor connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    6. Disconnect the evaporator temperature sensor connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  29. Loosen the heater & blower unit mounting bolt.

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  30. Remove the heater and blower unit (A) from the crash pad after loosening the mounting nuts.

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  31. Separate the blower unit (A) from the heater & blower unit assembly after loosening the screws.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  32. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
