DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Abnormal IATS signal (Signal value is stuck)
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. IATS
Prerequisite Condition
  • ECT at Start is less than 54.75°C (130.55°F)
  • Number of following driving conditions for at least 18 sec is higher than 3 times
    • Vehicle speed is lower than 4.8 Km/h
    • ECT is higher than 55°C (131°F)
    • Air flow is lower than 5.6g/sec
    • Integrated Air Mass is higher than 2000g
  • Number of following driving conditions for at least 8 sec is higher than 2 times
  • Vehicle speed is higher than 50 Km/h
  • Air flow is between 6.67 and 83.3g/sec
Threshold Value
  • The maximum temperature and minimum temperature difference value is lower than 1.5°C (34.7°F)
Diagnostic Time
  • 100 ms
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
