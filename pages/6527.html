Repair Procedures: Removal

NOTE:
  • Use Fender cover to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • To avoid damaging the cylinder head, wait until the engine coolant temperature drops below normal temperature before removing it.
  • When handling a metal gasket, take care not to fold the gasket or damage the contact surface of the gasket.
  • To avoid damage, unplug the wiring connectors carefully while holding the connector portion.

Information 

  • Mark all wiring and hoses to avoid misconnection.
  • Turn the crankshaft pulley so that the No. 1 piston is at top dead center.
  1. Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

    Tightening torque: 

  2. Remove the engine cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE COVER ")

  3. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  4. Drain the coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  5. Remove the air duct and air cleaner assembly.

    (Refer to Intake and Exhaust System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  6. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  7. Remove the engine ground cable (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    10.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.1 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.0 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Disconnect the radiator upper hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Disconnect the coolant reservoir tank coolant hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Disconnect the heater hoses (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Disconnect the brake vacuum hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Disconnect the fuel hose (A) and purge control solenoid valve (PCSV) hose (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Remove the intake manifold.

    (Refer to Intake and Exhaust System - "INTAKE MANIFOLD ")

  14. Remove the delivery pipe.

    (Refer to Engine Control/Fuel System - "DELIVERY PIPE ")

  15. Remove the exhaust manifold.

    (Refer to Intake and Exhaust System - "EXHAUST MANIFOLD ")

  16. Remove the vacuum pump (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  17. Remove the integrated thermal management module (ITM).

    (Refer to Cooling System - "INTEGRATED THERMAL MANAGEMENT MODULE (ITM) ")

  18. Remove the heater pipe mounting bolts (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Remove the spark plug.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "SPARK PLUG ")

  20. Remove the timing chain.

    (Refer to Timing System - "TIMING CHAIN ")

  21. Remove the camshaft front bearing cap (A) and camshaft bearing caps (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  22. Remove the CVVD assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • After removing the CVVD unit, make sure that the parts such as lifter, cam, etc. are not separated.
    • Ensure that the CVVD components are not damaged.

    When Using the CVVD fixture 

    1. Install the CVVD fixture (A) over the CVVD assembly.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Remove the CVVD assembly (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    When not using the CVVD fixture 

    1. Remove the CVVD assembly (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  23. Remove the exhaust camshaft & CVVT assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  24. Remove the cylinder head heat protector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  25. Remove the cylinder head degassing water hose and pipe (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  26. Remove the cam carrier (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  27. Remove the hydraulic lash adjuster (HLA) (A) and swing arm (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  28. Remove the cylinder head.
    1. Uniformly loosen and remove the cylinder head bolts, in several passes, in the sequence shown.
      Fig 1: Cylinder Head Bolts Loosening Sequence
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Removing cylinder head bolts in an incorrect order may result in head warpage or crack.
      • Use dodecagonal driver socket that has under 15 mm (0.5906 in.) external diameter and longer than 75 mm (2.9528 in.) cylinder to not have interference between cylinder head and dodecagonal driver socket when the cylinder head bolt removal.

      Removing cylinder head bolts in an incorrect order may result in head warpage or crack.

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Lift the cylinder head (A) from the cylinder block and put the cylinder head on wooden blocks.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Be careful not to damage the contact surfaces of the cylinder head and cylinder block.
    3. Remove the cylinder head gasket (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Do not reuse the cylinder head gasket.
