Smart Key Unit (IBU)

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the glove box housing cover.

    (Refer to Body - "GLOVE BOX HOUSING COVER .")

  3. Remove the smart key unit (IBU) (A) after loosening bolt and nuts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect smart key unit (IBU) connectors.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
