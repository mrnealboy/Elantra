DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Check Yaw Rate sensor
  1. Faulty yaw rate sensor
Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery voltage is normal
  • 1.5 sec. after turning the ignition switch on
  • Turn on the BSD or RCTA switch
  • Engine is running
Threshold Value
  • An abnormal signal was received from the yaw rate sensor
Diagnostic Time
  • 0.5 sec.
