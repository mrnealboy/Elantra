GDS Current Data

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn the ignition switch and engine "ON".
  3. Monitor the "BATTERY VOLTAGE" parameter on the "Current Data" menu of the GDS.

    Specification  : Below. 16.5±0.5V
