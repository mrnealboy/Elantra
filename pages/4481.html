Trunk Lid Latch

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to scratch the body surface.
  1. Remove the tail trunk lid trim. (Refer to Trunk Lid - "TRUNK LID TRIM ")
  2. Disconnect the connector (A) and cable (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Loosen the mounting bolts and remove the trunk lid latch (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    NOTE:
    • Make sure the tail gate locks/unlocks and opens/closes properly.
    • Make sure the connectors are connected in properly.
