Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Remove the rear door trim. (Refer to Rear Door - "REAR DOOR TRIM ")
  2. Remove the rear door belt inside weatherstrip (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the rear door belt outside weatherstrip (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting screw, remove the rear door frame garnish (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Loosen the mounting bolt and nuts, remove the rear door channel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the rear door window glass mounting hole plug (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Carefully adjust the rear door window glass (A) until the bolts are visible, then loosen them. Separate the glass from the glass run and carefully pull the glass out through the window slot.

    Tightening torque: 

    5.9 - 8.8 N.m (0.6 - 0.9 kgf.m, 4.3 - 6.5 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to drop the glass and/or scratch the glass surface.
  8. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure that the glass rolls up and down smoothly.
    • Adjust glass position as needed.
    • Make sure the door locks and opens properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
