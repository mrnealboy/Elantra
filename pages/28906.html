DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor FCA CAN signal
  1. pen/Short in CAN communication circuit between BCWECU and FCA
  2. Faulty FCA (FORWARDCOLLISIONAVOIDANCEASSIST UNIT)
Prerequisite Condition
  • 2 seconds after turning ignition switch ON
  • Under proper input voltage (No C110117/C110216 set)
  • BCW ON or RCCW ON
  • No CAN communication related DTC has set
  • FCA transmission type
Threshold Value
  • No CAN message has been transmitted from FCA
Diagnostic Time
  • After 1 sec.
