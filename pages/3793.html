DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy
  • Voltage monitoring
  1. Poor connection
  2. Open in rear right door speaker circuit
  3. Rear right door speaker
Enable Conditions
  • Speaker connection
Threshold Value
  • If it is detected that no sound is coming from the rear right door speaker when the speaker is connected
Diagnostic Time
  • 10s
Recovery Condition
  • When performing forced diagnosis
    • Built-in Amplifier: Determine whether to connect by reading specific register communication of built-in amplifier chip
    • External Amplifier: Check the DTC through UDS CAN communication (judged by an external amplifier for connection)
