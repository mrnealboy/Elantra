DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy 
  • Monitor the Ejector Pressure
  1. Poor connection
  2. High load purge line ejector stuck
  3. Leaking, disconnected or plugged high load purge line
  4. Damaged or missing Fuel Filler Cap
  5. Faulty or damaged Fuel Filler Pipe
  6. Leaking, Disconnected or Plugged Fuel Vapor Line
  7. Faulty Canister
PCSV Closed  Enable Condition 
  • PCSV* control: Closed
  • Common conditions for high load purge line monitoring*
Threshold Value 
  • Ejector Pressure exceeds the threshold value from -25 hPa to -110 hPa within Boost Pressure from 200 hPa to 1, 200 hPa.
PCSV Opened  Enable Condition 
  • PCSV* control duty: Above 99% (Wide Opened)
  • Common conditions for high load purge line monitoring*
Threshold Value 
  • Ejector Pressure is lower than the threshold value from -50 hPa to -280 hPa within Boost Pressure from 200 hPa to 1, 200 hPa.
Diagnostic Time 
  • Once per Driving Cycle*
MIL 
  • 2nd Driving Cycle*
Common conditions for high load purge line monitoring* 
  • Ejector Pressurization pressure: 300 hPa to 1, 200 hPa
  • Pressure upstream throttle valve: Valid
  • Pressure upstream booster: Valid
  • Ambient pressure: Valid
  • Measured Manifold Absolute Pressure: Valid
  • Dump valve: Closed
  • Difference between Manifold Absolute Pressure and Barometric Pressure: Above 200 hPa
  • Change of Difference between Manifold Absolute Pressure and Barometric Pressure: Above -150 hPa
  • Barometric Pressure: Above 746 hPa
  • Absolute value of Barometric Pressure change: Above 16 hPa
  • Power Voltage: 9V to 16V
  • Engine coolant temperature at startng: -54.4°F to 289.85°F (-48°C to 143.25°C)
  • Ambient air temperature: -54.4°F to 289.85°F (-48°C to 143.25°C)
  • Fuel level: 7.1 l  to 40 l 
Driving Cycle* 
  • When [IG ON→Engine ON→IG ON] is performed, the Driving Cycle* 1time (1DC) is completed.
  • "MIL ON" means when the fault during the 1st DC is still detected in the 2nd DC, DTC is confirmed and the ECM* outputs 'Warning Lamp ON'.
