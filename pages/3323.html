DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy
  • Check LTE1/3G antenna information
  1. Poor connection
  2. LTE1/3G antenna circuit is short
  3. Faulty combination antenna
  4. Faulty AVN
Enable Conditions
  • After power up, not during engine start and 15s after engine start
Threshold Value
  • Antenna short is detected
Diagnostic Time
  • 5s
Reset Criteria
  • No open load, antenna connected and system restart or self-test
Reset Time
  • 0s
Symptom
  • LTE performance not good
