DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the voltage
  1. Poor contact of wire harness
  2. Open/short in motor control circuit
  3. Faulty passenger seat temperature actuator
  4. Faulty A/C control module
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn the ignition switch ON
Threshold Value
  • When the actuator does not move more than 0.2V within 3 seconds after working
Fail Safe
  • Maintain the current position
