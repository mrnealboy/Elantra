Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Remove the manual transaxle assembly.

    (Refer to Manual Transaxle System - "MANUAL TRANSAXLE ")

  2. Loosen the bolts and then removing the concentric slave cylinder assembly (A) from the clutch housing.

    Tightening torque: 

    19.6 - 26.5 N.m (2.0 - 2.7 kgf.m, 14.2 - 19.5 lb-ft)
