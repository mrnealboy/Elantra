DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor signals
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Barometric Pressure Sensor (BPS)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Engine speed is lower than 1000 RPM
  • Throttle position is lower than 8%
Threshold Value
  • he difference between an atmospheric pressure sensor value and a manifold pressure sensor value is either too great or too little
Diagnostic Time
  • 2 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
