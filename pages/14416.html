DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Rationality check
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. E-WGA
Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery voltage is higher than 9.5V
  • ECT is higher than 5.25°C (41.45°F)
  • IAT is higher than 5.25°C (41.45°F)
  • Control duty is lower than -25%
  • Closed position adaptation
Threshold Value Case1
  • Waste gate close position voltage at closed position adaptation is higher than 4.6V
Case2
  • Waste gate close position voltage at closed position adaptation is lower than 3.2V
Diagnostic Time
  • Continuously
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
