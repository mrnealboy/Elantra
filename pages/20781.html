Piston Pins

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Measure the diameter of the piston pin.

    Piston pin diameter: 

    Standard: 19.997 - 20.000 mm (0.78728 - 0.78740 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Measure the piston pin-to-piston clearance.

    Piston pin-to-piston clearance: 

    Standard: 0.009 - 0.016 mm (0.00035 - 0.00063 in.)

  3. Check the clearance between the piston pin outer diameter and the connecting rod small end inner diameter.

    Piston pin-to-connecting rod clearance: 

    Standard: 0.005 - 0.014 mm (0.00020 - 0.00055 in.)
