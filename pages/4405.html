Trunk Open Switch

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the inside rear combination lamp (A).

    (Refer to Lighting System - "REAR COMBINATION LAMP ")

  3. Remove the trunk rid open switch assembly (A).
