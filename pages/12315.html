Removal and Installation

NOTE:
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • To avoid damage, unplug the wiring connectors carefully while holding the connector portion.

Information 

  • Mark all wiring connector and hoses to avoid misconnection.
  1. Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
  2. Remove the engine cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE COVER ")

  3. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  4. Drain the coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  5. Recover the A/C refrigerant and then remove the high & low pressure pipes of A/C compressor.

    (Refer to Heating, Ventilation Air conditioning - Compressor - "REPAIR PROCEDURES , and GENERAL SAFETY INFORMATION & CAUTION ")

  6. Remove the air duct and air cleaner assembly.

    (Refer to Intake and Exhaust System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  7. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "REMOVAL , and BATTERY TRAY ")

  8. Disconnect the brake booster vacuum hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Disconnect the fuel hose (A) and PCSV hose (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Disconnect the wiring connectors and harness clamps and remove the connector brackets around the engine and transaxle assembly.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Disconnect the engine ground cable (A).

    Tightening torque:  10.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.1 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.0 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Disconnect the transaxle ground cable (A).

    Tightening torque:  26.5 - 32.4 N.m (2.7 - 3.3 kgf.m, 19.5 - 23.9 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Remove the intercooler inlet hose and pipe.
    1. Disconnect the RCV vacuum hose (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Disconnect the RCV valve connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Remove the intercooler inlet hose and pipe (A).

      Tightening torque:  Clamp bolt: 4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • Insert the hose until it reaches the end of stopper.
  14. Remove the intercooler outlet hose and pipe.
    1. Disconnect the intercooler outlet hose (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

      Information 

      1. When disconnecting the intercooler hose, pull the quick connector clamp (A) in the direction of the arrow as shown in illustration below.
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      2. Move the quick connector clamp from B to C position as shown in illustrations below and then disconnect by pulling the quick connector hose.
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Loosen the intercooler outlet pipe bolt, and then remove the intercooler outlet hose and pipe (A).

      Tightening torque:  Bracket bolt: 6.86 - 10.78 N.m (0.7 - 1.1 kgf.m, 5.06 - 7.96 lb-ft)

      Clamp bolt: 4.90 - 6.86 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.62 - 5.06 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Disconnect the radiator upper hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Disconnect the radiator lower hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  17. Disconnect the coolant reservoir tank water hoses (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  18. Disconnect the heater hoses (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Remove the universal joint from the shaft joint

    (Refer to Steering System - "Steering Column and Shaft - MDPS COLUMN & HOUSING ")

  20. Remove the front muffler heat protector (A).

    Tightening torque:  9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  21. Loosen the hanger (A) and then remove the front muffler (B).

    Tightening torque:  39.2 - 58.8 N.m (4.0 - 6.0 kgf.m, 28.9 - 43.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  22. Remove the roll rod bracket (A).

    Tightening torque:  Bolt (B): 107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    Bolts (C): 49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  23. Remove the roll rod mounting support bracket (A).

    Tightening torque:  49.0 - 68.6 N.m (5.0 - 7.0 kgf.m, 36.2 - 50.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  24. Remove the sub frame.

    (Refer to Suspension System - "SUB FRAME ")

  25. Support the engine and transaxle assembly safely with a floor jack.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  26. Remove the engine mounting support bracket (A).

    Tightening torque:  Nut (B): 88.3 - 107.9 N.m (9.0 - 11.0 kgf.m, 65.1 - 79.6 lb-ft)

    Bolt, Nuts (C): 58.8 - 73.6 N.m (6.0 - 7.5 kgf.m, 43.4 - 54.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  27. Remove the service cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  28. Remove the transaxle mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque:  88.3 - 107.9 N.m (9.0 - 11.0 kgf.m, 65.1 - 79.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  29. Remove the engine and transaxle assembly from a vehicle by slowly lifting off the vehicle.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Before removing the engine assembly, make sure to check that the hoses and wire connectors are completely disconnected.
    • When removing the engine and transaxle assembly, be careful not to damage any surrounding parts or body components.
  30. Install in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:
    • Refill a radiator and a reservoir tank with engine coolant.

      (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    • Check the engine oil level.

      If necessary, refill engine with engine oil.

    • Check the transaxle oil level.

      If necessary, refill a transaxle with fluid.

    • Charging the refrigerant gas.
    • Inspect for fuel leakage.
