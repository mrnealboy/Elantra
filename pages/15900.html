DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor CAN message
  1. Poor contact of connectors
  2. Open/Short in CAN communication line
  3. Faulty FCA (=Radar) (Smart Cruise Control Radar)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn ignition switch ON
  • Battery voltage is within the normal value (9 ~ 16V)
Threshold Value
  • No message from CGW or Radar
Diagnosis Time
  • 5 sec
