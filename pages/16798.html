Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
  1. Remove the trunk lid trim. (Refer to Trunk Lid - "TRUNK LID TRIM ")
  2. Disconnect the rear combination lamp connector (A).
  3. Loosen the mounting bolts, remove the rear combination lamp (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting bolts, remove the trunk lid back panel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connectors are connected in properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips.
