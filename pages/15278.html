Corrective Action

  • See if there are DTCs related to engine's vehicle speed. If yes, inspect and repair the engine system. See if the DTC is cleared in the MDPS, and then go to the next procedure.
  • Replace the EMS ECU with a working one and check for proper operation. If it works properly, put the new EMS ECU in place. Then, go to "Verification of Vehicle Repair".
