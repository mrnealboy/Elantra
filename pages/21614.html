Repair Procedures: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal.
  2. Remove the fuse box cover.
  3. Remove the starter relay (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Check for continuity between the terminals (30 and 87) using an ohmmeter.
    Terminal Resistance Measure
    30 - 87 1 MΩ or higher If there is continuity between the terminals, replace the relay.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Apply 12V to the terminal 85 and ground to the terminal 86 and then check for continuity between the terminals (30 and 87).
    Terminal Resistance Measure
    30 - 87 1Ω or less If there is no continuity between the terminals, replace the relay.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Install the starter relay.
  7. Install the fuse box cover.
