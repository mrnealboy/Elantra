Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. Install the blind spot radar unit and bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    bracket: 3.9 - 5.9 N

    unit: 3.92 ± 0.105 N.m

  2. Install the rear bumper.
  3. Connect the negative (-) battery terminal.
    NOTE:
    • Take care not to confuse left side (master) and right side (slave) unit when installing.
    • BCW units connector connect to the wiring connector.
  4. Perform "CORRECTING THE BLIND-SPOT RADAR UNIT ANGLE " procedures.
  5. Perform "BSD Radar Calibration" procedures.
