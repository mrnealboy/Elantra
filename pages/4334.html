DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the CAN communication
  1. Driver side door is open (SMK* is not in Sleep mode)
  2. Poor contact of connector
  3. Input power to SMK* (PIC IMMO unit)
  4. Simultaneous Short-to-Ground or to power in both B-CAN High and Low communication lines
Prerequisite Condition
  • Power Voltage: Between 9V and 16V
  • B-CAN communication: Active
Threshold Value
  • There is a Short-to-Battery in both B-CAN High and Low communication lines simultaneously or there is a Short-to-Ground
Diagnostic Time
  • The trouble persists for 2 sec
DTC Clearing Active DTC
  • DTC will be cleared within 1 sec of B-CAN Bus Recovery after the B-CAN Lines return to the normal state
History DTC
  • DTC will be cleared when the B-CAN Error PIN returns to the normal state and the ignition switch is turned on and off for 30 times
NOTE: SMK*: "IBU"
