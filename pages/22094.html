DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy
  • CAN communication monitoring
  1. Poor connection
  2. Faulty P-CAN communication line
  3. Faulty EOPU
  4. Faulty PCM
Enable Conditions
  • Time after PCM turns on is longer than 0.5 sec
  • Battery voltage is between 10V to 16V
  • Turbine speed is higher than 400 RPM
Threshold Value
  • EOPU CAN timeout or abnormal massage is detected
Detecting Time
  • More than 1 sec
MIL ON Condition
  • No MIL On
Fail Safe
  • ISG prohibited
