DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor current
  1. Switch wire harness and connector
  2. Switch components
Prerequisite Condition
  • Ignition switch ON
Threshold Value
  • Switch line current is between 7.98 mA and 10.59 mA
Diagnostic Time Qualification
  • More than 1 sec
De-Qualification
  • More than 2 sec
