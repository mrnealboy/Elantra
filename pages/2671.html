DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the CAN signal
  1. Faulty navigation control module
  2. Device does not support HDA (make sure if it is the right type)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn the ignition switch ON or start the engine
Threshold Value
  • Specification signals from the navigation control module do not match with the signals from an HDA-installed navigation device
Diagnosis Time
  • Turn the ignition switch ON and wait for 60 sec The trouble will be detected after 30 sec
