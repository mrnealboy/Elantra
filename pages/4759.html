Brake Disc Temperature

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The ESP calculates the DISC temperature with the Wheel speed, vehicle speed change, control mode and BLS (Brake Lamp Signal) signal.

The calculated DISC Temperature is used to protect DISC from overheating caused by overuse which may cause a decrease in brake efficiency.
