DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Check for the CAN bus
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Open/short in CAN communication circuit
  3. Faulty ESC/ESP
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn the ignition switch on or start the engine
Threshold Value
  • An ESC/ESP CAN message has not been received
Diagnosis Time
  • 0.5 sec
