DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor signals
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Open or short in signal circuit
  3. Electronic Stability Control (ESC) Module
Case 1 Prerequisite Condition
  • Sensor signal no error
Threshold Value
  • The vehicle speed signal is over 300 km/h
Diagnostic Time
  • 2 sec
Case 2 Prerequisite Condition
  • Engine speed is over 1800 RPM
  • APS is higher than 25%
  • Engine clutch torque is over 25Nm
  • Engaged gear (AT Only, not required for MT) is higher than 1
Threshold Value
  • Vehicle speed is lower than 1 km/h
Diagnostic Time
  • 30 sec
Case 3 Prerequisite condition
  • Engine speed is between 1000 and 3500 RPM
  • Fuel system status is fuel cut-off
Threshold Value
  • Vehicle speed is lower than 4 km/h
Diagnostic Time
  • 20 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
