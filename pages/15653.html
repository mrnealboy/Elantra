DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor input signal (CAN Tx)
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Input power to IAU*
  3. Simultaneous short to battery/or a short to ground in both CAN HIGH and LOW communication lines
  4. IAU* module
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn the ignition switch ON.
Threshold Value
  • A simultaneous short to battery/or a short to ground in both CAN High and CAN LOW communication lines
Diagnosis Time
  • The trouble persists for 2 sec
DTC Clearing
  • Normal Can communication (DTC will be cleared within 1 sec after detecting normal operation)
NOTE: IAU*: "Identity Authentication Unit"
