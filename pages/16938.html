Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Remove the rear door window glass. (Refer to Rear Door - "REAR DOOR WINDOW GLASS ")
  2. Remove the rear door outside handle. (Refer to Rear Door - "REAR DOOR OUTSIDE HANDLE ")
  3. Loosen the mounting bolt and nut, remove the rear door channel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the outside handle pad (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Loosen the rear door outside handle base mounting bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Press the lock pin, separate the rear door main connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Loosen the front door latch mounting screws.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the mounting bolts and remove the rear door module (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure connectors are connected properly and each rod is connected securely.
    • Make sure the door locks/unlocks and opens/closes properly.
