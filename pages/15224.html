DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the battery supply voltage
  1. Poor contact of the ignition circuit connector
  2. Open/short in wire harness
  3. EPS ECU was removed while the ignition is not OFF
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn on the ignition switch and start the engine
Threshold Value
  • Inhibit the battery power supply before turning the ignition switch OFF
Diagnosis Time
  • Immediately
