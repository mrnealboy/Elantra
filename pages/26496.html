DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Case 1 Main action
  • Stuck Check
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Faulty TMM control circuit
  3. Faulty TMM
Prerequisite Condition
  • TMM Position is between 0° and 255°
  • 1.5 degree or less in location change of the TMM for 100 msec
Threshold Value
  • TMM control duty is 40% or more
Diagnostic Time
  • 5 sec
Case 2 Main action
  • Target Error Check
Prerequisite Condition
  • TMM Position is between 0° and 255°
  • 1.5 degree or less in location change of the TMM for 100 msec
  • TMM Motor Duty is greater than 0%
Threshold Value
  • 8 degree or more difference between the TMM's designated location and its actual location
Diagnostic Time
  • 20 sec
Case 3 Main action
  • Response Check
Prerequisite condition
  • TMM Position is between 0° and 255°
  • 0.5 degree or less in location change of the TMM for 100 msec
  • TMM Motor Duty is greater than 0%
Threshold Value
  • 0V or more difference between the Motor output voltage and its Current Batt. Voltage
Diagnostic Time
  • 20 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
