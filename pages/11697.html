Rear Seat Belt Pretensioner (BPT)

  1. Remove the rear package tray trim. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "REAR PACKAGE TRAY TRIM ")
  2. Disconnect the airbag connector (A) after pushing the lock pin.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the rear seat belt pretensioner (A) after loosening the mounting bolt.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
