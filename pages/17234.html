DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy
  • CAN Message Monitoring
  1. Poor connector connection
  2. Faulty P-CAN communication circuit
  3. Faulty PCM
Enable Condition
  • Battery voltage is greater than 9V
  • Delay time after ignition switch on is more than 0.5 seconds
Threshold Value
  • An invalid data is received from TCM
Diagnostic Time
  • Continuous
MIL ON Condition
  • 2 driving cycles
