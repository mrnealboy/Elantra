Wheel Speed Sensor

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

A Wheel Speed Sensor is an essential component of the ESP (ESC/Electronic Stability Control Program) because the ESP calculates vehicle speed and determines whether wheel lock occurs based on data from the sensor.

For example, front-wheel vehicles use speed of a faster rear wheel as a reference when determining vehicle speed, and, if the vehicle detects difference in speed between front and rear wheels, the ABS control sets in.
