10.25 TFF LCD [LHD]

NOTE:
  • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
  • When prying with a flat-tip screwdriver, wrap it with protective tape, and apply protective tape around the related parts, to prevent damage.
  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Using a flat-tip screwdriver or remover, remove the photo sensor cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect photo sensor connector and security sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Using a flat-tip screwdriver or remover, remove the steering column shroud upper panel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Using a flat-tip screwdriver or remover, remove the cluster fascia upper garnish (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the cluster fascia side panel after mounting screws.

    (Refer to Body - "CLUSTER FASCIA SIDE PANEL ")

  7. Remove the instrument cluster (A) after loosening the mounting screws.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the instrument cluster after disconnect cluster connectors.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
