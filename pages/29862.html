TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 24-EE-001H, Date of Issue:  May 07, 2024
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: ENGINE ELECTRICAL

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin describes operation of the DCA-8000 for battery related repair work. The DCA-8000 is a state of the art tool that checks battery health and then performs a reliable intelligent safe charge, which can quickly restore some batteries to good condition. For example, a battery may be fully revived within 1.5 hours if it has not been in a discharged state for an extended period of time.

Applicable Vehicles:  Vehicles not equipped with GDS Battery Diagnosis Function.

Information 

Battery claims will be subject to chargeback if submitted with the wrong tester code.

Connect DCA-8000 Battery Test clamps or Charger clamps directly to battery posts, and NOT  remote terminals in the engine compartment for batteries located remotely.

Select the correct battery type, "Flooded" or "AGM," during DCA-8000 Battery Testing to ensure accurate results.

Charging with the wrong battery type may damage and reduce battery life.

Before working on the battery, check the battery specification label on top and choose the appropriate battery type.

Enter the "SAE CCA" and RC values from the battery label for accurate DCA-8000 battery CCA and RC ratings.

Replace the battery with the same type originally installed from the factory.

It is recommended to keep a printed copy of the battery test result.

DCA-8000 BASIC OPERATION AND BATTERY WARRANTY POLICY

  1. Start by reviewing TSB 24-EE-003H  and use the appropriate testing method.
  2. For a Charge and Retest  result from Cadex Spectro Modular TSB 24-EE-004H  , perform a Diagnostic Charge  . If the battery can't be revived, it will typically indicate Replace Battery  within 20 minutes.
  3. If the battery passes the Diagnostic Charge  , it will continue charging until reaching a full charge (up to 1.5 hours) and display Good Battery  , generating a printout upon successful completion.
  4. Attach both Cadex Spectro Modular Battery Test  and Diagnostic Charge  printouts to the warranty repair order, entering VIN and RO numbers through the DCA-8000 keyboard.

    Information 

    The Diagnostic Charge  must be performed if the battery test from Cadex Spectro Modular TSB 24-EE-004H  resulted in Charge & Retest  .

Performing the Battery Test

  1. Clamp the cables onto the proper battery terminals.
  2. Select Diagnostic Charge  (A).
  3. Select Yes  (B).
  4. Scan the VIN (C) on the driver side door jamb with the handheld scanner.
  5. To determine the battery type and CCA rating, locate the information (D) on the battery.

    Select the proper Battery Type  (E), and then select Next  (F).

    Input the CCA Rating  (G), and select Next  (H).
  6. Confirm the battery information and press Next  (I).
  7. Wait for the battery test to be completed.
  8. Verify the charging results and select one of the following to access the barcode:
    • Output Result  (J) > Print  > Printed Receipt
    • Press for Barcode  (K)> Digital Receipt

Inputting Test Results into GDS-Smart

  1. Select GDS-Smart  (A).
  2. Select Battery Test/Claim  (B).
  3. Select Scan Battery Charge Result  (C).
  4. Use the GDS tablet to scan the barcode on the printed or digital receipt and select Next  (D).
  5. Select OK  (E).

    The procedure is now complete.
