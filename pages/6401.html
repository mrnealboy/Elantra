Cooling Fan Assembly

  1. Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
  2. Remove the air duct and air cleaner assembly.

    (Refer to Intake and Exhaust System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  3. Disconnect the cooling fan connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the degassing hose (A) and hose fastener.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the filler neck assembly (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 Nm (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the cooling fan (A) from the radiator.

    Tightening torque: 

    4.9 - 7.8 N.m (0.5 - 0.8 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Install in the reverse order of removal.

- Disassembly 

  1. Remove the cooling fan (A) from the cooling fan assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    6.86 - 8.82 N.m (0.7 - 0.9 kgf.m, 5.06 - 6.51 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Remove the fan motor (A) from the cooling fan shroud.

    Tightening torque: 

    8.83 - 10.79 N.m (0.9 - 1.1 kgf.m, 6.51 - 7.96 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install in the reverse order of removal.
