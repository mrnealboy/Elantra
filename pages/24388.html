Heated Steering Wheel Control Module (Integrated Body Control Unit (IBU))

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the glove box upper cover. (Refer to Body - "GLOVE BOX HOUSING COVER ")
  3. Remove the IBU (A) after loosening the mounting nuts and bolt.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the IBU after disconnecting the connector.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
