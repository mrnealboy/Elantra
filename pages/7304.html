DTC Description

ECM checks output signals from HO2S under detecting condition. ECM judges it's signals too slow and sets P013A00, if output signal from rear HO2S isn't increased up to specification level within a short time after fuel cut.

(This DTC might be caused by the malfunction of Heater circuit, so, check the heater circuit first.)
