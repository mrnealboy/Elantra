Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery (-) terminal.
  2. Remove the drive belt.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "DRIVE BELT ")

  3. Separate the wiring clip (A) and disconnect the alternator connector (B) and then remove the cable nut (C) from the alternator "B" terminal.

    Tightening torque: 

    18.6 - 24.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.5 kgf.m, 13.7 - 18.1 Ib-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the alternator mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 Ib-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the alternator (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
