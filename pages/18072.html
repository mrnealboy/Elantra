Oil Pump Solenoid Valve

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Variable Oil Pump is a system to improve fuel efficiency by optimizing engine oil pressure with controlling it as two modes (low/high) according to engine RPM.

Oil pressure regulating valve turns ON in low pressure mode. Discharge rate of engine oil is decreased as bypassed oil lifting up rotor. It makes pressure of engine oil decreased.

Fuel efficiency is improved as engine load to rotate oil pump pulley reduced.

Mode change depends on engine RPM, oil temperature, and engine load.
