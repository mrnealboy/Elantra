Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Perform the oil pressure characteristics backup procedure using the diagnostic tool when replacing with a new TCM.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Disconnect the TCM connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Loosen the bolts and then removing the TCM & bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the bolts and then removing the TCM (B) from the bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
