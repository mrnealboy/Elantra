Checking Stationary Steering Effort

  1. Position the vehicle on a level surface and place the steering wheel in the straight ahead position.
  2. While maintaining the engine speed at IDLE RPM, apply the spring balance to the end of the steering wheel and pull the scale to measure the force when the steering wheel starts moving.

    Standard value:  29.4 N.m (3.0 kgf.m, 21.7 Ib-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. If the measured value exceeds standard value, inspect the steering gear box and MDPS system.
