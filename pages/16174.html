Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Use a plastic panel removal tool to remove interior trim pieces without marring the surface.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
  1. Remove the crash pad garnish [CTR, RH]. (Refer to Crash Pad - "CRASH PAD GARNISH ")
  2. Remove the glove box housing cover. (Refer to Crash Pad - "GLOVE BOX HOUSING COVER ")
  3. Remove the crash pad lower panel. (Refer to Crash Pad - "CRASH PAD LOWER PANEL ")
  4. To remove the gear knob & gear boots (A) pull both of it up.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the floor console side garnish (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. After loosening the mounting screw and then using a screwdriver or remover, remove the console upper cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Press the lock pin separate the console upper cover connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the A/C & heater controller unit (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Disconnect the A/C & heater controller connectors (A) and hose (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the AVN keyboard assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Press the lock pin, separate the start button connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Remove the USB port assembly (A) by pulling it in the direction of the arrow.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the crash pad center panel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector are connected in properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
