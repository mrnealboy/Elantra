Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  2. Remove the battery and tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  3. Remove the shift cable (A) from the cable bracket, after loosening the mounting nut.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the shift lever knob & boots (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ")

  6. Remove the air duct (A) after loosening the bolts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Separate the shift cable socket (B) after removing the snap pin (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the retainer (B) by loosening the nuts (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the shift cable (A) from the vehicle.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
