Repair Procedures: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • Use fender covers to avoid damaging painted surfaces.
  • To avoid damaging the cylinder head, wait until the engine coolant temperature drops below normal temperature before removing it.
  • When handling a metal gasket, take care not to fold the gasket or damage the contact surface of the gasket.
  • To avoid damage, unplug the wiring connectors carefully while holding the connector portion.
  1. Remove the engine assembly from the vehicle.

    (Refer to Engine And Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE AND TRANSAXLE ASSEMBLY ")

  2. Disconnect the wiring connectors and harness clamps and remove the connector brackets around the engine and transaxle assembly
  3. Remove the transaxle assembly form the engine assembly.

    (Refer to Automatic Transaxle System - "REPAIR PROCEDURES ")

  4. Install the engine assembly to engine stand.
  5. Remove the intake manifold.

    (Refer to Intake And Exhaust System - "INTAKE MANIFOLD ")

  6. Remove the heater pipe.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "INTEGRATED THERMAL MANAGEMENT MODULE (ITM) "

  7. Remove the A/C compressor.

    (Refer to Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning - "COMPRESSOR ")

  8. Remove the water pump.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "WATER PUMP ")

  9. Remove the exhaust manifold.

    (Refer to Intake And Exhaust System - "EXHAUST MANIFOLD ")

  10. Remove the cylinder head cover.

    (Refer to Cylinder Head Assembly - "CYLINDER HEAD COVER ")

  11. Remove the timing chain.

    (Refer to Timing System - "TIMING CHAIN ")

  12. Remove the Integrated Thermal Management Module (ITM)

    (Refer to Cooling System - "INTEGRATED THERMAL MANAGEMENT MODULE (ITM) ")

  13. Remove the cylinder head assembly.

    (Refer to Cylinder Head Assembly - "CYLINDER HEAD ")

  14. Remove the oil filter body.

    (Refer to Lubrication System - "OIL FILTER & OIL COOLER ")

  15. Remove the oil pan.

    (Refer to Lubrication System - "OIL PAN ")

  16. Remove the oil pump.

    (Refer to Lubrication System - "OIL PUMP ")

  17. Check the connecting rod side clearance.
  18. Remove the piston and connecting rod assemblies from the cylinder block.
    1. Remove all the carbon from the top of the cylinder.
    2. Remove the connecting rod bearing caps (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

      Information 

      • When reassembling, mark the cylinder number on the connecting road and cap to install at the correct position.
    3. Push the piston, connecting rod assembly and upper bearing through the top of the cylinder block.

      Information 

      • Keep the bearings, connecting rod and cap together.
      • Arrange the piston and connecting rod assemblies in the correct order.
      • Mark the cylinder number on the piston and connecting rod assemblies to be able to reassemble in the original position.
  19. Check fit between piston and piston pin.

    Try to move the piston back and forth on the piston pin. If any movement is felt, replace the piston and pin as a set.

  20. Remove piston rings.
    1. Using a piston ring expander, remove the 2 compression rings.
    2. Remove 2 side rails and the oil ring by hand.
      NOTE:
      • Be careful when removing the oil ring as it may break if you open the both ends of the ring excessively.

      Information 

      • Arrange the piston rings in the correct order.
  21. Disassemble the connecting rod from the piston.
    1. Remove the snap ring (A) from the piston.
    2. Remove the piston pin (B).
    3. Disassemble the piston (C) and connecting rod (D).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
