Heater pipe

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the ITM.
  2. Disconnect the heater hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the intake manifold.

    (Refer to Intake and Exhaust System - "INTAKE MANIFOLD ")

  4. Remove the heater pipe & hose (A).

    Tightening torque 

    19.6 - 23.5 N.m (2.0 - 2.4 kgf.m, 14.5 - 17.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Install in the reverse order of removal.
  6. Fill the radiator with coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE:
    • When changing the coolant and draining air, be sure to follow the filling instructions of the Integrated Thermal Management Module (ITM).
  7. Start engine and check for leaks.
