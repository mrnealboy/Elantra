Repair Procedures: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using a gasket scraper, remove all the gasket material from the top surface of the cylinder block.
  2. Using a soft brush and solvent, thoroughly clean the cylinder block.
  3. Inspect top surface of cylinder block for flatness.

    Using a precision straight edge and feeler gauge, measure the surface contacting the cylinder head gasket for warpage.

    Flatness of cylinder block gasket surface: 

    Standard:

    Less than 0.05 mm (0.0020 in.)/total area

    Less than 0.02 mm (0.0008 in.)/100 mm (3.9370 in.) X 100 mm (3.9370 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Using a cylinder bore gauge, measure the cylinder bore diameter at position in the thrust and axial direction.

    Standard: 

    81.00 - 81.03 mm (3.1890 - 3.1902 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Measuring direction: Axial direction, Thrust direction
    • Measuring height: From the top of the cylinder block 5 mm (0.5906 in.)/71.5 mm (2.8150 in.)/132.5 mm (5.2165 in.)
  5. Visually check the cylinder for vertical scratches.

    If deep scratches are present, replace the cylinder block.

  6. Check the cylinder bore size code on the cylinder block side surface.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
