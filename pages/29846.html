DOOR TRIM PANEL INSPECTION AND SERVICE GUIDE

DOOR TRIM PANEL INSPECTION AND SERVICE GUIDE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 23-BD-005H, Date of Issue:  March 14, 2023
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: BODY
Superceded Bulletin(s): 19-BD-011H

REISION HISTORY

This TSB supersedes 19-BD-011H by adding more photo examples. 

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin provides information to help identify certain types of cosmetic damage on the door trim panel(s) and determine if it is a warrantable condition.

Applicable Vehicles:  All

WARRANTY INFORMATION

Normal warranty applies.

SERVICE INFORMATION

CONDITION PHOTO REMARKS
Paint Peeling Flaking
Nature Codes: A25, A26, A42
Inspection Method:  Inspect surfaces for paint flaking/peeling
Cause: 
Material degradation
Warranty: 
Covered under warranty
Discoloration Contamination
Nature Codes: A41, A43
Inspection Method: 
Inspect surfaces for discoloration/contamination from foreign substances
Cause: 
Cosmetics, cleaning products, hand sanitizer, lotion/cream, etc.
Warranty: 
NOT  covered under warranty
Scratches Blemishes
Nature Code: A32
Inspection Method: 
Inspect surfaces for scratches/blemishes after  retail delivery
Cause: 
External damage
Warranty: 
NOT  covered under warranty
Material Pulling Apart
Nature Codes: A22, B12, B19
Inspection Method: 
Inspect surfaces for air pockets, detachments, or missing screws/welds
Cause: 
Manufacturing
Warranty: 
Covered under warranty
Wrinkles
Imperfections
Loose Seams
Protrusions
Nature Codes:
A33, A34
Inspection Method: 
Inspect surfaces for tears, punctures or imperfections after  retail delivery
Cause: 
External damage Normal wear and tear
Warranty: 
NOT  covered under warranty
Installation Gap
Wrong Position
Adjustment Error
Bending
Deformation
Distortion
Nature Codes:
A31, A36, A37, B14, B17, B18
Inspection Method: 
Inspect fastening hardware (clips, bolts, washers, etc.)
Cause: 
Manufacturing
Warranty: 
Covered under warranty
