Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Remove the cylinder head cover.

    (Refer to Cylinder Head Assembly - "CYLINDER HEAD COVER ")

  3. Turn the crankshaft damper pulley so that No. 1 piston is at top dead center.
    1. Turn the crankshaft damper pulley and align its groove with the timing mark on the timing chain cover
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Check the timing chain, be sure that the timing mark (A) of each sprocket is matched with the timing mark (B) (color link) of the timing chain.

      If not, turn the crankshaft damper pulley by one revolution (360°).

      [Intake CVVT] 

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

      [Exhaust CVVT 

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the intake and exhaust variable force solenoid (VFS) valves (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the cam to cam guide (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the fuel pump bracket (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the camshaft front bearing cap (A) and camshaft bearing caps (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the service hole bolt (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Install the jack to the edge of oil pan
  10. Lift the engine using the jack to obtain space.
  11. Release the lock by putting down ratchet plate (B) of timing chain tensioner with using back of tiny awl (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Remove the intake oil control valve (OCV) & center bolt (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • To prevent impurities from entering intake oil control valve (OCV) & center bolt, wear rubber gloves.
    • Intake oil control valve (OCV) and center bolt, hold the hexagonal portion on the camshaft with a wrench to prevent the camshaft from rotating.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Remove the exhaust oil control valve (OCV) and center bolt (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • To prevent impurities from entering exhaust oil control valve (OCV) & center bolt, wear rubber gloves.
    • When removing the exhaust oil control valve (OCV) and center bolt, hold the hexagonal portion on the camshaft with a wrench to prevent the camshaft from rotating.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the CVVD assembly.
    NOTE:
    • After removing the CVVD unit, make sure that the parts such as lifter, cam, etc. are not separated.
    • Ensure that the CVVD components are not damaged.

    When Using the CVVD fixture 

    1. Install the CVVD fixture (A) over the CVVD assembly.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Remove the CVVD assembly.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    When not using the CVVD fixture 

    1. Remove the CVVD assembly.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Remove the Intake CVVT (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Remove the exhaust camshaft (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  17. Remove the exhaust CVVT (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Fasten the timing chain to the engine hanger with a cable tie or strap not to allow the timing chain to fall down when removing the intake and exhaust CVVT & camshaft.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
