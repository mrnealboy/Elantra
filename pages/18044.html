Battery Sensor

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Battery sensor is connected to the (-) battery terminal. It sends battery voltage, current, temperature information to the Engine Control Module (ECM). Then, the ECM adjusts voltage by duty cycle based on these signals.
