Boost Pressure Sensor (BPS)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Boost Pressure Sensor (BPS) is located between turbocharger and intake manifold, and it senses inner pressure supercharged by the turbocharger. The engine Electronic Control Unit (ECU) determines a suitable fuel ratio in line with the pressure of intake manifold and intake air amount measured by Manifold Absolute Pressure Sensor (MAPS).
