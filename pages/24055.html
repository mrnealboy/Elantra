SST WARRANTY

  • GEN 3 BCT unit and component parts are covered by a 1 year warranty and serviced via BCT Repair Request Process. 
  • Warranty coverage of the GEN 3 BCT unit is extended throughout the certification period when it is renewed via the outlined BCT Certification Process. 
NOTE: The BCT requires connection to a known good air supply during use.
  • Shop air supply must consistently provide at least 80 PSI of air pressure to the tester.
  • Do NOT use a portable air compressor.
  • Be sure to check that the shop air water separator is functioning correctly.
  • Tool internal damage caused by water intrusion is not covered within any tool warranty.

The BCT requires a consistently stable 12V power supply for both calibration and testing
  • Vehicle battery condition can affect unit operation (MIN. Operating Voltage: 12.3V).
  • A commercially available external 12V power supply is recommended for all BCT usage.
  • BCT unit calibration failure is possible if the 12V supply is not adequate.

CALIBRATION PROCEDURE - GEN 3 (AUTO BCT)

  1. A calibration self-test will occur within the GDS Bearing Clearance Test (BCT) Process.
    • Connect test hose (with other end of the test hose disconnected), shop air supply hose, and power supply cable to a stable power source.
    • Confirm all connections, then turn main power switch ON.
    • Follow prompts as shown within the GDS Software.
    • BCT Inspection will not be allowed if the tool does not pass the calibration self-test.
    NOTE: GEN 3 unit disassembly is NOT required for calibration.
    If the GEN 3 unit is disassembled, it will void any applicable warranty.
  2. If the self-test fails  , GDS will open a calibration screen to allow for necessary adjustments.

    Three options within GDS screen: 

    1. AP Adjustment (tap the AP value)
    2. VC Adjustment (tap the VC value)
    3. Cannot Calibrate (Refer to Step  6)

    Tap each reading to obtain a live value. 

    • If the item is shown in red, adjustment of that value is necessary.
    • Adjust in order shown below.
  3. VC Adjustment: 
    1. Utilize the Calibration Adjustment Tool.
    2. Counterclockwise: decrease (-) value
    3. Clockwise: increase (+) value
    4. Turn in small increments  while monitoring the value reading (some delay is present between tool and GDS).
    NOTE: Adjustment Tip: Each adjustment affects the other, so switch between AP/VC to monitor and adjust accordingly.
  4. AP Adjustment: 
    1. Utilize Calibration Adjustment Tool.
    2. Adjust as needed in very slight turns
    3. Counterclockwise:  decreases (-) value
    4. Clockwise:  increases (+) value
    5. Turn in small increments  while monitoring the value reading (some delay is present between tool and GDS).
    NOTE: Adjustment Tip: Each adjustment affects the other, so switch between AP/VC to monitor and adjust accordingly.
  5. Completed Calibration: 
    1. Tap between AP/VC values to confirm the calibration status.
    2. If both values are shown in GREEN, then all calibrations are completed.
    3. Tap "Next" to continue to the BCT Inspection process.
  6. Cannot Calibrate: 
    • Refer to "BCT Basic Troubleshooting" section of this TSB.

CALIBRATION PROCEDURE - PREVIOUS GENERATIONS (MANUAL BCT)

  1. Remove the (2) smaller hex screws located on the upper side of the BCT unit cover.

    Hex Screw: 2.5mm hex 
  2. Remove the (4) larger hex screws located on all lower sides of the BCT unit cover.

    Hex Screw: 3.0mm hex 
  3. Carefully lift and remove the BCT unit cover.
  4. Verify that the power switch is OFF. 
  5. Follow the EXACT power cable and air hose connection order: 
    1. Connect the main air compressor hose.
    2. Connect the 12V power cable to a stable power source (a fully charged 12 Volt battery or an external AC to DC 13.8 Volt power supply). 
    3. Connect the test hose to the BCT unit but leave the other end of the test hose disconnected (do not connect the spark plug rod). 
  6. Turn the power switch ON  and select the AP/VC switch to VC.
    • AP: Pressure 
    • VC: Vacuum 
    NOTE: To verify power to main unit solenoids:
    • Toggle the AP/VC transfer switch between AP <> VC.
    • During toggling, a "click" noise will indicate the solenoids are active.
  7. [Dial Type]  Inspect the VC gauge and verify that the reading is between: -73 ~ -83kPa. 
    NOTE: A red overlay on the VC gauge will indicate the recommended specification.

    [Digital Type]  Inspect the VC gauge and verify that the reading is between:

    -.073 ~ -.083MPa. 

    NOTE: Digital Type gauge shares the same display to show AP and VC set points.

    If the VC gauge is within specification, proceed to Step   9 . 
  8. If the VC gauge is NOT  within specification, pull the VC knob upwards to activate it.
    • Twist the VC knob to increase (+)  or decrease (-)  the set point value until the reading is within specification.

    Clockwise:  Increases (+) set point

    Counterclockwise:  Decreases (-) set point

    • After twisting the knob until the VC reading is within specification, press the knob downwards to deactivate and lock the knob.
  9. With the power switch still ON  , select the AP/VC switch to AP  .

    AP: Pressure 

    VC: Vacuum 
  10. [Dial Type]  Inspect the AP gauge and verify that the reading is between: 0.1 ~ .13MPa. 
    NOTE: A blue overlay on the AP gauge will indicate the recommended specification.

    [Digital Type]  Inspect the AP gauge and verify that the reading is between:

    0.1 ~ .13MPa. 

    NOTE: Digital Type gauge shares the same display to show AP and VC set points.

    If the AP gauge is within specification, proceed to Step   12 . 
  11. If the AP gauge is NOT within specification, pull the AP knob upwards to activate it.
    • Twist the AP knob to increase  (+) or decrease  (-) the set point value until the reading is within specification.

    Clockwise:  Increases (+) set point

    Counterclockwise:  Decreases (-) set point

    • After twisting the knob until the AP reading is within specification, press the knob downwards to deactivate and lock the knob.
  12. Turn the power switch OFF. 
  13. Follow the EXACT power cable and air hose disconnection order: 
    1. Disconnect the 12V power cable.
    2. Disconnect the test hose.
    3. Disconnect the main air compressor hose.
  14. Reinstall all parts in reverse order of removal. Calibration is now complete.

Recommended Unit Set Up: 

Using a utility cart as a self-contained "Bearing Clearance Tool Workstation" is recommended.

  • A suitable cart can be a commercial available welding cart (as shown), or equivalent.
  • This will allow:
    • Overall ease of use within the dealership.
    • Tool longevity due to improved portability and handling during use and storage.
    • Help prevent damage to vehicles during inspections (tool placed on fenders, radiator support, etc.).

BCT BASIC TROUBLESHOOTING

  • Supply Voltage (Most Important): 
    1. Inspect power input at the BCT Unit.
      • 12.3 Volts minimium required for operation.
      • Inspect Power Supply Cable for possible issues (KQ231-2T104QQH).
    2. Poor Source Voltage (Vehicle battery weak)
      • AC/DC 13.8 Volt power supply with at least 2-amp output recommended.
    3. Source Voltage will affect BCT Unit calibration and overall operation. 

      Example: External Power Supply

  • Unit will not calibrate (Voltage supplied is good): 
    1. Each adjustment effects the other as adjusted.
    2. Slight delay between adjustment and GDS display is normal.
      • Recommend quarter turn or less and monitor GDS for change.
    3. Pressure set too high  - Loud air exhaust:
      • Adjust VC clockwise, in quarter turns.
    4. Pressure set too low  - No or little air exhaust:
      • Adjust VC counterclockwise, in quarter turns until air exhaust sound is noted and start process over.
    5. Over adjustment of either VC, AP or Both 
      • This will affect the ability for the BCT unit to switch between AP/VC.
      • Obtain baseline calibtation.
      • Adjust both VC and AP clockwise until resistance is felt.
      • Adjust each item as shown below (starting at 12:00 position).
        • VC 1.0 turn counterclockwise 
        • AP 3.5 turns counterclockwise 
      • Connect unit to GDS and start calibration process over.
    6. IF adjustment spins freely in either direction internal, damage may have occurred. 
    7. If tool will still not calibrate, refer to "Repair Process" section of this TSB.
    NOTE: Tools are preset to be as close to calibration as possible when shipped. Very slight turns in either direction is all that is needed when adjusting. Possible to damage the BCT Unit (BCT GEN 3) if an adjustment valve is turned to either direction too far. Damaged caused by over adjustment will not be covered under any applicable tool warranty.
  • Bluetooth will not connect 
    1. The Bluetooth lamp will blink blue when properlly connected.
    2. Verify when unit powered on that the Bluetooth lamp illuminates.
    3. If unit fails to connect, check the following items:
      • BCT unit is NOT connected to any GDS tablet.
      • Issue with the bluetooth antenna of the tablet.
      • VCI is not connected.
    4. If no issue found with external items and BCT unit is determined to have an issue, refer to the BCT Repair Request Process section.
  • Clearance Gauge will not power ON, or is erratic 
    1. Remove and inspect Clearance Gauge Battery Tray.
    2. Inspect for missing or broken connection tangs as shown in illustration.
    3. Refer to Repair Request Process/Component Parts Ordering.

BCT UPGRADE PROCESS

  • CORE IS REQUIRED  , when upgrading from GEN 1/GEN 2 BCT  >> to >> GEN 3 BCT. 
  • Complete the BCT Upgrade/Certication Request Form - Select Upgrade 
    • https://hyundaitechinfo.com Hyundaitechinfo.com > Tools & Equipment > Bearing Clearance Tool 
  • Email The completed form to the BCT Repair/Certification team at: bctrepair@hmausa.com 
  • Upgrade team will acknowlege the request and provide instructions as needed.
  • Expected turn around depends on shipping utilized by the dealer.
  • Dealer is responsible for shipping to and from the repair/certification center.
  • All sent units need to have a return label included to expidite the process.
  • Fees will apply for the upgrade process.
  • This process is subject to change.

BCT CERTIFICATION PROCESS

  • All GEN 3 BCT  units are required to be certified by HMA on a Bi-Annual basis.
    • Previous GEN 1/GEN 2 units will require upgrading for the certification.
  • Testing will not be allowed with units that are not certified or the certification has expired.
  • To request a tool certification:
    • Complete the Upgrade/Certification Request Form - Select Certification 
  • Email The completed form to the BCT Repair/Certification team at: bctrepair@hmausa.com 
  • Certification team will acknowlege request and provide instructions as needed.
  • Expected turn around depends on shipping utilized by the dealer.
  • Dealer is responsible for shipping to and from the repair/certification center.
  • All sent units need to have a return label included to expedite process.
  • Allow for enough processing time prior to the certification expiration date of the current BCT unit.
