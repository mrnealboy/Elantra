Repair Procedures: Removal

Information 

  • For DTC C125901/C125929/C125938/C129002, refer to "MOTOR DRIVEN POWER STEERING SYSTEM DIAGNOSIS ".
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) cable.
    WARNING:
    • Disconnect the battery negative cable from the battery and then wait for at least 3 minutes.
  2. Remove the fuse cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the grommet (A) and MDPS connector (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When removing the connector, separate it as follows.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the wiring after disconnecting the MDPS connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
