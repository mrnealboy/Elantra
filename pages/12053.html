Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  2. Remove the rear wheel and tire (A) from the rear hub.

    Tightening torque:  107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the rear wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the rear caliper.

    (Refer to Brake System - "REAR DISC BRAKE ")

  4. Disconnect the rear wheel speed sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the wheel speed sensor bracket (A) after loosening the mounting bolt.

    Tightening torque:  19.6 - 29.4 N.m (2.0 - 3.0 kgf.m, 14.5 - 21.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the trailing arm (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque:  98.1 - 117.7 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 72.3 - 86.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Support the jack in the cross member lower part.
  8. Loosen the cross member mounting bolt (A).

    Tightening torque:  117.7 - 137.3 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 86.8 - 101.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the rear lower arm from the rear shock absorber after loosening the bolt and nut (A).

    Tightening torque:  98.1 - 117.7 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 72.3 - 86.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Loosen the cross member mounting nuts (A).

    Tightening torque:  156.9 - 176.5 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 115.7 - 130.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Loosen the cross member mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque:  156.9 - 176.5 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 115.7 - 130.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Remove the rear muffler.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "MUFFLER ")

  13. Slowly lower the jack and remove the rear cross member (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the coil spring (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Remove the rear upper arm (A) after loosening the mounting bolts and nuts.

    Tightening torque 

    Cross member:  117.7 - 132.4 N.m (12.0 - 13.5 kgf.m, 86.8 - 97.6 lb-ft)

    Rear carrier:  137.3 - 156.9 N.m (14.0 - 16.0 kgf.m, 101.3 - 115.7 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Remove the trailing arm (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque:  137.3 - 156.9 N.m (14.0 - 16.0 kgf.m, 101.3 - 115.7 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  17. Remove the rear assist arm (A) after loosening the mounting bolt and nut.

    Tightening torque 

    Cross member:  127.5 - 137.3 N.m (13.0 - 14.0 kgf.m, 94.0 - 101.3 lb-ft)

    Rear carrier:  137.3 - 156.9 N.m (14.0 - 16.0 kgf.m, 101.3 - 115.7 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  18. Remove the rear carrier (A) from the rear lower arm after loosening the mounting bolt and nut.

    Tightening torque:  117.7 - 132.4 N.m (12.0 - 13.5 kgf.m, 86.8 - 97.6 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Remove the rear lower arm (A) after loosening the mounting bolts and nuts.

    Tightening torque: 

    Rear cross member:  127.5 - 137.3 N.m (13.0 - 14.0 kgf.m, 94.0 - 101.3 lb-ft)

    Rear stabilizer bar link:  58.8 - 68.6 N.m (6.0 - 7.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 50.6 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  20. Remove the rear stabilizer bar (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque:  44.1 - 53.9 N.m (4.5 - 5.5 kgf.m, 32.5 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
