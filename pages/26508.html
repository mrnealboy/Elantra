DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Rationality check
  1. Fuse
  2. Poor contact of connector
  3. Open in HO2S (Up) Heater Control Circuit
  4. HO2S (Up)
Case 1 Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery voltage between 10.7 and 16.1V
  • Heating time within 35 sec
  • Internal resistance signal normal
Threshold Value
  • The ceramic temperature is less than 720°C (1328°F). (diagnosed for 35 seconds after the start of heating)
Diagnostic Time
  • 3 sec
Case 2 Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery voltage is between 10.7 and 16.1V
  • EGT around upstream O2S (Model) is higher than 450°C (842°F)
  • Sensor heating power is over 90%
  • Sensor heating time is over 50 sec
  • No fuel cut-off over 50s
  • Internal resistance signal normal
Threshold Value
  • The ceramic temperature is less than 720°C (1328°F)
Diagnostic Time
  • 60 sec
Case 2 Prerequisite condition
  • Battery voltage is between 10.7 and 16.1V
  • EGT around upstream O2S (Model) is higher than 450°C (842°F)
  • Sensor heating time is over 50 sec
  • No fuel cut-off over 50s
  • Internal resistance signal normal
Threshold Value
  • The ceramic temperature is less than 639°C (1182°F)
Diagnostic Time
  • 10 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
