SEAT COVER INSPECTION AND SERVICE GUIDE

Reference Number(s): 23-BD-004H, Date of Issue:  April 18, 2023
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: BODY

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin provides information on how to evaluate and repair certain types of cosmetic damage on seat covers. This bulletin also includes examples to help determine if the cosmetic damage is a warrantable repair.

Applicable Vehicles:  All

WARRANTY INFORMATION

Normal warranty applies.

GENERAL INFORMATION

  • Vehicle seats are commonly upholstered with natural leather, thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) for artificial leather, or fabric materials.
  • These materials provide the best combination of comfort, breathability, flexibility, and touch, but have a natural tendency to stretch and expand with friction or from the weight of a passenger and develop wrinkles
  • Some seats may also be upholserted with a combination of different materials. For example, the side bolsters and side cushions may use a different material than the rest of the seat cushion and seat back.
  • Certain models and/or trim levels have higher side bolsters to provide better side support, which are more susceptible to developing wrinkles through normal use.
  • For natural leather seats, only the main seating surfaces (seat cushion and seat back) are upholstered with natural leather. However, the sides of the seat are upholstered with artificial leather (TPU) instead.

SERVICE PROCEDURE

Information:  Refer to the photos in the previous sections to determine the seat material and respective repair procedure for that seat material.

A. Natural/Nappa Leather Materials

  1. Check and identify all affected areas (A).
  2. Spray water on the affected area (A).
  3. Use a heat gun (B) to dry out the affected area (A) using a continuous sweeping motion.
    NOTE:
    • Set the temperature of the heat gun to 300°C or 570°F.
    • Keep the heat gun at least than 6 in. (150 mm) away from the seat surface.
    • Do not hold the heat gun in the same place for more than a few seconds.
    • Failure to follow the instructions above may cause permanent damage to the leather material from overheating.

B. Artificial Leather (TPU) OR Fabric Materials

  1. Check and identify all affected areas (A).
  2. Cover the affected area with a clean towel or cloth (C) and spray it with water until it is damp.
    NOTE: Ensure the towel/cloth is thoroughly damp to prevent damage to the seating material from excessive heat.
  3. Iron the damp towel or cloth (C).

    Information: If the towel or cloth (C) becomes dry while ironing, spray more water and continue ironing. 

    Do not iron any seating surfaces that are not covered by the towel. 

C. Piping Features on Side Cushions/Bolsters

  1. Check and identify all areas with affected piping features (D).
  2. Use the same ironing method with a damp towel or cloth from the previous page on the affected piping features.
  3. After ironing, the piping features will become more flexible.

    Bend the piping features back to their original shape and allow the affected area to cool for a few minutes afterwards.

    Information: If necessary, iron the piping features with a damp towel or cloth again if they become less flexible.
