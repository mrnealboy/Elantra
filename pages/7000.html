Catalytic Converter

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Catalytic Converter of the gasoline engine is equipped with a three-way catalyst. It oxidizes carbon monoxide and hydrocarbons (HC), and separates oxygen from the oxides of nitrogen (NOx).
