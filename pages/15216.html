DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the supply voltage
  1. Open/short in power circuit
  2. Open in ground circuit
  3. Overcharging
Prerequisite Condition
  • Start the engine
Threshold Value
  • Battery voltage is greater than 16V
Diagnosis Time
  • The problem persists over 400 ms
