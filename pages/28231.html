Head lamps

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the front bumper cover.

    (Refer to Body - "FRONT BUMPER COVER ")

  3. Disconnect the head lamp connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting bolts and remove the head lamp assembly (A).
    NOTE:
    • Take care that holding clip (B) is not to be damaged.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
