Low Fuel Line

  1. Release the residual pressure in fuel line.

    (Refer to "RELEASE RESIDUAL PRESSURE IN FUEL LINE ")

  2. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal.
  3. Remove the air cleaner assembly.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  4. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - Battery - "REMOVAL , and BATTERY TRAY ")

  5. Disconnect the fuel feed tube quick-connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Open the clamp cover (A) before disconnecting the quick-connector. (If the clip is applied)
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • When removing the quick-connnector with the clip removing tool (A), be careful not to damage the plastic clip (B).
    • If the clip is damaged, fuel line leakage may occur due to poor connection.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Disconnect the vapor quick-connector (A) which is connected from the PCSV.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the bracket (A) after loosening the nut.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Lift the vehicle.
  9. Remove the fuel tank.

    (Refer to "FUEL TANK ")

  10. Remove the side under cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Remove the hit protector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Remove the fuel and brake line protector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Disconnect the vapor quick-connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the fuel line fixing clip (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
