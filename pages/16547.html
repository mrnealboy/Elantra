Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery (-) terminal.
  2. Release the residual pressure in fuel line.

    (Refer to Fuel Delivery System - "RELEASE RESIDUAL PRESSURE IN FUEL LINE ")

  3. Remove the rear seat.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "REAR SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  4. Remove the fuel pump service cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the fuel pump connector (A).
  6. Disconnect the fuel feed tube quick-connector (B).
  7. Disconnect the fuel tank pressure sensor connector (C).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Lift the vehicle.
  9. Separate the fuel filler hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Disconnect the vapor hose quick-connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Remove the floor under cover (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Separate the brake line after removing the bracket mounting bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Remove the fuel tank band (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 54.0 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.9 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Support the jack to avoid damaging the fuel tank.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the fuel tank (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
