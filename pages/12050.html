Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.
  2. Remove the rear stabilizer bar link after loosening the mounting nut (A).

    Tightening torque:  58.8 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.9 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • When loosening the nut, fix the outer hexagon of stabilizer bar link.
    • Be careful not to damage the stabilizer link boots.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the rear stabilizer bar (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque:  44.1 - 53.9 N.m (4.5 - 5.5 kgf.m, 32.5 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the rear stabilizer bar link (A) from the rear lower arm after loosening the mounting bolt and nut.

    Tightening torque:  58.8 - 68.6 N.m (6.0 - 7.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 50.6 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • When loosening the nut, fix the outer hexagon of stabilizer bar link.
    • Be careful not to damage the stabilizer link boots.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
