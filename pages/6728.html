Repair Procedures: Inspection

  1. Connect a diagnostic tool on Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Check MAPS output voltage at idle and IG ON.
    Condition Output Voltage (V)
    IG ON 3.9 - 4.1
    Idle 0.8 - 1.6
