Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedures.
    CAUTION:
    • New shift lever assembly is mounted in the 4th fixing pin.
    • After installation, remove the 4th fixed pin.
    • Check operating of lever of transaxle side when operating shift lever after assembly. (1~6, R)
    • If the jamming occurs at each stage when changing gears, readjust the cable length. (Refer to Manual Transaxle System - "CONTROL CABLE ")
