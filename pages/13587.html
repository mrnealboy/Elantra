DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the CAN communication
  • Faulty Power Supply
  • Open/Poor Connection in "BATTERY SENSOR"'s Signal Circuit
  • Short in "BATTERY SENSOR"'s Signal Circuit
  • "BATTERY SENSOR"'s Signal Circuit protocol error by electrical noise
  • Faulty "BATTERY SENSOR"
  • Faulty ICU*
Prerequisite Condition
  • Power voltage: Between 9V and 16V
  • P-CAN communication: Active
Threshold Value Time-out
  • "BATTERY SENSOR" data from ICU*: Not received
Message error
  • "BATTERY SENSOR" data from ICU*: Abnormal
Diagnostic Time
  • Continuously for more than 2 sec.
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle*
Driving Cycle*: DC 
  • When [IG ON→Engine ON→IG ON] is performed, complete the Driving Cycle* 1 time (1DC).
  • "MIL" means If a fault during the 1st DC is still detected in the 2nd DC, DTC is confirmed and the ECM* outputs "MIL ON".
