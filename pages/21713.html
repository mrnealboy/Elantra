Integrated thermal management module (ITM)

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Integrated thermal management module (ITM) has purpose to control coolant flow by the temperature of coolant. Integrated thermal management module (ITM) helps fast warm-up by controlling valve to flow coolant only in engine when the first engine start, and helps to improve fuel efficiency through fast warm-up.

Integrated thermal management module (ITM) controls coolant temperature by controlling coolant flow into radiator or heater through controlling valve when the coolant temperature goes higher.
