Seat Heater Unit Diagnosis Mode

  1. You can enter the diagnosis mode by turning the seat heater button on.
  2. You can enter the diagnosis mode by referring to following description.
    • Push IGN ON while pressing the seat heater button from IGN OFF.
    • Keep pressing IGN ON for more than three seconds before pressing it once again for more than three seconds within 2 seconds.
    • All LED indicators on the driver and passenger seat button will blink 1 times.
  3. After entering the diagnosis mode, you can check what failed by checking the blinking LED.

  4. You can check the malfunctioning by checking the blinking LED.
  5. Pressing the IGN UFF button will end the diagnosis mode for the heater seat.
  6. You can check whether the heating seat system works properly after turning the IGN UN. If you want to check the error code, you can refer to the procedure of 2 above.
