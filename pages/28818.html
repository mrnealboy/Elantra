DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the current of the left, outside mirror
  1. Open/short to battery in left side outside mirror
Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery voltage is normal
  • 1.5 sec. after turning the ignition switch on
  • Turn on the BSD or RCTA switch
  • Turn on the left side indicator ON
Threshold Value
  • There is an open/short to battery in the left side indicator circuit
Diagnostic Time
  • After 1 sec.
