Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the photo sensor cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the cluster fascia panel upper garnish (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting screw, remove the cluster fascia side panel (A).

    [4.2" TFT LCD] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [10.25" TFT cluster] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [N Line] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Press the lock pin, separate the cluster fascia side panel connector (A). [N Line]
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector are connected in properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
