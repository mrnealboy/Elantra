OCS Reset procedure

You should perform OCS Reset procedure after service or replacement about all part of the passenger seat. (Not included full seat assembly)

  1. Ignition "OFF", connect GDS.
  2. Ignition "ON" & Engine "OFF", select S/W Management and "Car model".
  3. Select OCS and "Body Sensor Reset".
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Perform the "Body Sensor Reset" procedure according to the GDS screen message.
