Cleaner Element Replacement

  1. Open the air cleaner cover by unhooking the clamps (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Replace the air cleaner element (A) with a new one.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • If there are foreign substances inside the air cleaner assembly, wipe the inside of the air cleaner assembly and then replace the air cleaner element.
  3. Close the air cleaner cover by hooking the clamps.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Install the air cleaner cover, insert the hinge (A) and fill the clamp.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
