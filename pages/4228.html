Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. If it is necessary to remove Multifunction Switch Assembly, remove the steering wheel.

    (Refer to Multifunction Switch Assembly - "REPAIR PROCEDURES ", and Steering Wheel - "REMOVAL ")

  3. Remove the steering column upper shrouds.

    (Refer to Body - "STEERING COLUMN SHROUD PANEL ")

  4. Disconnect the clock spring connector (A) and multifunction switch connector (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the clock spring (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the clamp (A) and remove the multifunction switch assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
