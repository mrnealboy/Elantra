GDS data Analysis

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn ignition switch "ON".
  3. Check the "Current Data" about related DTC with GDS.

    Specification  : When hitting the switch, TCS/ESP OFF warning lamp will respond and ESP OFF lamp will be ON.
