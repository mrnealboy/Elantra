Repair Procedures: Removal

CAUTION:
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Use seat covers to avoid damaging any surfaces.
  1. Remove the roof side molding. (Refer to Body Side Moldings - "ROOF SIDE MOLDING ")
  2. Remove the front pillar trim. (Refer to Interior Trim - "FRONT PILLAR TRIM ")
  3. Remove the inside rear view mirror assembly. (Refer to Mirror - "INSIDE REAR VIEW MIRROR ")
  4. Remove the cowl top cover. (Refer to "COWL TOP COVER ")
  5. Remove the A pillar garnish. (Refer to Body Side Molding - "A Pillar Garnish")
  6. Cut off the sealant on the edge (B) while pulling the piano wire (A) to the left and right.
    CAUTION:
    • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
    • Heat may occur when working on the wires. Be careful not to burn your hands.
    • Be careful not to disconnect the piano wire.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Put in a piano wire (A) through the corner of the windshield glass and insert wire grips into both ends of the wire.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Cut off the sealant on (A-A), (B-B), (C-C), while pulling a piano wire (A) to the left and right.
    CAUTION:
    • Put on gloves to protect your hands.
    • Heat may occur when working on the wires. Be careful not to burn your hands.
    • Be careful not to disconnect the piano wire.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the windshield glass (B) carefully using the glass holder (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
