Intercooler outlet pipe

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  3. Remove the intercooler outlet hose and pipe.
    1. Disconnect the intercooler outlet hose (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

      Information 

      1. When disconnecting the intercooler hose, pull the quick connector clamp (A) in the direction of the arrow as below.
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      2. Move the quick connector clamp from B to C position as below and then disconnect by pulling the quick connector hose.
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Loosen the intercooler outlet pipe bolt, and then remove the intercooler outlet hose and pipe (A).

      Tightening torque: 

      Bracket bolt: 6.86 - 10.78 N.m (0.7 - 1.1 kgf.m, 5.06 - 7.96 lb-ft)

      Clamp bolt: 4.90 - 6.86 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.62 - 5.06 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install in the reverse order of removal.
