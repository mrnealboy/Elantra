DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible cause
Main action
  • Monitor ECU supply voltage
  1. Poor connection in power supply circuit (IGN+)
  2. Open or short to ground in power supply circuit (ABS)
  3. Faulty Alternator
  4. Battery discharge
  5. Faulty ESP (ESC)
  6. Faulty Battery
Threshold value Case 1
  • When the Ignition voltage supplied to the ECU is lower than 9.5 V is continued for 500 milliseconds during vehicle speed is faster than 7 km/h (4.4 MPH). ABS/ESP control is prohibited.
    • If ignition voltage is recovered to normal operating voltage, the system recovers to normal state.
Case 2
  • When the Ignition voltage supplied to the ECU is lower than 9 V is continued for 500 milliseconds
    • If ignition voltage is recovered to normal operating voltage, the system recovers to normal state.
Case 3
  • When the ignition voltage supplied to the ECU is lower than 8V is continued for 500 milliseconds during vehicle speed is faster than 7 km/h (4.4 MPH).
    • If ignition voltage is recovered to normal operating voltage, the system recovers to normal state.
MIL
  • Case 1, Case 2: ABS/ESP (ESC) warning lamps are activated
  • Case 3: EBD/ABS/ESP (ESC) warning lamps are activated
Reset condition
  • Return to normal battery voltage
