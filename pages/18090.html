DTC Description

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The deviation of the camshaft position from the target point is evaluated during stable driving condition. The PCM accumulates this deviation for a certain period and sets DTC P000B00 when the accumulated deviation is too slow response. The target camshaft position is predetermined value depending on engine speed and throttle angle in the PCM.
