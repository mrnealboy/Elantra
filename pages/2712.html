Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the cover (A) & (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the front view camera unit connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Separate the fixed points (A) of coupler.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the front view camera (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
