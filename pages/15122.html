DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the CAN data
  1. Open/Poor Connection in B-CAN High line
  2. Open/Poor Connection in B-CAN Low line
  3. Short both B-CAN High line and Low line coincidentally
Prerequisite Condition
  • Power Voltage: Between 9V and 16V
  • B-CAN communication: Active
Threshold Value Case 1
  • B-CAN "High" line error
Case 2
  • B-CAN "Low" line error
Diagnosis Time
  • 2 sec.
Component Name_ETM ETM
PSM* "DRIVER IMS CONTROL MODULE" SD200-6
