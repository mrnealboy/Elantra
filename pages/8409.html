DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Electrical inspection
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. TPS 1, TPS 2
Prerequisite Condition
  • Engine speed is higher than 320 RPM
Threshold Value
  • Sensor value difference between TPS 1 and TPS 2 is greater than 6.3° (defect in sensor signals)
Diagnostic Time
  • 0.28 sec
MIL
  • 1st Driving Cycle
