DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Electrical inspection
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Short to ground in control circuit
  3. Injector
Prerequisite Condition
  • The battery voltage is between 9V and 16V
  • The engine revolution is greater than 80 RPM
Threshold Value
  • Circuit low
Diagnostic Time
  • Continuous
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
