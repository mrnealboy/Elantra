Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat-tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the bumper cover and panels.
  1. Loosen the front bumper upper mounting clips.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Loosen the front bumper lower mounting clips.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. After loosening the pin-type retainers and screws on the side of front bumper (A), detach the side part of front bumper.
    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the tabs on the side part of front bumper (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the front bumper main connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the front bumper assembly (A).

    [General type] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [N Line] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector is connected properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
