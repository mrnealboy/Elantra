Repair Procedures: Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Install the retainer (B) and then tighten the nut (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    11.8 - 14.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.5 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Install the shift cable socket (B) and then fix the snap pin (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Before installing, check that the shift lever and manual control lever are placed in the "N" position.
    • When assembling shift cable, the projection (A) must face upwards.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • When assembling the snap pin, be careful about the correct direction.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install the air duct (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ")

  5. Install the shift lever knob & boots (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Align the hole in the manual control lever with the "N" position hole of the inhibitor switch and then insert the inhibitor switch guide pin (SST No.: 09480 - A3800) (A) in the matched hole.
  7. Push shift cable (B) lightly to "F" direction shown to eliminate free play of shift cable.
  8. Tighten the nut (C) with the specified torque.

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.0 - 1.4 kgf.m, 7.2 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the inhibitor switch guide pin (SST No.: 09480-A3800) from the hole.
  10. Install the battery and tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  11. Install the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

    CAUTION:
    • Check that operating surely at each range of the inhibitor switch corresponding to each position of shift lever.
