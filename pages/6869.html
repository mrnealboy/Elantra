Repair Procedures: Installation

NOTE:
  • When installing the CVVD actuator the shaft gear do not fit properly, rotate the CVVD actuator in both directions and installing it naturally.

    (Do not impact when installing the component.)

  • Do not damage to the component when installating the cylinder head cover oil seal (A).
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Information 

  • Install the component with the specified torques.
  • Note that internal damage may occur when the component is dropped. If the component has been dropped, inspect before installing.
