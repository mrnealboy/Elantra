DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible cause
Main action
  • Monitor the state of SAS
  1. Steering Angle Sensor is not calibrated
  2. Faulty SAS
  3. Faulty ESP
Threshold value Case 1
  • In case of straight and stable driving over 30 km/h (18.6 MPH), a failure is detected if it is more than 25° to difference between previous SAS offset and mean of estimated SAS offset.
Case 2
  • In case of over 60 km/h (37.3 MPH) driving, monitoring a fault if conditions between estimated SAS values and measured SAS sensor value are not proper to driving status.
  • In case of there is no change in the SAS value when the vehicle is turning left or right (Yaw rate ± 5 deg./s or more).
MIL
  • ESP (ESC) warning lamp is activated
Reset condition Case 1
  • After ignition switch turned OFF and ON.
Case 2
  • When the followings are normal, on a driving above 12 km/h (7.45 MPH).
    1. In case of straight driving, SAS signal is within 25 degree.
    2. SAS signal on right/left turn is larger than 30 degree.
    3. When the vehicle is running to left or right more than 12 degrees of SAS, the measured SAS sensor value is between the lower limit 50% and the upper limit 150% of the estimated SAS value over a certain period of time.
