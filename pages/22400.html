Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Drain engine oil.

    (Refer to "ENGINE OIL ")

  2. Remove the service cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Using SST (09215-3C000), remove the oil pan (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Insert the SST between the oil pan and the bedplate by tapping it with a plastic hammer in the direction of arrow.
    • After tapping the SST with a plastic hammer along the direction of arrow around more than 2/3 edge of the oil pan, remove it from the bedplate.
    • Do not turn over the SST abruptly without tapping. It is result in damage of the SST.
    • Be careful not to damage the contact surfaces of the bedplate and the oil pan.
