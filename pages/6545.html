Transaxle mounting bracket

  1. Disconnect the negative battery terminal.
  2. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  3. Remove the air duct and air cleaner assembly.

    (Refer to Intake and Exhaust System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  4. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "REMOVAL , and BATTERY TRAY ")

  5. Place the jack under the transaxle.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Insert the rubber block between jack and transaxle.
  6. Remove the service cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Loosen the transaxle support bracket mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque:  88.3 - 107.9 N.m (9.0 - 11.0 kgf.m, 65.1 - 79.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the transaxle mounting bracket (A).

    Tightening torque:  49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Installation is in the reverse order of removal.

Roll rod bracket 

  1. Remove the roll rod bracket (A).

    Tightening torque:  Bolt (B): 107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    Bolts (C): 49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Remove the roll rod support bracket (A).

    Tightening torque:  49.0 - 68.6 N.m (5.0 - 7.0 kgf.m, 36.2 - 50.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Installation is in the reverse order of removal.
