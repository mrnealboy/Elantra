Monitor GDS Data

  1. Ignition switch OFF, connect GDS.
  2. Ignition switch ON and the engine OFF, select "Current Data" mode.
  3. Monitor the "Driver CAB resistance" parameter on the GDS.
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Reference  : Resistance of "Driver CAB" is between 1.0Ω and 6.9Ω

Reference  : In a case of an open in the "Driver CAB" circuit: FAIL

In a case of a short to battery in the "Driver CAB" circuit: FAIL

In a case of a short to ground in the "Driver CAB" circuit: FAIL
