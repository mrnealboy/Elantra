Repair Procedures: Inspection: Camshaft

  1. Measure the height of the cam.

    Check the surface of the cam lob for wear and tear. If necessary, replace the camshaft.

    Cam height 

    Intake: 37.33 mm (1.4697 in.)

    Exhaust: 37.44 mm (1.474 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Inspect the camshaft journal clearance.
    1. Clean the camshaft journals and bearing caps.
    2. Place the camshafts on the cam carrier.
    3. Lay a strip of plastigage across each of the camshaft journals.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Install the camshaft front bearing cap (A) and camshaft bearing caps (B).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      1. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

        Tightening torque: 

        4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

        Fig 1: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts Tightening Sequence
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      2. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

        Tightening torque: 

        4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      3. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

        Tightening torque: 

        4.9 - 6.9 N.m (0.5 - 0.7 kgf.m, 3.6 - 5.1 lb-ft)

        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      4. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

        Tightening torque: 

        9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

        Fig 2: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      5. Install the camshaft front bearing cap and camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

        Tightening torque: 

        18.6 - 22.6 N.m (1.9 - 2.3 kgf.m, 13.7 - 16.6 lb-ft)

        Fig 3: Camshaft Front Bearing Caps Bolts (Tightening Sequence)
        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      6. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

        Tightening torque: 

        11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)

        Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      7. Install the camshaft bearing cap installing bolt with direction from back of engine to the front of engine according to the procedure at down below.

        Tightening torque: 

        11.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.2 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.7 - 10.1 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    5. Remove the camshaft bearing caps.
    6. Measure the plastigage at the widest point.

      If the oil clearance is greater than the specification, replace the camshaft.

      If necessary, replace the bearing caps and cam carrier.

      Cam height 

      No. 1: 0.040 - 0.071 mm (0.0016 - 0.0028 in.)

      No. 2, 3, 4, 5: 0.035 - 0.065 mm (0.0014 - 0.0026 in.)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    7. Remove the plastigage.
    8. Remove the camshafts.
  3. Inspect the camshaft end play.
    1. Install the camshafts.
    2. Measure the end play while moving the camshaft back and forth using a dial gauge.

      Camshaft end play 

      Standard value: 0.10 - 0.19 mm (0.0039 - 0.0075 in.)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

      If the end play is greater than the specification, replace the camshaft. If necessary, replace the cylinder head.
