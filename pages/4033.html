DTC Description

B262500 is set when the Cockpit Mood Lamp 1* cannot transmit the LIN data or transmits the abnormal LIN data to the MLM*. It can occur when there is a Faulty Power Supply, or an Open/Poor Connection, or a Short in the LIN Circuit.
