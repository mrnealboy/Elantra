Passenger Side airbag (PSAB)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Side Airbag (hereinafter referred to SAB) located in the driver and passenger seat protects passenger's head and shoulder. The SAB is consist of an airbag and an inflator.

Airbag reduces impact of collision by fillied up gas.

An Inflator keeps gas and uses it to deploy airbag on collision.

CAUTION: Never measure the resistance of the SAB directly, Current of measuring the device may cause unexpected air bag deploy.
