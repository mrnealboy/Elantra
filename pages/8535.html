DTC Description

When all prerequisite conditions are met, the engine ECU will begin monitoring the catalytic converter by comparing the values of the front and rear oxygen sensors mounted at the front and rear of the catalytic converter. P042000 is set when the sum of the accumulated monitoring results exceed a threshold.
