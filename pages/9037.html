YES 

  • Check the P-CAN communication circuit terminal and the wire for corrosion, contamination, deformation, damage, and short to battery.

NO 

  • If the measured voltage is the battery voltage level (approximately 12V), check for short between the relevant wire and the battery power.
  • If the measured voltage is the ground level (approximately 0V).

→ Check for short between the "P-CAN High" line and the "P-CAN Low" line.

→ Check the relevant wire for open and poor connection.

→ Check for short between the relevant wire and the body ground.
