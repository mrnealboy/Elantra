Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
    NOTE:
    • Before installing the pin, apply the grease to the joint pin.
    • Use a new snap pin whenever installing.
  2. Refill the coolant.[G 1.6 T-GDI] 

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "COOLANT ")

  3. After installation, bleed the brake system.

    (Refer to Brake System - "BRAKE BLEEDING PROCEDURES ")

  4. Check the brake oil leakage and pedal operating condition.
