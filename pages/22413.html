TCM adaptive values learning procedure

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. P range learning (learning condition: Intelligent Variable Transmission Fluid (IVTF) temperature: 30 - 90°C (86 - 194° F), shift lever P range, brake off)
    1. Start the engine after checking for the IVTF temperature in state of IG ON.
    2. Engine RPM will rise to approximately 1000 RPM after starting the engine.
    3. After 30 seconds to 2 minutes, the learning is completed when the engine RPM reaches the IDLE level.

      (Learning completion can be confirmed from the "Sensor Data" of diagnostic tool)

      NOTE:
      • Do not operate the shift lever, step on the accelerator pedal or brakes during P-step learning.

        (If it is operated during learning, perform it again after turning off the position switch.)
