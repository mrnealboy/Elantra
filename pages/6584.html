Start/Stop Button (SSB)

A single stage push button is used for the driver to operate the vehicle. Pressing this button allows:

  • To activate the power modes 'Off, 'Accessory', 'Ignition' and 'Start' by switching the corresponding terminals
  • To start the engine
  • To stop the engine

The contact will be insured by a micro-switch and a back lighting is provided to highlight the marking of the button whenever necessary.

Three (3) LED colors are located in the outside ring of the switch assembly. They display the status of the system.

They are OFF (White)/ACC (Amber)/ON (Green).
