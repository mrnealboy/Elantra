    No Item Fault Fail Safe
    1 Incar sensor Open or Short Control with the value of 25°C (77.0°F)

    4 Mode door potentiometer Open or Short Fix vent position, while selecting vent mode.
    Fix defrost position, while selecting all except vent mode.
    5 Temperature door potentiometer (MTC) Open or Short If temperature setting 1 - 7 step, fix at maximum cooling position.
    If temperature setting 8 - 15 step, fix at maximum heating position.
    6 Temperature door potentiometer (DATC) Open or Short If temperature setting 17°C (62.6°F) - 24.5°C (76.1° F), fix at maximum cooling position.
    If temperature setting 25°C (77°F) - 32°C (89.6°F), fix at maximum heating position.
