Removal and Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the cooling fan.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLING FAN ")

  2. Drain the engine coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  3. Disconnect the radiator upper hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the radiator lower hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the front bumper cover assembly.

    (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "FRONT BUMPER ")

  6. Remove the radiator upper air guard (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the radiator side air guard (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the radiator mounting bracket (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Pull the radiator (A) upward and remove it from engine room.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Install in the reverse order of removal.
  11. Fill the radiator with coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE:
    • When changing the coolant and draining air, be sure to follow the filling instructions of the Integrated Thermal Management Module (ITM).
  12. Start engine and check for leaks.
