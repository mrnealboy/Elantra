Fail-safe Mode

Items Fail-safe
ETC Motor Throttle valve stuck at 7°
TPS TPS 1 fault Replace it with TPS2
TPS 2 fault Replace it with TPS1
TPS 1, 2 fault Throttle valve stuck at 9.2°
NOTE:
  • When throttle value is stuck at 9.2°, engine speed is limited at below 1, 200 - 1700 RPM.
