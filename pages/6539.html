Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  2. Remove the drive belt.

    (Refer to "DRIVE BELT ")

  3. Remove the crankshaft damper pulley (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    66.7 - 70.6 N.m + 98 - 102°[6.8 - 7.2 kgf.m + 98 - 102°, 49.2 - 52.1 lb-ft + 98 - 102°]

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Fix the ring gears using the SST (09231-2B100, 09231-3N100) after removing the starter. Then remove the crankshaft damper pulley.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install in the reverse order of removal.
