Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  2. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - Battery - "REMOVAL , and BATTERY TRAY ")

  3. Loosen the bolt and then separate the engine wiring (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the back up lamp switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the control cable bracket and control cable at the same time.
    1. Remove the snap pin (A)
    2. Separate the shift cable (B) and select cable (C).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Loosen the mounting bolts and then removing the control cable and control cable bracket (A) at the same time.

      Tightening torque: 

      14.7 - 21.6 N.m (1.5 - 2.2 kgf.m, 10.8 - 15.9 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the bolt and then separate the ground line (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the snap pin (A) and the clutch hose (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the starter motor mounting bolts (A) and the transaxle upper mounting bolts (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)
    2. 42.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.3-5.5 kgf.m, 31.1 - 39.8 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Assemble the engine support fixture use a (Beam SST No.: 09200 - 3N000, Adapter SST No.: 09200-4X000, Engine fixture adapter (rear) SST No.: 09200-L1100, Engine fixture adapter (front) SST No.: 09200-L1200.
  10. Using the engine support fixture (A), hold the engine and transaxle assembly safely.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Remove the dust cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Loosen the transaxle bracket support mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0-13.0 kgf.m, 79.6-94.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Loosen the bolts and then removing the transaxle bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    58.8 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.9 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the under cover.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  15. After loosening the drain plug (A), drain the manual transaxle fluid, then install the drain plug.

    Tightening torque: 

    58.8 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.9 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • The existing oil drain plug gasket (A) must be replaced with a new one. (Do not reuse it.)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Loosen the bolts and then removing the wiring bracket (A, B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  17. Loosen the bolts (A, B) and then removing the roll rod assembly (C).

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)
    2. 107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6-94.0 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  18. Loosen the bolts and then removing the roll rod support bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    49.0 - 68.6 N.m (5.0 - 7.0 kgf.m, 36.2 - 50.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Remove the drive shaft assembly.

    (Refer to Driveshaft and axle (2WD-FF) - "FRONT DRIVESHAFT ")

  20. Remove the lower mounting bolts (A), (B) of lower part of the transaxle, and the left side cover and remove the transaxle assembly by supporting it with a jack.

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 42.2 - 48.1 N.m (4.3-4.9 kgf.m, 31.1 - 35.4 lb-ft)
    2. 42.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.3 - 5.5 kgf.m, 31.1 - 39.8 lb-ft)
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to damage other system or parts near by when removing the engine and transaxle assembly.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
