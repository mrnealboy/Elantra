Actuation Test

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn the ignition switch ON and warm up the vehicle up to an operational temperature.
  3. Select "Intake Potentiometer" parameter on the "Current Data" of the GDS.
  4. Select "Actuation Test" for "Intake Door Selection - Recirc/Fresh" in order.
  5. During an actuation test, check whether the value of the potentiometer changes.

    Specification  : Recirc - About 90%, Fresh Air - About 10%
