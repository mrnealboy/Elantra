AUTO Defog Humidity Sensor

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The auto defog sensor is mounted on the front glass. It monitors the humidity of the glass surface and sends signals to the A/C control module. Then the module curbs and eliminates the moisture in advance by controlling the intake actuator, A/C, auto defog actuator (Auxiliary DEF actuator), blower motor, and mode actuator.
