Removal and Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the automatic transaxle.

    (Refer to Automatic Transaxle System - "REPAIR PROCEDURES ")

  2. Remove the drive plate (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    122.6 - 132.4 N.m (12.5 - 13.5 kgf.m, 90.4 - 97.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Always use a new drive plate mounting bolts.
  3. Install in the reverse order of removal.
