Conditions for Prohibiting the Reproduction

Prohibit the reproduction in case of falling on any of following conditions and stop controlling during reproduction action.

  1. IGN2 OFF (Excluding within 30 seconds after door opening or closing.)
  2. Higher than 3 km/h of vehicle speed
  3. When the switch is controlled manually
  4. Operation time chart
