DTC Description

The BCM/IBU (TPMS Module integrated) sets DTC C161100 when vehicle speed signal has not been received through the CAN signal from Engine ECM for 2.5 seconds.

* Body Control Module (BCM)

* Integrated Body Control Module (IBU)
