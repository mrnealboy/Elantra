DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the BCW CAN messages.
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Open/short in CAN communication circuit
  3. BCW module
Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery voltage is normal
  • 1.5 sec. after turning the ignition switch on
  • Engine is running
  • Turn on the BCW switch
  • CAN Bus-off DTC (C1616) is not detected
Threshold Value
  • The software version of the BCW RH and LH sensors does not match
Diagnostic Time
  • 0~60 sec. (varying by malfunction type)
