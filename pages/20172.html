GDS data Analysis

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn ignition switch "ON".
  3. Monitor the "Steering Angle Sensor" parameter on the "Current Data" menu of the GDS.

    Specification  : If SAS calibrated is yes, it is normal.
