Maintenance System Reminder Reset - Procedure 05

Always reset the remaining engine oil life whenever the engine oil is changed. Otherwise, the indication of remaining Oil life in the Oil Life Management System may not be accurate.

To reset the Oil Change Reminder, select Setup  > Vehicle  > Convenience  > Oil Change Reminder  > Reset  from the infotainment system. Then, select 'Yes  ' when the message 'Has the engine oil changed? Press [Yes] to reset the oil life.  ' appears on the screen.

If there is no alert until the maximum maintenance interval, have the vehicle inspected by an authorized HYUDNAI dealer.
