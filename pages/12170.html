Monitor GDS data

  1. Connect the GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Drive the vehicle at approx. 35 Km/h and higher longer than 10 min.
  3. Stop the vehicle and check the "Current Data" while the ignition is still ON.

    (If the ignition switch is turned off after driving, the current data stored in the BCM (IBU) will be cleared.)
