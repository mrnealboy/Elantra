SERVICE PROCEDURE

STUI 

This TSB includes Repair validation photos. Refer to the latest Warranty Digital Documentation Policy for requirements.

This campaign contains software update procedures for 2 different audio systems. Please refer to the correct section for your particular vehicle.

NOTE: Ensure the 12 volt battery is sufficiently charged and power is ON  to prevent battery drain during the update.

Information 

Before performing the update, verify that the AVN system is genuine Hyundai equipment.

SOFTWARE UPDATE FOR DISPLAY AUDIO 2.0V

  1. Turn ON  the ignition switch.
  2. Press Setup  (A) to enter the Setup menu.
  3. Press General  (B).
  4. Insert the USB flash drive into the USB slot (C).
  5. Press Update  (D) at the bottom of the screen to proceed with the software update.
    NOTE: Do NOT  remove the USB flash drive or turn power OFF  during the update. This may cause data loss or system failure.
  6. Select Yes  (E) on the pop-up.

    The update will process automatically, and will take approximately 15 minutes to complete.

    Information 

    There will be multiple occurrences where the Hyundai logo will appear, or the display will be black during the update process. This is normal.

    When the update has completed, the image shown to the right appears, and the system will automatically reboot.
  7. After the update has completed, confirm the Software Version  (F) has been correctly updated by referring to the SW Info/Update  screen.
    • Refer to Software Versions for Display Audio 2.0V  on page 8 for a list of updated software versions by model.

  8. STUI 

    Take a photo of the 'SW Info/Update' screen showing the updated S/W version with the last 6 digits of the VIN and date of repair on a piece of paper. Put post-it/piece of paper in top right-hand corner for photo. 

    Upload the photo to STUI. 
  9. After the update, turn engine/power ON and adjust the warning volume.
    • If applicable using the Menu mode button on the steering wheel and the instrument cluster menus on the LCD display, change Warning Volume to "Low  ".
  10. Procedure is complete. Remove USB flash drive from the vehicle.

SOFTWARE UPDATE FOR AVN 5TH GEN WIDE

  1. Turn ON  the ignition switch.
  2. Press Setup  (A) to enter the Setup menu.
  3. Press General  (B).
  4. Insert the USB flash drive into the USB slot (C).
  5. Press Update  (D) at the bottom of the screen to proceed with the software update.
  6. Select OK  (E) on the pop-up.
  7. Select Start  (F).
    NOTE: Do NOT  remove the USB flash drive or turn power OFF  . This may cause data loss or system failure.

    Information 

    There will be multiple occurrences where the Hyundai logo will appear, or the display will be black during the update process. This is normal.
  8. When update has completed, confirm the Software Version  (G) has been correctly updated by referring to the SW Info/Update  screen.

    The last 6 digits of the updated software version for all models should be "240313  ".

    Refer to Software Versions for AVN 5th Gen Wide  on page 12 for complete updated software versions for each model.

  9. STUI 

    Take a photo of the 'SW Info/Update' screen showing the updated S/W version with the last 6 digits of the VIN and date of repair on a piece of paper. Put post-it/piece of paper in top right-hand corner for photo. 

    Upload the photo to STUI. 
  10. After the update, turn engine/power ON  and adjust the warning volume.

    If applicable go to Settings  menu to change warning volume to "Low  ":

    • Driver Assistance > Warning Volume 
  11. Remove USB flash drive from vehicle.
  12. The service procedure is now complete.
