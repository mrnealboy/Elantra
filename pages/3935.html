Floor console assembly

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. To remove the gear knob & gear boots (A) pull both of it up.

    [General type] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [N Line] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the floor console side garnish (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. After loosening the mounting screw and then using a screwdriver or remover, remove the console upper cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Press the lock pin separate the console upper cover connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the storage box pad (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. After loosening the mounting screws and bolts, remove the floor console assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector is connected properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
