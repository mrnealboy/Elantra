Monitor GDS data

  1. Connect the GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Monitor the TPMS Sensor parameter data on the "Current Data".
  3. After driving at 35 Km/h (16 mile/h) for approx. 10 to 20 minutes
  4. Check that current data is correctly displayed while driving.
