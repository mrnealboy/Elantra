TGS Lever

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.

The TGS sensor is installed underside of Shift lever. TGS sensor sends the shift lever position information to the TCM. The TCM compares TGS sensor signal with Inhibitor switch signal and judges the shift lever position. Inhibitor switch signal has high priority over TGS sensor signal.
