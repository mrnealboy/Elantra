Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Remove the plug hole (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Loosen the mounting nuts and remove the wiper arm (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    22.6 - 26.5 N.m (2.3 - 2.7 kgf.m, 16.6 - 19.5 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the washer nozzle (A).
  4. Detach the clips, then remove the cowl top cover (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
