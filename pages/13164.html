Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  2. Remove the front wheel and tire (A) from the front hub.

    Tightening torque:  107. 9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the front wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Loosen the front strut upper mounting nuts (A).

    Tightening torque:  53.9 - 68.6 N.m (5.5 - 7.0 kgf.m, 39.8 - 50.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove stabilizer bar link from the front strut after loosening the mounting nut.
    NOTE:
    • When loosening the nut fix the outer hexagon of stabilizer bar link.
    • Be careful not to damage the stabilizer link boots.

    Tightening torque:  98.1 - 117.7 N.m (10.0 - 12.0 kgf.m, 72.3 - 86.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove brake hose bracket from the front strut after loosening the mounting bolt.

    Tightening torque:  8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the front strut assembly (A) from the front axle (B) after loosening the bolts and nuts.

    Tightening torque:  156.9 - 176.5 N.m (16.0 - 18.0 kgf.m, 115.7 - 130.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
