Rear Torsion Beam Axle Bush Replace

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the rear torsion beam axle from the vehicle.

    (Refer to Rear Torsion Beam Axle - "REMOVAL ")

  2. Mark (A) between the rear torsional beam chassis bracket and the torsional beam axle.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the rear torsion beam axle stay (A) after loosening the mounting bolt and nut.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Using the SST (09551-AL100), replace the bush (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • When press in the bush, press the same direction of the void (A) and the flange direction (B) before detaching.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Install the torsion beam axle stay (A) and tighten the bolt and nut to the specified torque.

    Tightening torque: 

    68.6 N.m + 270° (7.0 kgf.m + 270°, 50.6 lb-ft + 270° lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Do not reuse removed bolt and nut.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Before removing the torsion beam axle stay, attach the marking (A) to match.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
