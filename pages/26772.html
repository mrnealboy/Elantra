Air Cleaner Assembly

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Disconnect the breather hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the RCV hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the air intake hose (A), and then remove the air cleaner assembly (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    Intake hose clamp: 2.9 - 4.9 N.m (0.3 - 0.5 kgf.m, 2.2 - 3.6 lb-ft)

    Air cleaner assembly: 3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When assembling the intake hose, match the alignment marks (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Install in the reverse order of removal.
