DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy
  • Monitoring maximum length of left/right gear shift motor 1
  1. Faulty DCT
  2. Mechanical failure on gear 1 Shift direction
Enable Condition
  • Gear shift motor 1 hall sensor is normal when reference point is found during engine cranking
Threshold Value
  • Maximum motion of gear shift motor 1 is less than 19.4 mm or greater than 22 mm
Detecting Time
  • 3 times
Fail Safe
  • Drive at Even gear (2/4/6/R)
