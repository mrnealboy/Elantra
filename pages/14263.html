DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Air/fuel ratio control
  1. Intake or purge-related air leak
  2. PCV system
  3. O2, air volume sensor, PCSV
  4. Fuel system
Case 1 Prerequisite Condition
  • Engine Torque is between 17% and 57%
  • ECT is higher than 55°C (131°F)
  • IAT is lower than 85°C (185°F)
  • Fuel System Status Closed loop
Threshold Value
  • Idle long-term trim value is less than -0.77
Case 2 Prerequisite Condition
  • Engine Torque is between 5.8% and 270%
  • ECT is higher than 55°C (131°F)
  • IAT is lower than 85°C (185°F)
  • Engine speed less than 1000 RPM
  • Fuel System Status Closed loop
Threshold Value
  • Part load long-term trim value is less than -7%
Diagnostic Time
  • 10 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
