Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Remove the plug hole (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Loosen the mounting screw (A) and then remove the front door outside handle (B) by sliding it forward.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the front door lock assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the front door outside handle connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Be sure to install the outside handle pad (A) between the outside handle and the door panel.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • Make sure the connector is connected properly.
    • Make sure the door locks/unlocks and opens/closes properly.
