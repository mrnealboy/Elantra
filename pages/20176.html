GDS data Analysis

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn ignition switch "ON".
  3. Maintain the vehicle standing still.
  4. Monitor the "Lateral G Sensor, Yaw Rate Sensor, Longitudinal G Sensor" parameter on the "Current Data" menu of the GDS.

    Specification  : Approx. 0 in stand still condition.
