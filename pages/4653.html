Repair Procedures: Replacement: [RH]

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat-tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
  1. Remove the rear seat assembly [RH].

    (Refer to Rear Seat - "REAR SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  2. Remove the rear back armrest.

    (Refer to Rear Seat - "REAR BACK ARMREST ")

  3. Push the lock pins, and remove the rear seat headrest (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the rear seat armrest board (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the rear seat back cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Press the both hook (B) and remove the headrest guides (A) by pulling upward.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the rear seat back cover assembly (A) from the rear seat back frame.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. After removing the Velcro tape (A) and tie down clips (B) inside the rear seat cushion cover, remove the seat cushion cover (C).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. To install, reverse the removal procedure.
