Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal.
  2. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  3. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  4. Disconnect the input speed sensor connector (A) and TCM connector (B) and then loosening the bolts and then remove ground line (C).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the inhibitor switch connector (A) and then loosening the bolts and then remove the wiring bracket (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the bolts and then remove the ground line (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Disconnect the gear actuator connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the bolts and then remove the wiring bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the shift cable and cable bracket (C) after loosening the bolt (A) and nuts (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 19.6 - 26.5 N.m (2.0 - 2.7 kgf.m, 14.5 - 19.5 lb-ft)
    2. 9.8 - 13.7 N.m (1.0 - 1.4 kgf.m, 7.2 - 10.1 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Loosen the bolts and then remove the wiring bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Loosen the starter upper mounting bolts (A) and transmission upper mounting bolts (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)
    2. 42.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.3 - 5.5 kgf.m, 31.1 - 39.8 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Assemble the engine support fixture use a (Beam SST No.: 09200 - 3N000, Adapter SST No.: 09200-4X000, Engine fixture adapter (rear) SST No.: 09200-L1100, Engine fixture adapter (front) SST No.: 09200-L1200.
  13. Using the engine support fixture (A), hold the engine and transaxle assembly safely.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Remove the dust cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Remove the transaxle mounting bracket bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Remove the transmission support bracket (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.

    Tightening torque: 

    58.8 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.9 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  17. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  18. Remove the intercooler inlet pipe bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    6.9 - 10.8 N.m (0.7 - 1.1 kgf.m, 5.1 - 8.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Loosen the starter lower mounting bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  20. Remove the sub frame.

    (Refer to Suspension System - "SUB FRAME ")

  21. Remove the roll rod mounting support bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    49.0 - 68.6 N.m (5.0 - 7.0 kgf.m, 36.2 - 50.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  22. Remove the front drive shaft assembly.

    (Refer to Driveshaft and Axle - "FRONT DRIVESHAFT ")

  23. Support the transmission safely by using the jack.
  24. Loosen the transmission lower mounting bolts (A, B).

    Tightening torque: 

    1. 42.2 - 48.1 N.m (4.3 - 4.9 kgf.m, 31.1 - 35.4 lb-ft)
    2. 42.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.3 - 5.5 kgf.m, 31.1 - 39.8 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  25. After separating the transmission from the engine, remove the transmission by lowering the jack slowly.
    CAUTION:
    • Be careful not to damage other nearby systems or parts when removing the transmission assembly.
