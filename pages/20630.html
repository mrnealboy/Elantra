Water Pump

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Drain the coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  2. Remove the drive belt.

    (Refer to Timing System - "DRIVE BELT ")

  3. Remove the water pump pulley (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the water pump (A) and gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When installing the water pump, replace with new gaskets.
  5. Install the order parts in the reverse order of removal.
  6. Fill the radiator with coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE:
    • When changing the coolant and draining air, be sure to follow the filling instructions of the Integrated Thermal Management Module (ITM).
  7. Start engine and check for leaks.
