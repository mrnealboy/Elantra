Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Make sure vehicle does not roll before setting shift lever to "N" position.
  2. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) terminal.
  3. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  4. Disconnect the inhibitor switch connector (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Loosen the shift cable mounting nut (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the manual lever mounting nut (A) and inhibitor switch mounting bolts (B) and then remove the inhibitor switch (C).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
