Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  2. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Body Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  3. Loosen the nut (A) and then separate the shift cable (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ")

  5. Remove the snap pin (A) and then separate the shift cable socket (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the nuts and then removing the shift cable retainer (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the shift cable (A) by pulling it toward the interior.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
