GDS data Analysis

  1. Turn ignition switch "ON".
  2. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  3. Start the engine and drive the vehicle at more than 40 km/h (24 MPH).
  4. Monitor the "WHEEL SPEED SENSOR" parameter on the "Current Data" menu of the GDS.

    Specification 

    : Compare the result with other parameters of wheel speed sensor. If it is the same as other parameters, it is in normal condition.
