Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the floor console assembly. (Refer to Floor Console - "FLOOR CONSOLE ASSEMBLY ")
  3. Remove the front pillar trim.
    1. To remove the front pillar trim, the fastener remover (A) below must be used.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. After slightly lifting up the front pillar trim and putting the tool in, remove the mounting clip (A) by pulling it.

      Information 

      • Be sure to replace the existing clips with new ones when removing or replacing the front pillar trim.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Remove the front pillar trim (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Detach the clips, then remove the front door body side weatherstrip (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad side cover (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the crash pad lower panel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Press the lock pin, separate diagnosis connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the photo sensor cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the cluster fascia panel upper garnish (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Remove the crash pad garnish

    [LH] 

    1. Using screwdriver or remover, remove the steering column shroud upper panel (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Loosening the mounting screws, using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Press the lock pin, separate the connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    1. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish [CTR] (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Loosening the mounting screws, using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Loosen the mounting screws by turning the steering wheel to the left and right, and remove the steering column shroud lower panel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Remove the instrument cluster. (Refer to Body Electrical System - "INSTRUMENT CLUSTER ")
  14. Loosen the mounting screw, remove the center speaker (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Press the lock pin, separate the center speaker connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Remove the glove box housing cover. (Refer to Crash Pad - "GLOVE BOX HOUSING COVER ")
  17. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the A/C & heater controller unit (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  18. Disconnect the A/C & heater controller connectors (A) and hose (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the AVN keyboard assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  20. Press the lock pin, separate the start button connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  21. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  22. Remove the USB port assembly (A) by pulling it in the direction of the arrow.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  23. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  24. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the crash pad center panel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  25. Remove the steering wheel. (Refer to Steering System - "STEERING WHEEL ")
  26. Remove the multifunction switch assembly. (Refer to Body Electrical System - "MULTIFUNCTION SWITCH ")
  27. Remove the crash pad air vent.

    [LH] 

    1. Loosen the mounting screw, remove the crash pad air vent (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Press the lock pin, separate the mood lamp connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    1. Loosen the mounting screw, remove the crash pad air vent (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Press the lock pin, separate the hazard switch connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  28. Loosen the passenger's airbag (PAB) mounting nuts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  29. Loosen the mounting bolts and nuts and remove the main crash pad assembly (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    16.7 - 25.5 N.m (1.7 - 2.6 kgf.m, 12.3 - 18.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  30. Press the lock pin, separate the passenger's airbag (PAB) connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  31. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the crash pad fits onto the guide pins correctly.
    • Before tightening the bolts, make sure the crash pad wire harnesses are not pinched.
    • Make sure the connectors are plugged in properly, and the antenna lead is connected properly.
    • Make sure that each of the assembly components operates properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
