Removal and Installation

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Remove the engine room under cover, and then Drain the engine coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  3. Remove the exhaust manifold.

    (Refer to Intake And Exhaust System - "EXHAUST MANIFOLD ")

  4. Remove the EGR cooler pipe B (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    18.6 - 23.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.4 kgf.m, 13.7 - 17.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When installing, replace with new gasket.
  5. Remove the EGR cooler (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    18.6 - 23.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.4 kgf.m, 13.7 - 17.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When installing, replace with new gasket.
    • When installing the EGR cooler, tighten the bolts (or nuts) with pre-torque first, and then tighten the bolts (or nuts) with specified torque in the sequence shown.
      Fig 1: EGR Cooler, Bolts And Nuts Tightening Sequence
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Install in the reverse order of removal.
  7. Fill the radiator with coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE:
    • When changing the coolant and draining air, be sure to follow the filling instructions of the Integrated Thermal Management Module (ITM).
