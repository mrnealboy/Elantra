DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Leakage inspection
  1. Fuel filler cap is damaged or out of place
  2. Fuel filler pipe
  3. Fuel evaporative line
  4. Canister closed valve
  5. Canister purge solenoid valve
  6. Canister
Prerequisite Condition
  • Canister Close Valve closed
  • Canister Purge Valve closed
  • Conditions for evap. system monitoring are met
Threshold Value
  • FTP gradient is higher than threshold value
Diagnostic Time
  • 25 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
