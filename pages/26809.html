Removal and Installation

  1. Remove the drive belt.

    (Refer to "DRIVE BELT ")

  2. Remove the drive belt tensioner (A).

    Tightening torque 

    Bolt (B): 18.6 - 23.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.4 kgf.m, 13.7 - 17.4 lb-ft)

    Bolt (C): 34.3 - 41.2 N.m (3.5 - 4.2 kgf.m, 25.3 - 30.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:
    • Check the belt tensioner for crack, and damage. Replace if necessary.
