DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Calculate the misfire rate (a misfire took place in more than 2 cylinders)
  1. Ignition plug or coil
  2. Valve timing
  3. Compression pressure
  4. Air leak
  5. Fuel (injector)-related parts
  6. Cooling system
Prerequisite Condition
  • Engine speed is higher than 550 RPM and lower than 6500 RPM
  • Engine load is higher than Zero torque line
  • Coolant temperature is higher than -7°C (19.4°F)
  • Time after engine start is higher than 0 sec
  • Then ECT is greater than 21°C (69.8°F)
Case 1 Threshold Value
  • Misfire rate is greater than 2.25%~3% pre 1000 revolutions
Diagnostic Time
  • Within 1000 revolutions
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
Case 2 Threshold Value
  • Rate of misfire per 200 revolutions is greater than 5~25% in driving condition
Diagnostic Time
  • Within 200 revolutions
MIL
  • Immediately (Blink)
