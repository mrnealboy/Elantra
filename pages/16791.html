Repair Procedures: Replacement

Information 

  • When removing and installing the trunk lid, an assistant is necessary.
NOTE:
  • Be careful not to damage the trunk lid and body surface.
  1. Remove the trunk lid trim. (Refer to Trunk Lid - "TRUNK LID TRIM ")
  2. Disconnect the connectors and trunk lid wiring harness (A).
  3. After loosening the mounting bolts, remove the trunk lid assembly (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the trunk lid locks/unlocks and opens/closes properly.
    • Adjust the trunk lid as necessary.
    • Make sure the connectors are connected in properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips.
