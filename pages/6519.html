Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. Install the cylinder head cover.
    1. Install the new gasket.
      NOTE:
      • Always use the new inner and outer cylinder head cover gaskets.
    2. The hardening sealant located on the upper area between timing chain cover and cam carrier should be removed before assembling cylinder head cover.
    3. Apply liquid gasket sealant to the marked area of the cam carrier and assemble within 5 minutes of application.

      Width:  2.0 - 3.0 mm (0.0787 - 0.1181 in.)

      Specification:  MS721-40AA or above

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Install the cylinder head cover (A) by tightening the bolts, in several passes, in the sequence as shown.

      Tightening torque 

      Step 1: 3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

      Step 2: 7.8 - 9.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.0 kgf.m, 5.8 - 7.2 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      Fig 1: Cylinder Head Cover Bolts Tightening Sequence
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      NOTE:
      • The firing and/or blow out test should not be performed within 30 minutes of assembling the cylinder head cover.
  2. Install the other parts in the reverse order of removal.
