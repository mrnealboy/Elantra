DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Performance monitoring
  1. Air cleaner is clogged
  2. Air leak or clog in intake manifold
  3. Malfunction of EWGA and RC
  4. Boost pressure sensor
Prerequisite Condition
  • The atmospheric pressure (BARO) is greater than 70 kPa
  • Fuel cut does not start
  • The engine revolution is greater than 2200~2800 RPM
  • The bypass valve (RCV) does not work
Threshold Value
  • The Difference value between actual boost pressure and target boost pressure is less than -20 kPa
Diagnostic Time
  • 3 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
