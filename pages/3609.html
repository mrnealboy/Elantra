Vehicle S/W Management

  1. Plug GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn "ON" the ignition switch.
  3. Select "AMP" system and perform "VEHICLE S/W MANAGEMENT " with GDS.
  4. Perform "AMP Operation Test" of "Vehicle S/W Management".
