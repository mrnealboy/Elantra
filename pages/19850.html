Actuation Test

  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn the ignition switch ON and warm up the vehicle up to an operational temperature.
  3. Select "Auto Defog Direction Potentiometer" parameter on the "Current Data" of the GDS.
  4. Select "Actuation Test" for "Auto Defog Mode Door - OPEN/CLOSE" in order.
  5. During an actuation test, check whether the value of the actuator changes.

    Specification  : Open - Approx. 95%, Close - Approx. 30%
