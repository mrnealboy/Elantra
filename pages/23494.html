Front Seat Belt Pretensioner (BPT)

  1. Install the front seat belt pretensioner (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    Bolt B: 7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Bolt C: 39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.9 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Connect the BPT connector (A) and PSB connector (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Install the front seat belt upper anchor (A) bolt.

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.9 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install the center pillar upper trim. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "CENTER PILLAR UPPER TRIM ")
  5. Install the center pillar lower trim. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "CENTER PILLAR LOWER TRIM ")
  6. Install the door scuff trim. (Refer to Body (Interior and Exterior) - "DOOR SCUFF TRIM ")
  7. Reconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  8. After installing the seat belt pretensioner, confirm proper system operation.
    NOTE:
    • Turn the ignition switch ON the airbag warning lamp should be turned on for about 6 seconds and then go off.
