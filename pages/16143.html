Repair Procedures: Disassembly

  1. Remove the lower cover (A) after loosening the screw.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Disconnect the remote control switch connector (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the wiring after disconnecting the heated steering wheel connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the switch bezel (A) after loosening screw.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the remote control switch (A) after loosening the screw.

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the steering wheel damper (A) after loosening the mounting bolts.
