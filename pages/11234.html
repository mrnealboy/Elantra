Monitor GDS Data

  1. Ignition switch OFF, connect GDS.
  2. Ignition switch ON and the engine OFF, select "Current Data" mode.
  3. Monitor the "Passenger Pretensioner resistance" parameter on the GDS.
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Reference  : Resistance of "Passenger Pretensioner" is between 1.0Ω and 6.9Ω

Reference  : In a case of an open in the "Passenger Pretensioner" circuit: FAIL

In a case of a short to battery in the "Passenger Pretensioner" circuit: FAIL

In a case of a short to ground in the "Passenger Pretensioner" circuit: FAIL
