Passenger Front Impact Sensor (PFIS)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Front Impact Sensor (FIS) located at the both sides of the front of engine room detects a head-on collision. When the FIS delivers a collision signal to the SRSCM, the SRSCM checks if the safing sensor located in the SRSCM detects a collusion.

and if the both FIS and the safing sensor detects a collision simultaneously, the SRSCM operates front airbag.
