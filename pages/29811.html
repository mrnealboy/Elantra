INTRODUCTION TO THE PIN TENSION GAUGE

INTRODUCTION TO THE PIN TENSION GAUGE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 23-BE-013H, Date of Issue:  September 30, 2023
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: BODY ELECTRICAL

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin introduces a new special service tool (SST) called the pin tension gauge and provides instructions for proper usage.

Connector issues are common causes of electrical problems, which may include:

  • Excessive female terminal spread
  • Connector not properly locked
  • Terminals not seated or backed out
  • Corrosion or moisture

Detecting excessive female terminal spread can be challenging, especially with micro female terminals. The pin tension gauge simplifies the process by allowing a quick and easy check of female terminals to ensure a secure connection.

Applicable Vehicles:  All models

SERVICE PROCEDURE

  1. Find the connector information in the applicable Electronic Troubleshooting Manual (ETM) on Hyundai Tech Info (HTI) and click the connector number.
  2. Locate the Vendor P/Name  and the numbers of the terminal series.
    Connector MF22
    1. Vendor AMP
    2. Terminal Series: 060110
    • Break Down
    		 060-110
    • Pin Series
    		 060-110
    3. Number of Pins 18
    4. Pin/Terminal Type
    (F = Female, M = Male)    		 F

    Information 

    Occasionally, the Vendor P/Name fields may not include a pin series number(s). Always measure the thickness of the connector pin and compare it with the pin gauge tool to choose the most suitable pin.
  3. Find the pin series number and select the pin tool by matching the color of the gauge wire in the table.
  4. Insert the appropriate male pin from the pin gauge tool into the female terminal of the connector and remove.

    Information 

    A slight drag should be felt when removing the pin tension gauge.

    Refer to the ETM on HTI for more information.
