Front Door Antenna

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the front door trim.

    (Refer to Body - "FRONT DOOR TRIM ")

  3. Remove the front door antenna (B) after loosening mounting screws and disconnecting the connector (A).
