Moisture Absorbent of the Head Lamp

NOTE:
  • After the testing of moisture, replace if necessary.

Testing of moisture 
  • Start the engine, turn on the head lamp and open the dust cover.
  • If there is moisture after 30 minutes, replace the moisture absorbent.
  1. Remove the head lamp.
  2. Remove the dust cap (A) and replacement the bulb.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Loosen the mounting screws and remove the moisture-absorbing agent.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Grasp the moisture absorbent and form a round shape.
    • Do not insert the moisture absorbent folded.
