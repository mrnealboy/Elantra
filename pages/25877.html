DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor signals
  1. Intake CVVT assembly
  2. e-CVVT
Prerequisite Condition
  • The battery voltage is between 10V and 16V
  • The coolant temperature is between 14°F (-10°C) and 109.4°F (43°C) when the engine starts
  • The vehicle speed is less than 37.2 mph
  • The TPS is less than 0.01%
Threshold Value
  • The difference between the target value and the actual value of the intake cam is greater than 6°
Diagnostic Time
  • 5 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
