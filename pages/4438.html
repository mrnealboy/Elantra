Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Remove the front door window glass. (Refer to Front Door - "FRONT DOOR WINDOW GLASS ")
  2. Remove the front door outside handle. (Refer to Front Door - "FRONT DOOR OUTSIDE HANDLE ")
  3. Remove the outside handle pad (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the front door outside handle base mounting bolt (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    3.9 - 5.9 N.m (0.4 - 0.6 kgf.m, 2.9 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Press the lock pin, separate the outside rear view mirror connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Press the lock pin, separate the front door main connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Loosen the front door latch mounting screws and bolts.

    Screw: 9.8 - 14.7 N.m (1.0 - 1.5 kgf.m, 7.2 - 10.8 lb-ft)

    Bolt: 8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 8.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the mounting bolts and remove the front door module (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure connectors are connected properly and each rod is connected securely.
    • Make sure the door locks/unlocks and opens/closes properly.
