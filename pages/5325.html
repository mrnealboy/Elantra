Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the negative (-) battery cable.
  2. Release the parking brake.
  3. Remove the floor console assembly. (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ASSEMBLY ")
  4. Disconnect the parking brake switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the parking brake switch (A) after loosening the screw.

    Tightening torque: 

    2.00 - 2.9 N.m (0.2 - 0.3 kgf.m, 1.4 - 2.2 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
