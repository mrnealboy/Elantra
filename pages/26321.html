Canister Closing Valve (CCV), Canister

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Evaporative Emission Control System (EVAP) prevents hydrocarbon (HC) vapors from the fuel tank from escaping into the atmosphere where they could form photochemical smog. Gasoline vapors are collected in a charcoal canister. Canister Closing Valve (CCV) closes off the air flow that comes into the canister in order to find a leak in the Evaporative Emission Control System (EVAP). The CCV also prevents fuel vapors from escaping the canister. When the engine purges the HC vapors from the canister, clean air comes into the canister through Canister Air-Filter and the CCV.
