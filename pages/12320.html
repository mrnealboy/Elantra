Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  2. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  3. Loosen the bolt and then separate the engine wiring (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the snap pin (A) and then disconnect the shift cable (B) and the select cable (C) from the control shaft complete.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the shift lever knob & boots (A) pull both of it up.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ")

  7. Remove the snap pin (A) and then separate the select cable (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Separate the cable socket (A) from the shift lever, after release the lock.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Loosen the bolts and then disconnect the shift lever (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Remove the shift cable (A) and shift lever (B) by spreading the clips on both sides of the cable end.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Loosen the nuts (A) and then removing the retainer (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Remove the control cable assembly (A) from the vehicle.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
