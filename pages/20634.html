Repair Procedures: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Remove the cooling fan (A) from the cooling fan assembly.

    Tightening torque: 

    2.8 - 3.3 N.m (0.28 - 0.33 kgf.m, 2.0 - 2.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Disconnect the fan motor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the fan motor from cooling fan shroud.
