Measuring Body Dimensions

  1. Basically, all measurements in this repair information are taken with a tracking gauge.
  2. When a measuring tape is used, check to be sure there is no elongation, twisting or bending.
  3. For measuring dimensions, both projected dimensions and actual - measurement dimensions are used in this repair information.
