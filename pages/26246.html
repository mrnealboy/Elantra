DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Rationality check
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Leak in intake or exhaust system
  3. O2 Sensor (Down)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Battery voltage is higher than 10.7V
  • Engine speed is between 1200 RPM and 3520 RPM
  • EGT around downstream O2S is higher than 300°C (572°F)
  • Intergrated Exhaust gas mass flow is higher than 6.1g/sec.
  • Delta exhaust gas mass flow is between -8.3g/sec. and 41.7g/sec.
  • Fuel cut in
  • Downstream O2S voltage before fuel cut is lower than 0.3V
Threshold Value
  • Transient Time is higher than Threshold (Duration of O2 Sensor (Down) voltage from 0.3V to 0.725V during fuel cut in)
Diagnostic Time
  • -
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
