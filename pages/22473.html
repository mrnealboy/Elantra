Repair Procedures: Inspection

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
NOTE:
  • In principle, the transmission oil is maintenance-free, and no exchange is required.
  1. Uplift the vehicle with lift.
  2. Remove the under cover.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  3. Loosen the oil filler plug (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Check the oil level.
    NOTE:
    • For the accuracy, inspect the oil level after the oil is completely drained.
    • Loosen the drain plug (A) and wait for over 10 minutes until the oil is drained enough.
    • Tighten the drain plug.

      Tightening torque: 

      44.1 - 58.9 N.m (4.5 - 6.0 kgf.m, 32.5 - 43.4 lb-ft)

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • Install the drain plug bolt with new gasket (A).

      New gasket part number  : 45323-39000

      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • After the inspection, inject the oil through the filler plug hole according to the specification.

      Oil capacity: 

      1.6 - 1.7 L (0.42 - 0.44 U.S gal., 1.69 - 1.79 U.S.qt., 1.41 - 1.49 Imp qt.)

    • While draining, some of the oil flows into the transmission part and the oil may be less than the specification by 0.1 - 0.15 L (0.03 - 0.04 U.S.gal., 0.10 - 0.16 U.S. qt., 0.09 - 0.13 Imp qt.) This is normal and additional oil injection is not required.
  5. Install the filler plug (A) with new gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    58.9 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • The existing filler plug gasket (A) must be replaced with a new one. (Do not reuse it.)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Install the under cover.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")
