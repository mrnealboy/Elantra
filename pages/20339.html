On Vehicle Inspection

  1. Accelerate the engine to about 3000 RPM 3 times or more.
  2. Remove the spark plug.
  3. Check the spark plug visually.

    If the electrode is dry, the spark plug is normal.

    If the electrode is wet, check the damage and electrode gap as below in component inspection.
