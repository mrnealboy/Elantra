Door Outside Handle

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Loosen the mounting bolt after remove the plug hall (A). And then push the outside handle in the direction of arrow as shown in illustration below.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Loosen the mounting screw (A) and then remove the front door outside handle (B) by sliding it forward.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the front door lock assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the front door outside handle connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
