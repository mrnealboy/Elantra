DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Rationality check
  1. Air cleaner is clogged
  2. Air leak in intake manifold
  3. Malfunction of EWGA and RCV hose oil leak or clog
  4. Boost pressure sensor
  5. Atmosphere sensor inspection
Prerequisite condition
  • The engine revolution is less than 1000 RPM
  • The throttle angle is less than 8%
Threshold Value Case1
  • The pressure difference between the turbocharger boost pressure (PUT) and the atmospheric pressure (AMP) is greater than 11 kPa
Case2
  • The pressure difference between the turbocharger boost pressure (PUT) and the atmospheric pressure (AMP) is less than -18 kPa
Diagnostic Time
  • 5 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
