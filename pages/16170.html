Repair Procedures: Replacement: [RH]

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Remove the instrument cluster. (Refer to Body Electrical System - "INSTRUMENT CLUSTER ")
  2. Remove the crash pad lower panel. (Refer to Crash Pad - "CRASH PAD LOWER PANEL ")
  3. Detach the clips, then remove the front door body side weatherstrip (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad side cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the steering column shroud upper panel (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. To remove the gear knob & gear boots (A) pull both of it up.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the floor console side garnish (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. After loosening the mounting screw and then using a screwdriver or remover, remove the console upper cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Press the lock pin separate the console upper cover connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish [CTR] (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the A/C & heater controller unit (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  12. Disconnect the A/C & heater controller connectors (A) and hose (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  13. Loosen the mounting screws, remove the AVN keyboard assembly (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  14. Press the lock pin, separate the start button connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  15. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  16. Loosening the mounting screws, using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  17. Loosen the mounting screw, remove the crash pad air vent (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  18. Press the lock pin, separate the hazard switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  19. Press the lock pin, separate the connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  20. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector are connected in properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
