Repair Procedures: Replacement

  1. Uplift the vehicle with lift.
  2. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  3. Drain the manual transaxle oil after loosening the drain plug (A) and then tighten the drain plug with new gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    58.9 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • The existing oil drain plug gasket (A) must be replaced with a new one. (Do not reuse it.)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the oil filler plug (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Add new fluid through the filler plug hole.

    Standard fluid:  SAE 70W, API GL-4, TGO-9 (MS517-14)

    Recommend oil: 

    (SHELL: SPIRAX S6 GHME 70W, SK: HK MTF 70W, GS CALTEX: GS MTF HD 70W)

    Specified fluid:  1.5 - 1.6 L (0.40 - 0.42 U.S.gal., 1.58 - 1.69 U.S.qt., 1.32 - 1.40 lmp qt.)

  6. Install the filler plug (A) with new gasket.

    Tightening torque: 

    58.9 - 78.5 N.m (6.0 - 8.0 kgf.m, 43.4 - 57.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • The existing oil filler plug gasket (A) must be replaced with a new one. (Do not reuse it.)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Install the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")
