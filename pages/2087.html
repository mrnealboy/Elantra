DTC Description

C181800 is set when the CGW* cannot receives the C-CAN message from the BCW* although the C-CAN Bus is normal.

It can occur when there is a Faulty Power Supply to the BCW*, or an Open/Poor Connection in the High line or the Low line of the BCW*'s branch circuit.
