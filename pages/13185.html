Run out inspection

  1. Jack up the vehicle.
  2. Measure the wheel run-out by using a dial indicator as shown in illustration below.
    Limit Radial Axial
    Run-out mm (in.) Steel wheel 0.6 (0.0236) 0.8 (0.0315)
    Aluminum wheel 0.3 (0.0118) 0.3 (0.0118)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. If measured value run-out the standard value, replace the wheel.
