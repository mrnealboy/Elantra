DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Trouble shooting Prerequisite condition Possible Cause
Main action
  • Pressure system rationality
  1. Open/Short in circuit
  2. Faulty driven pulley pressure sensor
  3. Faulty hydraulic system
Prerequisite condition
  • Battery voltage is higher than 10V
  • Engine speed is higher than 350 RPM
  • No CAN error
  • Solenoid condition "ON"
  • Torque signal state (from engine) is normal
  • Pulley safety torque change "ON"
  • APS is lower than 0%
  • Lever Position "P"
  • Brake "Off"
  • Vehicle speed by secondary pulley speed sensor is higher than 20 km/h
  • Line pressure of HW is higher than target secondary pressure
  • Secondary pulley speed is 0 RPM
Threshold Value
  • Deviation between target secondary pulley press and current secondary pulley press is higher than 7 bar
Detecting Time
  • 6 sec
MIL ON Condition
  • 2 driving cycles
Fail safe
  • Limited learning
