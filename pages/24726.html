Maintenance System Reminder Reset - Procedure 03

NOTE: For models without LCD Display.

Service Information 

If the remaining mileage or time reaches 900 miles (1, 500 km) or 30 days, the service symbol (wrench) will blink for several seconds each time you set the ignition switch or Engine Start/Stop button to the ON position. If you exceed the specified service interval, the service symbol (wrench) will blink each time you turn ON the vehicle. To reset the service interval, press the RESET button for more than 5 seconds and then when the miles and days blink press the RESET button for more than 1 second. If the service interval is not set, the service symbol (wrench) will not be displayed.
