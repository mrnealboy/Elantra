Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Remove the air cleaner and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  2. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - Battery - "REMOVAL , and BATTERY TRAY ")

  3. Loosen the flare nut and then separate the clutch tube (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    12.7 - 16.7 N.m (1.3 - 1.7 kgf.m, 9.4 - 12.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    CAUTION:
    • Do not spill brake fluid on the vehicle it may damage the paint if brake fluid does contact the paint, wash it off immediately with water.
  4. Remove the snap pin (A) and then separate the clutch hose & tube (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the clutch hose & tube from the vehicle.
