Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
    NOTE:
    • Unscrew in turn the mounting bolt (A) in small steps (0.5 turns). If one of the two bolts is fully unscrewed while the other is installed, the housing surface of the cylinder head may get broken due to the tension on the pump spring.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Do not reuse the high pressure fuel pipe.
    • Be careful not to deform the shape of the high pressure fuel pipe.
    • Do not tilt the torque wrench when tightening the nut.
    • Install the component to the specified torques.
    • Check the fuel leakage after installing the high pressure fuel pipe.
    • Use the special service tool [SST No.: 09314-3Q100 or 09314-27130] to install the high pressure fuel pipe.
    CAUTION:
    • Before installing the high pressure fuel pump, position the roller tappet in the lowest position by rotating the crankshaft. Otherwise the installation bolts may break due to the tension on the pump spring.
    • Do not reuse the bolt.
    • Install the component to the specified torques.
    • Note that internal damage may occur when the component is dropped. In this case, use it after inspecting.
    • Apply engine oil to the O-ring (A) of the high pressure fuel pump, the roller tappet (B), and the protrusion (C). Also apply engine oil to the groove on the location where the protrusion (C) is installed.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
