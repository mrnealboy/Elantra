Transaxle Mounting Bracket

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  3. Remove the air cleaner assembly.

    (Refer to Intake and Exhaust System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  4. Remove the battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "REMOVAL , and BATTERY TRAY ")

  5. Install the jack to the edge of transaxle.
  6. Remove the service cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the transaxle mounting bracket bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    88.3 - 107.9 N.m (9.0 - 11.0 kgf.m, 65.1 - 79.6 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the transaxle mounting bracket (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    49.0 - 63.7 N.m (5.0 - 6.5 kgf.m, 36.2 - 47.0 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Install in the reverse order of removal.
