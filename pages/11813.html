DTC Description

C16034B is set when the temperature of the EPS ECU (MDPS=Motor Driving Power Steering Control Module) surpasses 85°C due to continued sharp steering. Under this circumstance, the EPS limits the assistant steering power to decrease the current to the motor. When it reaches over 120°C, it stops the operation of the assistant steering power.
