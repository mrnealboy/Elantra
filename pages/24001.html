Aftermarket Dongle Installed At DLC (22-GI-002H)

Publication date: 2022-03-01
Reference number: 22-GI-002H

AFTERMARKET DONGLE INSTALLED AT DATA LINK CONNECTOR (DLC) P060141

AFTERMARKET DONGLE INSTALLED AT DATA LINK CONNECTOR (DLC) P060141

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 24-GI-011H, Date of Issue:  July 01, 2024
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: GENERAL INFORMATION
Superceded Bulletin(s): 22-GI-002H, Date of Issue:  July 01, 2024

REVISION HISTORY

This TSB supersedes 22-GI-002H to update dongle samples and the list of issues described on page 1. 

DESCRIPTION

Aftermarket dongles installed via DLC on Hyundai vehicles may interfere with the Control Area Network (CAN) system causing the following issues:

  • Bluelink performance.
  • Telematic concerns.
    • Door lock cycling or inability to lock.
    • Over The Air (OTA) update related failures related to navigation and OTA software update. o OTA rollback failure - unable to recover a module that attempted to install an OTA software update.
  • 12V battery drains when parked for several days.
  • Loss of CAN communication.
  • CAN errors causing temporary or historical DTCs.
  • Shifter system malfunction warning on the cluster.
  • Check engine light is ON. 
  • Drivability issues that cannot be replicated by technicians.
  • DTC example P060141  - Internal Control Module Memory Check Sum Error.

Applicable Vehicles:  All vehicles equipped with a Data Link Connector (DLC)

NOTE: Any repairs, damage, diagnosis, and/or parts replacement attributed to an aftermarket device that was or is connected to a vehicle's DLC are not covered by warranty.

SERVICE PROCEDURE

  1. Check under the dash to left of the steering wheel for an installed aftermarket dongle in the DLC:
    • Dongle is present. Remove before any diagnosis is performed.
      • If the aftermarket dongle causes issues, document the cause on the repair order (RO) and inform the customer.
    • Dongle is NOT  present, and the issue cannot be replicated. Ask the customer if an aftermarket dongle was installed.

    Information 

    Customers often install aftermarket dongles for insurance purposes, fuel monitoring, GPS tracking, or to monitor driving habits. Customers may remove the aftermarket dongle before bringing the vehicle in for service.
