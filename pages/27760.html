GDS data Analysis

  1. Parking a car flat on the ground. Step on the brake, shift the gear lever to "P" position and keep the engine "ON" condition.
  2. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  3. Select "Current Data" mode and monitor "Apply Switch" & "Release Switch" parameter.
  4. Pull and push EPB switch once, and then let it be in neutral position and check EPB switch status from current data.

    Specification  : When the switch is activated, the data changes to 'ON', 'OFF'.
