Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  3. Drain the coolant.

    (Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

  4. Drain the engine oil.

    (Refer to "ENGINE OIL ")

  5. Disconnect the oil pressure temperature sensor connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Disconnect the oil cooler hoses (A), and then remove the oil filter & cooler assembly (B).

    Tightening torque: 

    22.6 - 27.5 N.m (2.3 - 2.8 kgf.m, 16.6 - 20.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Install the other parts in the reverse order of removal.
  8. Refill with the engine oil.

    (Refer to "ENGINE OIL ")

  9. Fill the radiator with coolant.

    Refer to Cooling System - "COOLANT ")

    NOTE:
    • The coolant must be injected according to the integrated thermal management module (ITM) coolant filling method.
