Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Disconnect the negative (-) battery terminal.
  2. Remove the glove box housing cover.

    (Refer to Body - "GLOVE BOX HOUSING COVER ")

  3. Loosen the mounting bolts, nuts and remove the immobilizer unit (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Press the lock pin and disconnect the immobilizer unit connectors (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
