Charging Inspection

  1. Engine starts.
  2. Maintain ENG RPM 2500 RPM over 2 minutes.
  3. Measure voltage between the battery terminal (+) and the battery terminal (-).

    Reference:  Approx. 12.5 ~ 14.7V

    YES 

    • Replace the battery sensor with a working one and see if the problem is corrected. If so, replace the battery sensor.

    NO 

    • Check that the tension of drive belt, ENG. RPM, fuse, terminal of battery, all terminals of generator are in good condition and Check for damaged harness and poor connection between generator and battery. Repair or replace relevant component(s) as necessary.
