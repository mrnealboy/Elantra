Repair Procedures: Removal: Battery

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery (-) terminal (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 9.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.0 kgf.m, 5.8 - 7.2 Ib-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Disconnect the battery (+) terminal (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 9.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.0 kgf.m, 5.8 - 7.2 Ib-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the battery mounting bracket.

    Tightening torque: 

    7.8 - 11.8 N.m (0.8 - 1.2 kgf.m, 5.8 - 8.7 Ib-ft)

  4. Remove the battery (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
