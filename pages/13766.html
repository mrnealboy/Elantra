DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Air-fuel ratio control
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Air leak on Cylinder #2
  3. O2 Sensor, Air volume sensor, PCSV
  4. Injector #2
Prerequisite Condition
  • The engine coolant temperature is between 131.9°F (55.5°C) and 221°F (105°C)
  • The catalyst temperature (Model) is between 932°F (500°C) and 1652°F (900°C)
  • The engine revolution is between 1320 RPM and 3000 RPM
  • The engine load is between 30% and 100%
  • Air-fuel ratio control
Threshold Value
  • The #2 Cylinder Air fuel ratio adaption value is less than -0.9 or greater than 1.1
Diagnostic Time
  • 40 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
