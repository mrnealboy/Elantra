Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Detach the clips, then remove the front door body side weatherstrip (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad side cover (A).

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the crash pad garnish

    [LH] 

    1. Using screwdriver or remover, remove the steering column shroud upper panel (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Loosening the mounting screws, using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Press the lock pin, separate the connector (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    1. Using screwdriver or remover, remove the steering column shroud upper panel (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Using screwdriver or remover, remove the floor console side garnish (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish [CTR] (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    4. Loosening the mounting screws, using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [CTR] 

    1. Using screwdriver or remover, remove the steering column shroud upper panel (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Using screwdriver or remover, remove the floor console side garnish (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    3. Loosening the mounting screws, using a screwdriver or remover, remove the crash pad garnish (A).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Install in the reverse order of removal.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector is connected properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
