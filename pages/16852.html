Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Set shift lever to N position.
  2. Remove the air cleaner assembly and air duct.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ")

  3. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  4. Loosen the bolt and then separate the engine wiring (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the back-up lamp switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the snap pin (A) and then separate the shift cable (B) and the select cable (C) from the control shaft complete.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the control shaft assembly (A) after loosening the bolts.

    Tightening torque: 

  8. 19.6 - 26.5 N.m (2.0 - 2.7 kgf.m, 14.5 - 19.5 lb-ft)
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
