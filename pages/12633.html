DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy
  • Monitoring the voltage of control circuit
  1. Poor connector connection
  2. Open in harness
  3. Faulty variable oil pressure control valve
Enable Condition
  • No disabling faults present
  • Engine run-time longer than 0.03s
  • Battery voltage is between 11V and 16V
Threshold Value
  • Voltage represents open circuit
Diagnostic Time
  • Continuous
MIL ON Condition
  • No MIL on (DTC only)
