Removal and Installation

* There are two methods to when removing the drive belt.

[When removing the drive belt from the top of the vehicle]

  1. Install the wrench to the drive belt tensioner repair hole (A) and turn it clockwise.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Fix the drive belt tensioner by installing fixing pin after turning the drive belt tensioner to clockwise.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • Use fixing pins that have the same outer diameter as a 6 mm (0.2362 in.) hex wrench or a 6 mm (0.2362 in.) hex wrench.
  2. Remove the drive belt (A).
    Fig 1: Engine Drive Belt Routing Diagram
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • When removing the drive belt, remove the drive belt from the water pump pulley.
  3. Install in the reverse order of removal.

    Information 

    • When installation of drive belt, make the drive belt as a round hook shape bigger than alternator pulley as a picture down below. Then install it with following order of alternator → air-conditioner compressor → crankshaft damper pulley → water pump → drive belt tensioner.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

[When removing the drive belt from the bottom of the vehicle] 

  1. Remove the engine room under cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE ROOM UNDER COVER ")

  2. Turn the spanner to clockwise after installing it to drive belt tensioner arm boss.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • Fix the drive belt tensioner by installing fixing pin after turning the drive belt tensioner to clockwise.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    • Use fixing pins that have the same outer diameter as a 6 mm (0.2362 in.) hex wrench or a 6 mm (0.2362 in.) hex wrench.
  3. Remove the drive belt (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    • When removing the drive belt, remove the drive belt from the water pump pulley.
  4. Install in the reverse order of removal.

    Information 

    • When installation of drive belt, make the drive belt as a round hook shape bigger than alternator pulley as a picture down below. Then install it with following order of alternator → air-conditioner compressor → crankshaft damper pulley → water pump → drive belt tensioner.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
