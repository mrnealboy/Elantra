Repair Procedures: Installation

  1. Insert fully the stop lamp switch as hiding contact part.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. After inserting, turn the stop switch 45° clockwise, and then assemble locking plate (A) by pushing.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Confirm the gap between stop lamp switch.

    Stop lamp clearance (A):  1.0 - 2.0 mm (0.04 - 0.08 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • If the gap between stop lamp switch and bracket is not 1.0-2.0 mm (0.04-0.08 in), perform the above process again.
  4. Connect the stop lamp switch connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Install the lower crash pad.

    (Refer to Body - "CRASH PAD ")
