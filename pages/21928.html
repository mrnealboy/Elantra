Cylinder Head

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Using a precision straight edge and feeler gauge, measure the contacting surface of the cylinder block and the manifolds for warpage.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Flatness of cylinder head gasket surface: 

    Standard: Less than 0.05 mm (0.0020 in.)/total area

    Less than 0.02 mm (0.0008 in.)/100 mm (3.9370 in.) X 100 mm (3.9370 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Flatness of manifold mounting (Intake/Exhaust) surface: 

    Less than 0.10 mm (0.0039 in.)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Inspect for cracks.

    Check the combustion chamber, intake ports, exhaust ports and cylinder block surface for cracks. If cracked, replace the cylinder head.
