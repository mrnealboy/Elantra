BCA (BLIND-SPOT COLLISION-AVOIDANCE ASSIST) SOFTWARE UPDATE (SERVICE CAMPAIGN T8H)

SERVICE CAMPAIGN BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 22-01-038H, Date of Issue:  April 12, 2022
HYUNDAI: 2022 Elantra (CN7)
GROUP: CAMPAIGN

SERVICE INFORMATION

IMPORTANT: ***Dealer Stock and Retail Vehicles ***
Dealers must perform this Service Campaign on all affected vehicles prior to customer retail delivery and whenever an affected vehicle is in the shop for any maintenance or repair.
When a vehicle arrives at the Service Department, access the "Vehicle Information" screen via WEBDCS to identify open Campaigns.

DESCRIPTION

Some 2022 Elantra (CN7) vehicles may exhibit one or both of the following conditions:

  • When driving and the vehicle blind spot collision warning alert sound is on; upon reduction of speed below 10 mph, the warning sound does not turn off.
  • When backing up/moving in reverse and the rear cross traffic collision audible warning sound is on; upon changing the transmission into Drive, the warning sound does not turn off.

This bulletin provides the service procedure to update the left and right BCA (Blind-Spot Collision-Avoidance Assist) modules software to resolve the condition(s) stated above.

Applicable Vehicle: Certain 2022MY Elantra (CN7) equipped with 1.6T engine.

rear radar (left and right) software update procedure 

NOTE: Check if the vehicle's software version is affected:
  • Check the current version of the left and right REAR RADAR ROM ID and compare it to the ROM ID information table listed below before attempting to perform the software update.
NOTE: You must initially perform the GDS BCA Update in Auto Mode.
  • Refer to the TSB 15-GI-001  for the tablet-based Mobile GDS ECU update procedures.
  • If the BCA update starts but then fails in Auto Mode, perform the update in Manual Mode to recover.
NOTE:
  • In the ECU Upgrade screen, select "RR RADAR (L)" and then "RR RADAR (R) as the system to update.

SERVICE PROCEDURE

  1. Select "RR RADAR (L)" and then "RR RADAR (R)" as the system to update.
    NOTE: As noted above, the part number will change along with the ROM ID. Picture to the left is an example of the rear right corner radar updating information for both the part number and ROM ID.
  2. Check the current version of the left and right REAR RADAR ROM ID and compare it to the ROM ID Information Table listed above before attempting to perform the software update.
  3. Perform GDS software update. Refer to TSB 15-GI-001  for the tablet-based Mobile GDS ECU update procedures.
  4. After the software has updated, check for Diagnostic Trouble Codes in the ALL menus and erase any DTC.
  5. Confirm vehicle is operating normally.
