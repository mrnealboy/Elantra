Engine Bearing Clearance Tester Calibration Procedure (20-GI-009H)

WARNING: This page is about the Elantra N Line, Automatic DCT Trans, which is a different variant/trim than selected.
Publication date: 2021-10-31
Reference number: 20-GI-009H

ENGINE BEARING CLEARANCE TESTER CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

ENGINE BEARING CLEARANCE TESTER CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 21-GI-009H, Date of Issue:  October 31, 2021
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: General Information
Superceded Bulletin(s): 20-GI-009H, Date of Issue:  October 01, 2021

REVISION HISTORY

This TSB supersedes 20-GI-009H to include information on units with the digital AP/VC gauge. 

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin provides the calibration procedure for the engine bearing clearance tester. The bearing clearance tester is already pre-calibrated, but additional calibration may be required due to variable shop compressor air pressure when testing engine rod bearing wear.
NOTE: The bearing clearance tester requires connection to a known good air supply during use.
  • Shop air supply must consistently provide at least 50 PSI of air pressure to the tester.
  • Do NOT use a portable air compressor.
  • Be sure to check that the shop air water separator is functioning correctly.
NOTE: For Bearing Clearance Tester software  related issues, contact GITA at: 888-437-0308
For Bearing Clearance Tester hardware  related issues, refer to HTSS:
"Fix it Right" under Symptom "SST - Other"

NOTE: Before performing the calibration procedure, verify that the power cable and air hoses are NOT attached to the bearing clearance tester, and that the power switch is set to the OFF position.

CALIBRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Remove the two, smaller hex screws located on the bearing clearance tester cover.

    Hex Screw: 2.5mm hex 
  2. Remove the four, larger hex screws located on each side of the bearing clearance tester cover.

    Hex Screw: 3.0mm hex 
  3. Lift and remove the bearing clearance tester cover.
  4. Verify the power switch is OFF. 
  5. Follow the EXACT power cable and air hose connection order: 
    1. Connect the main air compressor hose.
    2. Connect the 12V power cable to a stable power source (a fully charged 12 volt battery or an external AC/DC 13.8 volt power supply). 
    3. Connect the test hose. (The other end of the test hose should be left disconnected from the spark plug rod.)
  6. Turn the power switch ON,  and select the AP/VC switch to VC. 
    • AP: Pressure 
    • VC: Vacuum 
    NOTE: To verify power to main unit solenoids:
    • Toggle the AP/VC transfer switch between AP <> VC.
    • During toggling, a "click" noise will indicate the solenoids are active.
  7. [Dial Type]  Inspect the VC gauge and verify that the reading is between: -73 ~ -83kPa. 
    NOTE: A red overlay on the VC gauge will indicate the recommended specification.

    [Digital Type]  Inspect the VC  gauge and verify that the reading is between: -.073- -.083MPa. 

    NOTE: Digital Type gauge shares the same display to show AP and VC set points.

    If the VC  gauge is within specification, proceed to Step   9 . 
  8. If the VC gauge is NOT  within specification, pull the VC knob upwards to activate it.

    Twist the VC knob to increase (+)  or decrease (-)  the set point value until the reading is within specification.

    Clockwise: increases (+) set point Counterclockwise: decreases (-) set point 

    After twisting the knob until the VC reading is within specification, press the knob downwards to deactivate and lock the knob.
  9. With the power switch still ON,  select the AP/VC switch to AP. 

    AP: Pressure 

    VC: Vacuum 
  10. [Dial Type]  Inspect the AP gauge and verify that the reading is between: 0.1 ~.11MPa. 
    NOTE: A blue overlay on the AP gauge will indicate the recommended specification.

    [Digital Type]  Inspect the AP  gauge and verify that the reading is between: 0.1 ~.11MPa. 

    NOTE: Digital Type gauge shares the same display to show AP and VC set points.

    If the AP  gauge is within specification, proceed to Step   12 . 
  11. If the AP gauge is NOT within specification, pull the AP knob upwards to activate it.

    Twist the AP knob to increase  (+) or decrease  (-) the set point value until the reading is within specification.

    Clockwise: increases (+) set point Counterclockwise: decreases (-) set point 

    After twisting the knob until the AP reading is within specification, press the knob downwards to deactivate and lock the knob.
  12. Turn the power switch OFF. 
  13. Follow the EXACT power cable and air hose disconnection order: 
    1. Disconnect the 12V power cable.
    2. Disconnect the test hose.
    3. Disconnect the main air compressor hose.
  14. Reinstall all parts in reverse order of removal.
  15. The calibration procedure is now complete.
