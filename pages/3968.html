Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat - tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
NOTE:
  • Take care not to bend or scratch the trim and panels.
  1. Remove the rear seat cushion assembly.

    (Refer to Rear Seat - "REAR SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  2. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the rear door scuff trim (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the rear pillar trim (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen mounting clips and bolts, remove the luggage partition side trim (A) and (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. After loosening the mounting bolts, remove the rear wheel house trim (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Loosen the mounting bolt, remove the rear seat belt lower anchor (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    39.2 - 53.9 N.m (4.0 - 5.5 kgf.m, 28.9 - 39.8 lb-ft)

    [LH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [CTR] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    [RH] 

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Open the rear seat belt cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Press the lock pin, separate the connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Using a screwdriver or remover, remove the rear package tray trim (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. To install, reverse the removal procedure.

    Information 

    • Make sure the connector are connected in properly.
    • Replace any damaged clips (or pin - type retainers).
