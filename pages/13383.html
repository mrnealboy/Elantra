Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Turn the ignition switch OFF and disconnect the battery negative (-) cable.
  2. Remove the air cleaner assembly.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "AIR CLEANER ASSEMBLY ")

  3. Disconnect the RCV control solenoid valve connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the vacuum hose (A).
  5. Remove the RCV control solenoid valve (C), after loosen mounting bolt (B).

    Tightening Torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb - ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
