DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy Case 1
  • Rationality signal monitor - high side
  1. Poor connector connection
  2. Air cleaner pollution
  3. Faulty intake system leakage
  4. Faulty MAPS
Case 2
  • Rationality signal monitor - low side
Enable Condition
  • Pressure quotient difference between manifold/ambient pressure is less than 0.8
  • Time after start for more than 0.5s
  • Battery voltage between 11 and 16V
  • Signal change is more than 12°/s
  • Failure not detected for DTCs
Threshold Value Case 1
  • Filtered reduced area controller is higher than -60 to -40% and lower than 40 to 60% (versus ambient pressure, throttle position)
Case 2
  • Filtered reduced area controller is higher than -30 to -20% and lower than 20 to 30% (versus ambient pressure value)
Diagnostic Time
  • 5 seconds
MIL ON Condition
  • 2 driving cycles
