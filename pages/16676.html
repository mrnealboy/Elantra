Repair Procedures: Disassembly

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Separate the M-terminal (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. After loosening the screws (A), remove the magnetic switch assembly (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Loosen the through bolts (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the brush holder assembly (A), york assembly (B) and armature assembly (C).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Separate the planet gear shaft assembly (A) from front housing (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the lever (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the shield (A) and packing.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the planet gears (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Press the stopper (A) using a socket (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Remove the stop ring (A) using the stop ring pliers (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  11. Remove the stopper (A), overrunning clutch (B), internal gear (C) and planet shaft (D).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • Using a suitable pulling tool (A), pull the overrunning clutch stop ring (B) over the stopper (C).
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
