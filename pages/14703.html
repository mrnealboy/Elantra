IBU (SMK + BCM + TPMS) & PCM (ECM/TCM) Control Module (Authentication Fail)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Smart Key system (SMK) consists of a Smart Key module, power distribution module (PDM), external receiver, and Smart Key holder. On the other hand, the SMK (smart key) system without a FOB holder has an embedded Demodulator which is connected to the SSB with two immobilization coil lines. The Smart Key unit transmits authentic signals to the engine ECM and receives an ignition approval signal to start the engine through this process.
