Removal and Installation

  1. Disconnect the battery negative terminal.
  2. Remove the engine cover.

    (Refer to Engine and Transaxle Assembly - "ENGINE COVER ")

  3. Remove the engine wire harness connectors and wire harness clamps from cylinder head and the intake manifold.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Disconnect the intercooler outlet hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

    Information 

    1. When disconnecting the intercooler hose, pull the quick connector clamp (A) in the direction of the arrow as below.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    2. Move the quick connector clamp from B to C position as below and then disconnect by pulling the quick connector hose.
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the purge control solenoid valve (PCSV) hose (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the water return hose & pipe bracket bolts (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    9.8 - 11.8 N.m (1.0 - 1.2 kgf.m, 7.2 - 8.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the Intake Manifold (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    18.6 - 23.5 N.m (1.9 - 2.4 kgf.m, 13.7 - 17.4 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    NOTE:
    • When installing, replace with new gaskets.

      When installing the intake manifold, tighten sequence shown.

      Fig 1: Intake Manifold Bolts Tightening Sequence
      Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Install in the reverse order of removal.
