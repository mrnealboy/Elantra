The Type Can Not Replace the Wheel Side Joint Boot

  1. Remove the front drive shaft.

    (Refer to "FRONT DRIVESHAFT ")

  2. Remove the transaxle side joint.

    (Refer to "JOINT ASSEMBLY (TRANSAXLE SIDE) ")

  3. Remove the dynamic damper.

    (Refer to "DYNAMIC DAMPER ")

  4. Replace the wheel side joint assembly and shaft .
NOTE:
  • The wheel side joint boot can not be replaced, so replace the wheel side joint assembly and shaft together as an assembly.
