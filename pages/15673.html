DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Inspect wiring
  1. Faulty door handle (Right) (Door handle/NFC Antenna)
  2. Door handle overheating (Right) (NFC Antenna/Lamp)
  3. Door handle input voltage errors (9v or below, 16v or more)
Prerequisite Condition
  • Turn the ignition switch ON.
Threshold Value
  • Faulty door handles (Right)
  • Door handle overheating (Right)
  • Door handle input voltage errors (Right) (Maintain 9v or below & 16v or more for 2 sec)
Diagnosis Time
  • Immediately
DTC Clearing
  • "Immediately after confirming "normal operation" from the BLE*
NOTE: BLE*: "Bluetooth Low Energy"
