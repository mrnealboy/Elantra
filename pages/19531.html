OCS (Occupant Classification System) Sensor

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The OC system monitors a front passenger seat for occupancy by means of a pressure sensitive matrix sensor, but the BodySense system detects seat occupancy and classifies the occupant by using an electricalfield sensor. Unlike the pressure sensitive matrix sensor, the electrical field sensor is very sensitive tomoisture and electronic devices and therefore please give customers advice not to put any electronicdevices on or around the passenger seat cushions and also spill water or beverage on the seat cushions.
