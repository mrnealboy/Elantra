Monitor GDS Data

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Engine "ON" and Operate vehicle or system in accordance with Enable Condition in DTC Detecting Condition.
  3. Monitor "Input Speed 1, 2", "Shift Lever Switch", "Current Gear" and "Engine RPM" parameter in "Current Data" with GDS.
