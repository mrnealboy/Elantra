ENGINE OIL FILTER COVER DRAIN PLUG INSTALLATION GUIDELINES

ENGINE OIL FILTER COVER DRAIN PLUG INSTALLATION GUIDELINES

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 22-EM-003H-1, Date of Issue:  February 15, 2022
HYUNDAI: 20MY-later Sonata (DN8a/DN8); 21 MY-later Santa Fe (TMa); 22MY-later Santa Cruz (NXT); 22MY-later Tucson (NX4a/NX4); 21 MY-later Elantra (CN7a/CN7)
GROUP: ENGINE
Superceded Bulletin(s): 22-EM-003H, Date of Issue:  February 01, 2022

REVISION HISTORY

This TSB supersedes 22-EM-003H to update the Applicable Vehicles list. 

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin provides important information regarding the oil filter cover drain plug that is included in the engine oil filter service kit.

  • When performing an engine oil/filter change service, be sure to verify and install the new oil filter cover drain plug (item A shown below) from the oil filter service kit.

Applicable Vehicles: 

  • 20MY-later Sonata (DN8a/DN8) equipped with 2.5L and 2.5T engines 
  • 21 MY-later Santa Fe (TMa) equipped with 2.5L and 2.5T engines 
  • 22MY-later Santa Cruz (NXT) equipped with 2.5L and 2.5T engines 
  • 22MY-later Tucson (NX4a/NX4) equipped with 2.5L engine 
  • 21 MY-later Elantra (CN7a/CN7) equipped with 2.0L engine 

PARTS INFORMATION

PARTS INFORMATION

PART NAME PART NUMBER APPLICATION NOTE
SERVICE KIT-OIL FILTER 26350-2S000 2.5L and 2.5T engines
SERVICE KIT-OIL FILTER 26350-2J000 2.0L engines

WARRANTY INFORMATION

  • Any engine damage that occurs from not following the service guideline outlined in this bulletin is subject to warranty chargeback.

SERVICE PROCEDURE

  1. This oil filter cover drain plug is provided in the oil filter service kit.

    As instructed in the applicable Shop Manual, the oil filter cap drain bolt must be replaced with a new one, installed and tightened to the specified torque.

    • Tightening torque: 4.5 N.m (39 ib-in) 

      It is important to locate the new oil filter drain plug.

  2. If the oil filter drain plug is not found to be secured to the packaging, check if the drain plug is located inside of the oil filter element.
  3. If the new oil filter cover drain plug wasn't installed and was left remaining inside the oil filter element, it will get lodged into the oil filter housing passage. This will obstruct oil flow and cause SEVERE engine damage.
    • Be sure to install the new drain plug to the oil filter cover.
      CAUTION: The drain plug of the oil filter cover must not be mispiaced inside the oil filter.
      • Any engine damage that occurs from not following this service guideline is subject to warranty chargeback.
