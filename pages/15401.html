DTC Setting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor the CAN data
  1. Open/Poor Connection in PSM*'s B-CAN "High" line
  2. Open/Poor Connection in PSM*'s B-CAN "Low" line
  3. Faulty Power Supply to PSM*
Prerequisite Condition
  • IG key "ON"
  • Power Voltage: Between 9V and 16V
  • B-CAN Bus: Active
Threshold Value
  • PSM*'s B-CAN message: Not received
Diagnosis Time
  • 10 sec.
