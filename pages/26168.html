DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Rationality check
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Check coolant volume
  3. ECTS
Case 1 Prerequisite Condition
  • The ECT and ECT at start difference value is lower than -4.5°C (23.9°F)
Threshold Value
  • The ECT and Modeled ECT 2 (low side) difference value is lower than -15°C (5°F)
Diagnostic Time
  • 30 sec
Case 2 Prerequisite Condition
  • The Engine Speed is higher than 400 RPM
  • Integrated air mass is greater than 1000g
Threshold Value
  • The ECT and Modeled ECT 3 (High side) difference value is greater than 13.5°C (56.3°F)
Diagnostic Time
  • 120 sec
Case 3 Prerequisite Condition
  • ECT at start is between -10.5°C (13.1°F) and 90°C (194°F)
  • Number of following driving conditions for at least 18 sec. is higher than 4 times
    • Vehicle speed is between 40 km/h and 180 km/h
    • Air flow is between 6.67 and 83.3g/sec.
  • Number of following driving conditions for at least 18 sec. is higher than twice
  • Vehicle speed is lower than 5 km/h
  • Air flow is between 1.11 and 4.44g/sec.
Threshold Value
  • The maximum temperature and minimum temperature difference value is lower than 0.8~3.0°C (33.44~37.4°F)
Diagnostic Time
  • 1 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
