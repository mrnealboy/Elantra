Read the VIN

  1. Plug GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Turn "ON" the ignition switch.
  3. Select "Vehicle S/W Management" and click "Read VIN".
  4. Are you able to confirm the VIN properly?

    YES 

    • Repair completed.

    NO 

    • Go to next step as below.
