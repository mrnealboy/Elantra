Repair Procedures: Removal

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Turn OFF ignition switch and then diconnect the battery negative (-) cable.
  2. Remove the floor console assembly.

    (Refer to Body - "FLOOR CONSOLE ")

  3. Disconnect the wiring and connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the snap pin (A) and then separate the shift cable socket (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Loosen the bolts and then removing the shift lever (A).

    Tightening torque: 

    8.8 - 13.7 N.m (0.9 - 1.4 kgf.m, 6.5 - 10.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
