Repair Procedures: Replacement

CAUTION:
  • When removing with a flat-tip screwdriver or remover, wrap protective tape around the tools to prevent damage to components.
  • Put on gloves to prevent hand injuries.
  1. Remove the front seat assembly.

    (Refer to Front Seat - "FRONT SEAT ASSEMBLY ")

  2. Remove the front seat headrest (A) by pulling it upward.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the front seat lower protector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the front seat back board (A).
    NOTE:
    • Grasp the both lower sides of back board with hands and then remove it in the direction of the arrow.
    • Be careful not to damage the fixing clips.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Disconnect the front seat ventilation duct (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Press the both hook (B) and remove the headrest guides (A) by pulling upward.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Press the lock pin, separate the lock pin (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Separate the front seat back cover protector, remove the front seat back cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  9. Remove the velcro tape (A) and tie down clips (B) inside the front seat back cover and remove the seat back cover (C).
    CAUTION:
    • Make sure that the sponge insert wire is not dislocated.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  10. Install in the reverse order of removal.

    Information 

    • Make sure that the connectors are connected completely
    • Replace any damaged clips.
