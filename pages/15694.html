LKAS (Lane Keep Assistant System)

  1. LKAS (Lane Keep Assistant System) 

The LKA-L has a CAN line that is directly linked to the SCC besides a Chassis CAN line, which is called the Local CAN. The Local CAN line carries CAN signals administering operation of the FCA (Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist). This function is mainly controlled by the SCC, not the LKA-L, thus, a LKA-L warning lamp would not illuminate when an error occurs regarding the CAN signals. Still, the DTC will be stored.

Steering Angle Sensor is to determine turning direction of the steering wheel. It will deliver calculated turning direction to each module through CAN communication. Each modules calculates steering wheel speed and angle and they will use them as a input signal to control Anti-roll.
