Curtain airbag (CAB)

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Curtain Airbag (hereinafter referred to CAB) is located at the driver and the passenger side of the headlinerIt protects the passenger's head and shoulder from fragments of glass or something sharpen caused by overturn. The CAB consists of an airbag and an inflator.

Airbag reduces impact of collision by fillied up gas.

An Inflator keeps gas and uses it to deploy airbag on collision.

CAUTION: Never measure the resistance of the CAB directly, Current of measuring the device may cause unexpected air bag deploy.
