DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Electrical inspection
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Open or short in harness

  • The battery voltage is greater than 10V

  • No message has been received from the Alternator via external LIN

  • 5 sec
