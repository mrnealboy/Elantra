DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Item Detecting Condition Possible Cause
DTC Strategy Case 1
  • Comparison of battery voltage and voltage after main relay (voltage low)
  1. Poor connector connection
  2. Open in circuit
  3. Faulty engine control relay
Case 2
  • Comparison of battery voltage and voltage after main relay (voltage high)
Case 3
  • Monitor voltage supply after main relay (open circuit check)
Enable Condition Case 1
  • Ignition Switch "ON"
  • Battery voltage higher than 10V
  • 0.05 sec. of delay time after main relay "ON"
Case 2
  • Ignition Switch "OFF"
  • 0.05 sec. of delay time after main relay "ON"
Case 3
  • Voltage after main relay higher than 8.5V
  • Voltage after ignition switch higher than 5V
  • 0.05 sec. of delay time after main relay "ON"
Threshold Value Case 1
  • Voltage after main relay lower than 6V (Ignition "ON" condition)
Case 2
  • Voltage after main relay lower than 6V (Ignition "OFF" condition)
Case 3
  • Deviation of voltage after main relay and voltage after ignition switch higher than 3.8 to 8.5v
Diagnostic Time Case 1
  • 0.2 sec.
Case 2
  • 0.2 sec.
Case 3
  • 0.1 sec.
MIL ON Condition
  • 2 driving cycles
