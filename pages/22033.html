DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
Trouble shooting Prerequisite condition Possible Cause
Main action
  • Monitor the voltage range (Open/Short)
  1. Open/Short in circuit
  2. Faulty shift solenoid C (Clutch) valve
  3. Faulty shift solenoid C (Clutch) hydraulic system
  4. Faulty valve body
Prerequisite condition
  • Battery voltage is higher than 10V
Threshold Value
  • Open in the circuit
Detecting Time
  • 0.3 sec
MIL ON Condition
  • 2 driving cycles
Fail safe
  • Solenoid power "OFF", Learning should not be performed, Lower engine torque, Control Cooling FAN
