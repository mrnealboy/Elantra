[G 1.6 T-GDI]

  1. Turn ignition switch OFF and disconnect the negative (-) battery cable.
  2. Drain the coolant.

    (Refer to Engine Mechanical System - "COOLANT ")

  3. Remove the battery and battery tray.

    (Refer to Engine Electrical System - "BATTERY ")

  4. Remove the master cylinder.

    (Refer to Brake System - "MASTER CYLINDER ")

  5. Disconnect the heater hose quick connector from the heater pipe.
  6. Remove the fixing clip and the vacuum hose from the brake booster.
  7. Remove the snap pin (A) and the clevis pin (B).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Loosen the fixed nut (A) and then removing the brake booster .

    Tightening torque:  16.7 - 25.5 N.m (1.7 - 2.6 kgf.m, 12.3 - 18.8 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
