Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Remove the inside rear view mirror cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  2. Using a remover and remove the rain sensor cover (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Disconnect the inside rear view mirror connector (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Loosen the mounting screw and remove the inside rearview mirror (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
