BEARING CLEARANCE TEST SERVICE PROCEDURE

BEARING CLEARANCE TEST SERVICE PROCEDURE

TECHNICAL SERVICE BULLETIN

Reference Number(s): 24-EM-004H, Date of Issue:  May 31, 2024
HYUNDAI: All Models
GROUP: ENGINE
Superceded Bulletin(s): 21-EM-004H-1, Date of Issue:  May 01, 2024

REVMISION HISTORY

This TSB supersedes 21-EM-004H-1 to include service procedure for the GEN 3 Auto BCT unit. 

DESCRIPTION

This bulletin provides the service procedure for engine Connecting Rod Bearing clearance testing utilizing the Bearing Clearance Tool (BCT). Perform the procedure outlined in this bulletin to fulfill the Bearing Clearance Test inspection requirements to determine the next required steps.

APPLICABLE VEHICLES

  • Certain models as indicated per separate TSB.
  • This TSB fulfills its Inspection Procedure requirements for the engine Bearing Clearance Test.

SST INFORMATION

Refer to TSB 24-GI-008H for SST information, calibration procedures, and basic troubleshooting.

SST INFORMATION

GEN 3 "Auto" BCT Unit 
 1) VC Calibration Adjustment Port
2) AP Calibration Adjustment Port
3) Test Hose Port
4) Main Power Switch
5) Shop Air Supply Port
6) Power Supply Port
7) Calibration Adjustment Tool
NOTE: The BCT requires connection to a known good air supply during use.
  • Shop air supply must consistently provide at least 80 PSI of air pressure to the tester.
  • Do NOT use a portable air compressor.
  • Be sure to check that the shop air water separator is functioning correctly.
  • Tool internal damage caused by water intrusion is not covered within any tool warranty.

The BCT requires a consistently stable 12V power supply for both calibration and testing.
  • Vehicle battery condition can affect unit operation (SST requires at least 12.3 Volts).
  • A commercially available external 12V power supply is recommended for all BCT usage.
  • BCT unit calibration failure is possible if the 12V supply is not adequate.

For Bearing Clearance Tester software  related issues, contact GITA at: 888-437-0308
For Bearing Clearance Tester hardware  related issues, refer to TSB 24-GI-008H (or latest)

SERVICE PROCEDURE: (BEARING CLEARANCE TEST)

STUI 

This TSB includes Repair validation photos. Refer to the latest Warranty Digital Documentation Policy for requirements.

  1. Connect the GDS to the vehicle and turn the ignition key ON.
    1. Once vehicle is selected, tap "Tool Box" within GDS-Smart Menu and select "Special Inspection" function.
    2. Select "Bearing Clearance Measurement".
    3. Tap "Verify" to validate VIN.
    4. Enter Repair Order Number and current Vehicle Mileage.
    5. Tap "Next" when active.
    6. Review Bearing Clearance Test Instructional and Caution Screens.
  2. Record vehicle's oil level as received.
    1. Remove VCI and turn vehicle ignition off.
    2. Tap "Next".
    3. When prompted, record Engine Oil Level.
    4. Tap "Take a picture".
    5. Take a picture of the dipstick clearly showing the engine oil level and markings.
    6. Tap "Next" when the selection becomes active.
  3. Connect the following items:
    1. Test hose (1) with other end of the test hose disconnected from the spark plug rod.
    2. Shop air supply hose (3).
    3. Power supply cable (4) to a stable power source (a fully charged 12 Volt battery or an external AC to DC 13.8 Volt power supply with at least 2-amp output recommended).
    • Confirm the above connection items, then turn the main power switch (2) ON.
    NOTE: Source Voltage lower than 12.3 Volts may prevent the unit from being properly calibrated or result in testing errors.

    Example:  External Power Supply

    ** GEN 1/GEN 2 BCT Unit > Skip to Step   6  ** 

  4. Select GEN 3 BCT Unit when prompted.
    1. Unit power must be ON. 
    2. Connect GEN 3 BCT unit to GDS.
      • If this is a new connection  (GEN 3 BCT Unit has not been connected to the tablet), the connection screen will be shown.
      • If no unit is shown  , tap "refresh" (A) to allow unit to populate.
    3. Select the appropriate BCT Unit shown.
    4. If a GEN 3 BCT Unit has been previously connected to a tablet, this screen will not show and GDS will connect to the "last known device".
    NOTE: Use only the GDS app to connect the GEN 3 BCT Unit via Bluetooth  . Do not pair the GEN 3 BCT Unit Bluetooth using any other method.
  5. Perform GEN 3 BCT Self-Calibration check.
    • Once connected, the GDS application will perform a self-calibration test on the unit.
    • If the unit passes  , no further user input is needed.
    • If the unit fails  , the calibration screen will be shown.
    NOTE: If any of the AP/VC air pressure indications are out of the specified range, recalibration of the Bearing Clearance Tester is required.
    • Refer to latest instructions outlined in TSB 24-GI-008H or later for calibration procedure.

    ** GEN 3 BCT Unit > Skip to Step   8  ** 

  6. Perform manual AP/VC air pressure calibration check for GEN 1/GEN 2 BCT.
    1. Confirm connection to the test hose, shop air supply hose, and power supply.
    2. Compare the set point AP/VC readings with the specification range after toggling the switch to AP and VC positions:
    • AP: 0.1 ~ 0.11MPa
    • VC (Digital Type):  -.073 ~ -.083MPa
    • VC (Dial Type):  -73 ~ -83kPa
    NOTE: If any of the AP/VC air pressure indications are out of the specified range, recalibration of the Bearing Clearance Tester is required.
    • Refer to latest instructions outlined in TSB
  7. Take STUI photo(s) of the AP/VC air pressure readings.

    STUI 

    Using the camera app of the tablet, take photos of the AP/VC air pressure readings with the pressure levels clearly visible and the VIN in the background of the photo (RO or last 6 digits written on paper) for records.

    • (Digital Type)  Take 2 separate pictures showing the AP and VC readings of the digital gauge.
    • (Dial Type)  Take 1 picture showing both AP and VC dial gauge readings.
    • Upload the calibration setting photos to STUI, after exiting the GDS BCT application. 
  8. Prepare the engine to begin the BCT process.
    1. Remove engine cover.
    2. Remove 4 ignition coils.
    3. Remove 4 spark plugs (see note).
    NOTE: Use air blow gun to clear away any dirt or debris from inside spark plug tubes before removing the spark plugs.

    Tightening Torque (Ignition Coils): 

    lb-ft 8

    N.m 10.8

    Tightening Torque (Spark Plugs): 

    lb-ft 14.5

    N.m 19.6

  9. Install appropriate Spark Plug Rod SST (B)
    1. Start with Cylinder #1 Spark Plug hole and turn until hand tight. 
      • M12 for GDI and Theta Hybrid MPI 
      • M14 for all others 
    2. Insert the Clearance Gauge SST (C) into the Spark lug Rod and tighten using the thumb screw.
    NOTE: DO NOT connect the test air hose to the Spark Plug Rod at this time.
  10. Connect Clearance Gauge to GDS via Bluetooth:
    1. Press the "Set" button (D) on the gauge to turn it on.
    2. Press the "Set" (D) and "Mode" (E) buttons at the same time for about 4 sec until the "reset" message is displayed.
    3. Select the gauge in the list of devices on the GDS. Select the 'Refresh' icon (F) if the gauge doesn't appear automatically.
      NOTE: If more than one device appears in the list of devices, there is another device in range. Move away from the other device to pair the gauge.
    4. Check that the ball point tip (G) of the clearance gauge SST is securely installed to the tool as shown. Do not continue if ball point tip (G) is loose or missing. 
    NOTE: If loose, apply Blue Loctite (or equivalent) to the tip thread and retighten securely to the gauge plunger.
    NOTE:
    • For the following steps, the Crank Rotator Tool or a long-handled wrench should be used for moving the crankshaft slowly and precisely.
    • Ensure the test air hose is disconnected from the Spark Plug Rod (to allow venting of the air from the cylinder during crank rotation) when finding TDC piston depth.

    Please take note of the following crank/piston position definitions.
    • BTDC (Before Top Dead Center)
    • TDC (Top Dead Center) → Find TDC during the compression stroke.
    • ATDC (After Top Dead Center)

    In the following steps, the TDC point for testing must be at:
    • MAX (piston height) value minus 0.03mm as the crank has moved slightly into ATDC (from its TDC dwell point).

    If TDC is difficult to locate, refer to Shop Manual Section Location:
    • Engine Mechanical > Timing System> Timing Chain
    NOTE:
    • For the step below, the engine must be rotated SLOWLY using the crank rotator SST.
      • Abrupt or erratic movements may cause improper TDC setup and test errors.
    • Do NOT stop rotating the engine before reaching MAX TDC, especially during the initial rotation towards TDC where the value may be at 0mm and change all the sudden.
      • Be sure to find TDC correctly by slowly and slightly nudging the engine a little more to verify no change in value to confirm that the piston is at MAX TDC.
  11. Locate TDC for Cylinder #1.
    1. To find TDC compression (TDC), rotate the crankshaft with the crank rotator SST at least one cycle (1/2 turn). (If the SST does not fit the specific vehicle type, remove the front passenger wheel, and wheel liner or underbody tray as needed to rotate the crankshaft using standard shop tools.)
    2. Monitor the piston height value on GDS screen or the clearance gauge SST while rotating the engine clockwise using the Crank Rotator Tool
      • Rotate the crankshaft SLOWLY  as the value increases towards a MAX value. (BTDC → TDC)
      • When the value changes direction (increasing/BTDC → TDC → decreasing/ATDC), stop rotating the crank when the piston height is at MAX value minus 0.03mm.
      NOTE: A "False TDC" issue can result if the crankshaft is rotated too rapidly while setting the TDC Value.
      • Turn the crankshaft slowly  while monitoring the height value.
      • The testing point is slightly towards ATDC from TDC when the piston has moved down 0.03mm from its highest position.
    3. When STOP sign is shown, tap "Start".
  12. When TDC Setting Error has occurred:
    1. Once TDC Value is set, DO NOT turn the crankshaft until the testing is completed for that cylinder.
      • A TDC Setting Error can result if the set point changes.
    2. In certain cases, this error may occur due to an internal engine issue specific for that cylinder.
      • Continue attempting to locate the TDC testing point for that cylinder until the Skip option appears, if unsuccessful.
    NOTE: The value at TDC varies depending on the specific vehicle and engine type. If TDC will not set, an error will be shown. If the error continues to occur after three attempts the cylinder may be skipped.
  13. Begin cylinder testing.
    1. Verify all tool connections.
    2. Connect Air Hose to Spark Plug SST.
    3. Tap "Next" and perform the cylinder testing.

      GEN 3 BCT:  Allow BCT unit to Auto Test.

      • Each "Round" will be shown on screen.
      • Testing for that cylinder is completed when the BCT unit finishes 3 rounds.

      GEN 1/GEN 2 BCT:  Perform manual BCT.

      • Move the AP/VC switch to the "AP" position on BCT unit.
      • Follow the GDS to test the cylinder.
      • Move the AP/VC switch to the "VC" position on BCT unit per GDS screen.
    4. When completed, tap "Take a Picture".
      NOTE: Photo needs to be clearly showing the cylinder tested  and the displayed value  on the Clearance Gauge SST.
  14. Repeat steps  11~ 13 for remaining cylinders.
    • Cylinders will be tested per the engine firing order: #1 → #3 → #4 → #2.
  15. If test result is a "PASS": 
    1. Save a screenshot of the test result.
    2. Reinstall all components in the reverse order of removal.
    3. Check for DTCs and perform the appropriate diagnostic service.
    4. Ensure no warning lights are present to complete the procedure.

    If test result is a "No PASS": 

    1. A draft PA request and Engine Diagnosis Worksheet (EDW) will be automatically generated and 'saved'.
    2. Complete the EDW, including the Service Manager electronically signing the form.
    3. Follow the instructions in the draft PA and submit for HMA warranty team review.
    4. Follow remainder of the separate reference TSB to complete the service procedure.
    5. Check for DTCs and perform the appropriate diagnostic service.
    6. Ensure no warning lights are present to complete the procedure.
    NOTE: PA Approval is required whenever an engine replacement is determined necessary. Submit PA and refer to the applicable Dealer Best Practices guide for the latest requirements for engine approval.

    If test result is a "No BCT":

    1. This screen may result if one or more cylinders were skipped in the BCT Process.
    2. Save a screenshot of the test result.
    3. Record Error Code Number on Repair Order.
    4. Follow "Skipped Cylinder Video Submission" steps in the following information.

Errors During Testing:  (Cylinder Skipping)

NOTE: If the bearing measurement value does not change when the unit switches from AP/VC or the "Unable to measure" message appears on the GDS, rotate the crankshaft further as the exhaust valves could be open. Set the crankshaft to TDC again (Step  11).
Ensure the test hose is disconnected from the clearance gauge SST when finding TDC.
NOTE: If an error occurs, there will be a pop up message (as shown on the right). Then click the [Back] symbol button to go back and do the test step again.
Review items within error pop up for items to inspect that maybe cause for error.
If the same error occurs within a cylinder during the BCT process after three consecutive attempts, the "Skip" button will be shown within the third error pop-up. Once the skip button is pressed, the BCT process will continue on to the next cylinder.
Possible Errors:
  1. Unexpected Measurement
  2. Exhaust Stroke
  3. TDC Setting
  4. Clearance Deviation is too Large
  5. TDC Height Setting
  6. Range Exceeded

Skipped Cylinder Video Submission:  ("No BCT" Result)

After "Next" is tapped from "No BCT" result screen, a checklist will be shown related to the error that was populated. Complete the appropriate skipped cylinder check items* below for test submission.

NOTE: Do not disconnect BCT unit power supply or Bluetooth from the GDS until test submission has been completed.

*Non-Exhaust Stroke Error (for skipped cylinder) 

  1. Refer to "Service Procedure: (Bearing Clearance Test)"  step  11 to set skipped cylinder to TDC (set manually without GDS screen).
  2. TDC is found when clearance gauge (H) shows highest reading (the reading may vary by engine type and condition).
  3. Connect BCT unit as shown in Step  13.
  4. Tap Camera Icon (I) to open the camera application.
    • GEN 3 BCT:  The AP/VC switching will automatically begin while the camera is in operation.
    • GEN 1/GEN 2 BCT:  Manually cycle the AP/VC during the video recording.
  5. Record a video containing the following (30sec/30MB max):
    • Vehicle VIN Plate.
    • Skipped cylinder to test, preset to TDC.
    • Clear view of the clearance gauge while test is being completed.
    • GEN 3 BCT:  Allow Auto switching of AP/VC to complete all 3 cycles.
    • GEN 1/GEN 2 BCT:  Manually cycle the AP/VC switch (3) three times, pausing 1~2 seconds at each position.
    • Clearance Gauge needs to be clearly shown reflecting the value change (3x).
  6. Tap "Stop" icon when completed.
  7. Check off remaining Items.
  8. Tap "Print" to finish Test Submission.
  9. Complete the Engine Diagnostic Worksheet (EDW).
  10. Verify PA Submission for HMA review.

    *Exhaust Stroke Error (for skipped cylinder) 

  11. Complete "Skipped Cylinder Video Submission"  Steps a)  through h)  from the previous information.
    • Submitted video of Clearance Gauge needs to be clearly shown reflecting the NO  value change (3x).

    STUI 

  12. Submit STUI Video while performing a Compression Test with 6 ~ 8 engine revolutions (refer to Shop Manual for procedure).
    • Clear Video of VIN Plate, Cylinder and Compression Gauge Reading in one take.
    • Record Compression reading on the Engine Diagnosis Worksheet (EDW).
  13. Complete the Engine Diagnostic Worksheet (EDW).
  14. Verify PA Submission for HMA review.
NOTE: STUI video mode using the GDS can be found by following figures 1 - 3 shown below. Be sure to monitor the session status bar (figure 4 shown below) within the STUI Video Screen to allow the complete test to be captured within the session limit.
NOTE: PA Approval is required whenever an engine replacement is determined necessary. Submit PA and refer to the applicable Dealer Best Practices guide for the latest requirements for engine approval.
