Monitor GDS Data

  1. Ignition switch OFF, connect GDS.
  2. Ignition switch ON and the engine OFF, select "Current Data" mode.
  3. Monitor the "DAB-2nd resistance" parameter on the GDS.
Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

Reference  : Resistance of "DAB-2nd" is between 1.5Ω and 6.9Ω

Reference  : In a case of an open in the "DAB-2nd" circuit: FAIL

In a case of a short to battery in the "DAB-2nd" circuit: FAIL

In a case of a short to ground in the "DAB-2nd" circuit: FAIL
