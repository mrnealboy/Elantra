Monitor GDS Data

WARNING: This page is about a different variant/trim than selected.
  1. Connect GDS to Data Link Connector (DLC).
  2. Engine "ON" and Monitor "Select Lever (from TGS CAN Line)", "Select Lever (from Inhibitor Switch)" and "Current Gear" parameters in A/T system "Current Data" with GDS.
  3. Move the shift lever to check the "Current Data" output value.
