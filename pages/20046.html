EPB Actuator

Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA

The Electronic Parking Brake (EPB) switch can apply the parking brake instead of using pedal or lever.

Operate the EPB switch → Switch signal to EPB Module → EPB Module activate actuator → Parking brake apply/release.

When EPB switch is pulled, parking brake gets applied. When EPB switch is pushed down, parking brake is released.
