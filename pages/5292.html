Repair Procedures: Removal

  1. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly.

    Raise the vehicle, and make sure it is securely supported.

  2. Remove the front wheel and tire (A) from the front hub.

    Tightening torque:  107.9 - 127.5 N.m (11.0 - 13.0 kgf.m, 79.6 - 94.0 lb-ft)

    NOTE:
    • Be careful not to damage the hub bolts when removing the front wheel and tire (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  3. Remove the hose after loosening the brake hose bolt (A) from the caliper.

    Tightening torque:  24.5 - 29.4 N.m (2.5 - 3.0 kgf.m, 18.1 - 21.7 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  4. Remove the caliper body (A) after loosening the guide rod bolts.

    Tightening torque:  21.6 - 31.4 N.m (2.2 - 3.2 kgf.m, 15.9 - 23.1 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  5. Remove the brake pad (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  6. Remove the pad liner (A).
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  7. Remove the caliper carrier (A) after loosening the caliper mounting bolts.
    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
  8. Remove the front brake disc (A) after loosening the screw.

    Tightening torque:  4.9 - 5.8 N.m (0.5 - 0.6 kgf.m, 3.6 - 4.3 lb-ft)

    Courtesy of HYUNDAI MOTOR AMERICA
