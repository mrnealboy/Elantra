DTC Information And Inspection: DTC Detecting Condition

Trouble Shooting Condition Possible Cause
Main Action
  • Monitor signals
  1. Poor contact of connector
  2. Exhaust system is clogged
  3. Carbon build-up in throttle body
  4. ETC system
Prerequisite Condition
  • Vehicle speed = 0
  • Coolant temperature is higher than 55°C (131°F)
  • Intake air temperature is higher than -10.5°C (13.1°F)
  • Accel. Pedal = 0%
Threshold Value
  • Desired engine speed is over 100 RPM greater than actual engine speed or Actual Idle torque is 10Nm higher than Idle torque
Diagnostic Time
  • 8 sec
MIL
  • 2nd Driving Cycle
